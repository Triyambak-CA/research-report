## Reverse DCF (my model, model.py)
Base FY26 revenue 374, EBITDA margin as stated, capex 12% of sales, tax 25%,
depreciation 26% of sales, WACC 13%, terminal growth 5%, 10-year explicit period.

| Revenue CAGR | EBITDA margin 31% | 38% | 43% |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10% | 1,240 | 1,607 | 1,869 |
| 15% | 1,770 | 2,294 | 2,668 |
| 20% | 2,527 | 3,274 | 3,808 |
| 25% | 3,596 | 4,660 | 5,420 |

To justify EV of ~3,012 the market needs roughly 19-20% revenue CAGR for a decade at a
38% EBITDA margin, or ~17% CAGR at a 43% margin. At the FY26 realised margin of 31% it
needs about 23% CAGR for ten years.
Against: FY26 revenue DECLINED 8.9% and FY26 margin was 31.0%.

Capex sensitivity at 20% growth and 38% margin: capex 8% of sales -> EV 3,844;
12% -> 3,274; 16% -> 2,705; 20% -> 2,136. Theme parks are capex-hungry and ride
refresh is not optional, so the higher capex columns are the realistic ones.

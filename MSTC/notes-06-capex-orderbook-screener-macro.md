## F2. Capex IRR - the arithmetic

- Direct revenue attributed: NONE in any filing or call.
- Rent saved: at most Rs 3.63 cr/yr (MSTC's ENTIRE FY25 rent across all locations; FY24 Rs 3.85 cr).
- Actual outcome: other expenses ROSE Rs 12.10 cr (+34.0%) in FY26, attributed by the CFO to the office.
- Opportunity cost: Rs 141 cr at MSTC's own realised 6.97% deposit yield = Rs 9.83 cr/yr pre-tax.

- Verdict: negative IRR on the disclosed record before any property appreciation.

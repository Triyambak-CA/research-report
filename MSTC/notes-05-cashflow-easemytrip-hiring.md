** THE ADJUSTMENT THAT MATTERS **
Reported CFO of MINUS Rs 27.79 cr in FY26 is an ARTEFACT, not a warning. Two corrections are needed for
a float-carrying auction platform:
 (a) the Rs 270.65 cr fall in "trade payables and other financial liabilities" is almost entirely the
     CLIENT FLOAT (EMD and security/customer deposits) unwinding - it is other people's money leaving,
     not MSTC's cash burning;
 (b) interest actually received (Rs 77.33 cr) is classified in INVESTING but is core operating income
     for this business.
Underlying cash generation:
   FY26 = CFO (27.79) + float outflow 270.65 + interest received 77.33 = Rs 320.19 cr vs PAT Rs 218.43 cr
          => 1.47x. Strip the one-off Rs 97.95 cr receivables release from the trading wind-down and it
          is Rs 222.24 cr = 1.02x PAT. CLEAN.
   FY25 = CFO 260.30 + interest received 69.90 - float inflow 183.12 = Rs 147.08 cr vs PAT ex-exceptional
          Rs 183.19 cr => 0.80x; ex-receivables Rs 143.64 cr = 0.78x.
The same logic explains the screener history that looks alarming: FY23 CFO +Rs 844 cr and FY24 CFO
-Rs 283 cr are float inflow and float outflow, not operating swings.
CAPEX is genuinely tiny: Rs 22.26 cr cash capex in FY26 (FY25 Rs 24.63 cr) against depreciation of
Rs 10.44 cr. The Rs 141 cr World Trade Centre office landed in PP&E mostly by converting the capital
advance that had been sitting in "other non-current assets" (Rs 149.72 cr -> Rs 52.46 cr), i.e. it was
paid for in earlier years. Maintenance capex is essentially hardware and software refresh.
Dividend paid in cash FY26 only Rs 53.50 cr (timing), vs Rs 288.64 cr in FY25 (which included the
Rs 32.00/share special interim funded by the FSNL sale).

## E2. ** THE EASEMYTRIP DISCLOSURE ASYMMETRY ** (strongest new finding)
Media release filed under Reg 30 on 26-Mar-2026 (release dated New Delhi, 27-Mar-2026):
"EASEMYTRIP ENTERS EXCLUSIVE PARTNERSHIP WITH MSTC LTD. TO POWER TRAVEL SERVICES FOR GOVT. SECTOR"
- EaseMyTrip (Easy Trip Planners Ltd, listed) will INTEGRATE ITS APIs with MSTC's system, supplying
  flights, hotels, holiday packages, bus bookings, activities and cab services.
- "MSTC will facilitate Corporate Travel for Govt Sector Organisations, LEVERAGING EASEMYTRIP'S EXTENSIVE
  TRAVEL INVENTORY AND ADVANCED TECHNOLOGY INFRASTRUCTURE."
- "EaseMyTrip will also provide end-to-end TECHNICAL SUPPORT to ensure smooth integration."
- Rikant Pittie, CEO and Co-Founder, EaseMyTrip: "This exclusive partnership with MSTC Ltd. marks a
  strategic expansion of EaseMyTrip into the Govt sector travel segment. By combining MSTC's
  INSTITUTIONAL REACH with OUR TECHNOLOGY AND SUPPLY ECOSYSTEM ..."
** ZERO mentions of "EaseMyTrip" or "Easy Trip" in ANY of the eight concall transcripts, in the FY26
investor presentation (29-May-2026) or in the Q1 FY27 investor presentation (13-Aug-2026). **
Why this matters:
1. On the Q4 FY26 call (30-May-2026 - two months AFTER the media release) the CMD said "Our portal, as a
   travel portal, is in our final stages of testing" in direct answer to a question about competing with
   Balmer Lawrie, without disclosing that the inventory and technology come from EaseMyTrip.
2. On the Q2 FY26 call, Bhanu Kumar justified entering travel on MSTC's OWN capability: "our expertise,
   our exposure to newer technologies, they have given us the confidence that we can probably enter this
   area with newer technologies as well as a better interface for the users ... we already have
   established data centers and redundant capacities."
3. Economically this converts "MSTC builds a travel platform" into "MSTC is a government-sector
   distribution channel taking a share of an OTA's commission." Far less capital at risk, far less
   margin captured, and the differentiation is MSTC's government access, not its technology.
4. It also explains the MoA alteration: MSTC needs travel-agency objects because it will act as an
   AGENT, not merely as a platform operator.
This is the clearest instance in the file of material economics being disclosed to the media but not
explained to investors on a call where the exact question was asked.

## E3. Hiring (scuttlebutt corroboration)
- MSTC Management Trainee recruitment notified 15-Nov-2025, applications closed 30-Nov-2025:
  37 vacancies across Systems (7), Operations (4), Personnel and Administration (2), Law (1) and Finance
  (balance). Pay scale Rs 50,000-1,60,000. Age below 28 as on 31-Oct-2025. All-India CBT plus GD.
- 37 hires on a base of 299 employees = a 12.4% headcount expansion. That is a genuine capacity-building
  signal and the largest single discipline is Systems.
- BUT every one of them is an entry-level Management Trainee. No senior lateral hire from a larger
  competitor, no CTO/CDO/Chief Product appointment has been disclosed, at a company simultaneously
  claiming to build an EPR exchange, a travel OTA, a TReDS platform, an equipment-leasing marketplace
  and a monetisable data centre. The EaseMyTrip outsourcing is the visible consequence of that thin bench.

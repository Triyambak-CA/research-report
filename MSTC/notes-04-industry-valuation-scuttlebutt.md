# INDUSTRY, TRIGGERS, VALUATION, SCUTTLEBUTT

## D1. What MSTC actually is (the corrected frame)
- Incorporated 08-Aug-1964 as Metal Scrap Trade Corporation to regulate ferrous scrap exports. CIN
  L27320WB1964GOI026211. Mini Ratna Category-I CPSE under the Ministry of Steel. Listed Mar-2019 (OFS).
- 299 employees as at 31-Mar-2025 (233 executives + 66 non-executives; 194 male / 39 female executives).
  Attrition (resignations only) ~1.5%; total turnover including retirements ~5.2%.
  Revenue per employee FY26 Rs 1.24 cr; PAT per employee Rs 0.73 cr; average employee cost Rs 33.0 lakh.
  Employee cost is 63% of total FY26 expenses - this is a people-plus-platform business, not an asset business.
- The business is a TRANSACTION-FEE PLATFORM with a large, interest-earning client float, wrapped in a
  legacy trading balance sheet that is finally being wound down.

CONCLUSION: the take rate is NOT a ratchet. It rose in FY25-FY26 because low-fee bulk volume (coal e-sale,
e-procurement) fell away while high-fee EVENT mandates (mineral blocks, coal blocks, property, spectrum,
FM) grew. As GMV recovered through FY26 and Q1 FY27, the take rate fell right back. Anyone modelling MSTC
off GMV x take rate will be wrong in both directions. Management confirms: "some of our revenue is based
on percentage basis and some of it is on event basis ... the Telangana ... land parcel sale. They might
have got about 2,900 or 3,000 crores odd, but what we get is for the event only. So it has got no relation
with the value" (Bhanu Kumar, Q2 FY26).

## D3. Industry - the auction mandate pipeline is genuinely accelerating
MINERALS (the actual FY26 growth engine):
- FY26: 212 mineral blocks auctioned in India, nearly DOUBLE the cumulative total of the first six years
  of the auction regime. MSTC claims to have auctioned "200+ major mineral blocks" in FY26 - implying a
  dominant share of the national pipeline (management claim, not independently verified).
- 70 further blocks carried live NITs (38 mining leases + 32 composite licences) into FY27.
- 7th tranche of Critical and Strategic Mineral blocks: 19 blocks, NIT 23-Mar-2026. MSTC allocated 10.
- 8th tranche launched 15-Jul-2026: 20 blocks across 9 states (13 new + 7 re-offered). Minerals include
  molybdenum, graphite, glauconite, REE, vanadium, gallium, titanium, tungsten, phosphorite, potash,
  lithium, caesium, rubidium.
- 2nd tranche of Exploration Licence auctions extended the EL regime to Arunachal Pradesh, UP and Odisha
  for the first time; MSTC allocated 4 EL blocks in Q1 FY27.
- Offshore mineral blocks Tranche 1 launched in FY25 with the Ministry of Mines - a new category.
- Sand/minor mineral blocks: UP model replicated in Chhattisgarh; Goa, Arunachal, Ladakh minor blocks.
This is a real, policy-driven, multi-year tailwind and it is the honest reason FY26 revenue grew 19%.

COAL - a structural fork:
- Coal Exchange Rules, 2026 notified 04-Jun-2026. Coal Controller Organisation registers and regulates
  exchanges; 25-year authorisations; delivery-based spot trading on standardised contracts; explicit
  shift from a "one-to-many" (agent) model to "many-to-many" (exchange). 16 sampling TPAs empanelled
  as of 31-Jul-2026.
- MSTC won the CIL NRS Linkage Auction ESP mandate (L1, 26-Feb-2026) for THREE YEARS via a GeM tender.
- MSTC is "examining the feasibility of setting up Coal & other Mineral exchanges" (Q1 FY27). mjunction
  has already submitted framework recommendations to the Ministry. If MSTC does not become a registered
  exchange, the agent role it just won for 3 years is the thing being disintermediated.

SCRAP / VEHICLE SCRAPPAGE:
- Scrap is ~50% of MSTC's revenue on a percentage-of-value basis, so it is directly exposed to scrap
  PRICES, which management says have been falling for several quarters ("scrap rates have fallen during
  this quarter, and in fact, it's continuing to fall" - Q1 FY26; "softening of prices in scrap, that
  trend has continued over quite a few quarters now" - Q2 FY26). Volume growth has been offsetting price.
- ELV: ~70,000 vehicles scrapped nationally in FY25, ~2 lakh in FY26, against a NITI Aayog (Jan-2026)
  potential of ~1 crore per year. Penetration ~2%. MSTC's portal is MANDATED for ELV auctions.
- Delhi EV Policy 2026 (approved Aug-2026, effective 01-Jul-2026 to 31-Mar-2030): up to Rs 1 lakh
  scrappage incentive for scrapping a BS-IV petrol/diesel car when buying an EV under Rs 30 lakh;
  100% road tax and registration fee exemption; Rs 15,000 cr state outlay over four years; electric-only
  new autorickshaw registrations from 2027 and two-wheelers from 2028.
  ** THIS IS THE STATED REASON FOR THE STOCK'S 57% ONE-MONTH RALLY. **
  Reality check from management's own mouth: ELV auction revenue "just is a drop in the total revenue of
  scrap auction" (Q1 FY26), and on the ecosystem, "we would develop as a part of the overall ecosystem
  developing and would not really be able to be the primary driver" (Q4 FY26).

EPR CERTIFICATE EXCHANGE (the stated "game changer"):
- MSTC won the CPCB mandate (tender committee recommendation 12-Sep-2025) to design, develop AND OPERATE
  the National EPR Certificate Trading Platform. Revenue model = COMMISSION ON VOLUME TRADED.
- Five waste categories currently under EPR (plastic, e-waste, battery, tyre, used oil); management
  expects "another 5 to 10 more categories" over one-two years.
- Rough market sizing (SECONDARY sources, low reliability): >50,000 PIBOs registered on the CPCB EPR
  portal; ~1.5 million tonnes of plastic waste handled annually; plastic EPR certificates quoted anywhere
  between Rs 500/tonne and Rs 20,000/tonne depending on source and category; e-waste Rs 10,000-30,000/tonne.
  On a plausible Rs 2,000-5,000 cr of annual certificate GMV and a 0.5-2% commission, the revenue range
  is Rs 10-100 cr - i.e. anywhere from 3% to 27% of FY26 revenue. THE UNCERTAINTY IS THE POINT.
- STATUS AS AT 13-Aug-2026: "developed ... awaiting go ahead for operation." FOUR consecutive quarters of
  "ready, waiting for approval". CMD: "I am not in a position to give that confirmation because it is not
  MSTC's decision." Third-party blogs claiming the platform is already live are NOT corroborated by any
  MSTC filing - treat as unverified.

TRAVEL (the MoA change):
- MSTC applied to add travel agency / tour operator objects to its MoA (board 10-Jul-2026, postal ballot
  notice 27-Jul-2026, special resolution). Prior MoA change covered data-centre business.
- MSTC Smart Travel: B2B portal "ready", being upgraded for B2C (Q1 FY27).
- Competitors named by management: Balmer Lawrie (~Rs 250 cr travel revenue; total company Rs 2,717 cr
  revenue, Rs 270 cr PAT, 10.7x P/E, 4.86% yield) and IRCTC. Government use requires empanelment; the
  Department of Expenditure entitlement process was expected to take 4-5 months from Feb-2026.
- Timeline slippage: "we should be making it operational" by FY27 Q1 (said Nov-2025); "we will be able to
  launch it by April or so" (said Feb-2026); as at Aug-2026, B2B "ready", B2C "being upgraded".
- Honest read: a PSU e-auction house entering a commoditised, thin-margin OTA market against an entrenched
  incumbent, justified on the call as "the cost associated with this; we saw that wasn't much because we
  already have established data centers and redundant capacities". Optionality, not a thesis.

TReDS:
- MSTC has APPLIED TO RBI for a TReDS licence (disclosed Q1 FY27). There are already FIVE licensed
  platforms: RXIL (SIDBI+NSE), M1xchange (Mynd Solutions), Invoicemart (A.TREDS), C2treds, KredX/DTX.
- Industry throughput ~Rs 1.4 lakh crore of bills financed in a recent year across the three largest.
  Platform fees are a few basis points, so the entire industry revenue pool is plausibly Rs 70-210 cr
  across five-plus players. MSTC would be entrant number six into a crowded, bank-relationship-driven
  market. Low expected value; label as optionality.

OTHER PIPELINE ITEMS: Upkaran (equipment leasing marketplace - launched, revenue model still not
finalised as of Nov-2025); data-centre monetisation ("not yet"); SaaS/portal development for ministries
(SIMS 2.0, Green Steel Certification, KPKB, AYUSH, Information Commission); SBI CAPS transaction-advisory
tie-up (Kolar Gold Fields tailings dumps flagged as one live opportunity with the Ministry of Mines);
Ministry of Defence strategic asset disposal and rare-earth recovery; private-sector listing platform.

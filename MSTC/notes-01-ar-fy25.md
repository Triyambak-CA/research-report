## A3. The Rs 145 cr "borrowings" is NOT debt - RESOLVED (open question 3)
Note 18 / 8.2:
- Rs 1.38 cr: Indian Overseas Bank legal fees debited since 19-Nov-2011, under protest. MSTC has sued IOB in
  Calcutta HC for Rs 36.56 cr (Rs 27.98 cr LC value + Rs 8.58 cr legal expenses).
- Rs 143.62 cr: Standard Chartered Bank claim on 2008-09 GOLD JEWELLERY export bills bought under a Receivable
  Purchase Agreement. Foreign buyers never paid; insurer (ICICI Lombard) repudiated; SCB converted the purchased
  receivables into a loan claim on MSTC; DRT Mumbai OA filed 2012.
- DRT interim order 16-Jul-2017 claiming Rs 222.51 cr was SET ASIDE by DRAT Mumbai on 07-Aug-2023. Recovery
  proceedings dropped. MSTC recovered Rs 90.00 cr pre-deposit + Rs 5.34 cr interest; attached properties released.
- SCB withdrew its suit against ICICI Lombard (Bombay HC order 17-Jan-2024); MSTC filed counterclaim against SCB.
- MSTC discloses the SAME Rs 143.62 cr SIMULTANEOUSLY as Unsecured Borrowings AND as Trade Receivables.
  => Balance sheet is GROSSED UP by Rs 143.62 cr on both sides. Reported "debt" is an accounting artefact.
  => Also inflates debtor days. Interest cost is nil because it is not a real loan.
- Finance cost FY25 total = Rs 0.18 cr ("interest paid to customers") - confirms zero real borrowing cost.

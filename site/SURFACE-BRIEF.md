# Surface brief: index.html

Mode: Read. A register of published reports; the visitor's success is opening one.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A chartered accountant's running account of companies examined, kept as a
bahi khata. Each company is a dated entry in an open account, never a verdict.
It refuses the category default this site already shipped and failed with: the
dark brokerage terminal with neon tickers, graticule and glowing figures.

OWN-WORLD: Vermilion cloth binding board holding a whole region (about a third of
the surface), unbleached ledger paper as the reading field, faded red vertical
column rules that BOUND columns and are never crossed by text, folio numbers in
tabular figures, a hairline double rule closing the register. Recognizable with
all content removed by the cloth board against ruled paper.

STORY: The visitor sees a kept book rather than a pitch, understands that seven
companies have been examined and dated, believes the author records rather than
recommends, and opens the entry they came for.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop, cloth board down the left at 28% carrying the title
stacked, the folio count and the coverage span reversed out in paper-white; the
register fills the right on paper, column heads in letterspaced caps over a heavy
rule, then seven entries, each one line, company at semibold with the note beside
it and the date right-aligned in tabular figures. The primary action is the
company name itself, the largest target in each row. Mobile turns the board into
a top band and stacks each entry.

FORM: Bahi khata, candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered grounded list, seed key 5a5fc7c2.

RAISE (from rw-timetable-slide-rack, declined): one single type size across the
whole register. Rank is carried by weight, case, reversal and rule, never by size.
This is the exact discipline the failed page lacked, where five competing sizes
fought each other.

RAISE (from operate-a-cutting-bench-select-rail, declined): the vermilion commits
to a whole region rather than being a timid accent, and status is a printed mark
in a fixed cell, never a hue.

# Surface brief: index.html

Mode: Read. A register of published reports; the visitor's success is opening one.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The index has no look of its own. It is the reports' own world applied to
a list, so that opening an entry is a continuation rather than a change of site.
It refuses both the category default (a dark brokerage terminal with neon tickers
and a graticule through the text) and the temptation to give the front door a
personality the rooms behind it do not share.

OWN-WORLD: The reports' two themes verbatim, glass default and terminal alternate,
same tokens, same three faces, same faint grid canvas, same floating control.
Recognizable with all content removed by the black ground, the hairline grid and
the mono meta rail punctuated with double slashes.

STORY: The visitor recognizes the surface as the same publication they were linked
to, sees six dated entries and what each covers, and opens the one they came for.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Display title in Cormorant at up to 78px, a lede at 62ch beneath
it, a mono meta rail carrying the count, the coverage span and "no rating issued"
in the accent colour, a rule, then the register head and the first three entries.
The primary action is the company name, set at 16px against 14px notes.

FORM: Inherited world. No concept roll: a coherent identity already exists in the
report code, and section 1 of new-work says to inherit rather than replace it.

SUPERSEDED: a bahi khata direction (seed 5a5fc7c2, candidate 7) was built here
first. It was dropped on the captain's instruction to match the reports. Its one
surviving discipline is that column rules bound columns and never cross text.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

### A0. Method and a hard finding
- No Indian listed pure-play maker of stainless-steel flexible hose, metal bellows or expansion joints was identified. Searches (18-Nov-2026) returned only unlisted names: Witzenmann India (Chennai, subsidiary of German parent), Flexpert Bellows, Flexatherm Expanllow, Belman Flexibles India, Ocelflex, Shivam Cast Products, Precise Engineers, LSI Mech, MB Metallic Bellows (directory listings, secondary). Verdict: **no listed Indian like-for-like peer verified.** The comparables below are therefore adjacent (SS pipes/tubes, engineered flow, data-centre cooling exposure) plus global.
- Aeroflex's promoter entity (Aeroflex Enterprises Ltd, formerly Sat Industries) is listed but is a holding entity, not a peer.

### A6. Who is the "leading global provider of digital infrastructure and thermal management"? INFERENCE: Vertiv. Not stated in any filing.

Evidence for:
1. Aeroflex decks Q1FY27 and Q2FY27 label the SFN skid customer only as "Approved supplier to a leading global provider of digital infrastructure and thermal management" (source-docs/calls/Q1FY27-deck.txt line 332; Q2FY27-deck.txt line 570). No filing, press release or results note names the customer. A text search of all source-docs for "Vertiv" hits one file only: the Q2FY27 transcript.
2. Q2FY27 call, 29-Oct-2026: management says it works "with one customer exclusively for India", "this very large U.S. multinational company"; "the biggest player in this industry is Vertiv Energy globally ... and they are also the biggest ones in India" (Q2FY27-transcript.txt lines 350-370).
3. Same call: an analyst asks whether "our current skid assemblies are compatible with Vertiv's planned 800 VDC data centers". Management answers without correcting the premise: "the design team of Vertiv works in close connection with our design team to develop the SFN design ... our products will be in accordance to what is the requirement by Vertiv" (lines 825-836). Closest thing to an acknowledgement. It is still an oral answer, not a disclosure.
4. "Secondary Fluid Network (SFN)" and "SFN skid" are Vertiv product terms (Vertiv 12-Oct-2026 deployment coverage, secondary). Aeroflex uses the same term and acronym.
5. Aeroflex Q1FY26 call (29-Jul-2025): "the U.S. Company has received the contract for the data center cooling for the India market". Q2FY26: "we will be their sole suppliers for the liquid cooling project in India ... speaking to the top level of their company in India". Q4FY26 (06-May-2026): skids are "100% in the domestic market only"; no patent because the product is jointly developed with the principal. All consistent with Vertiv India-built skids.
6. Vertiv (secondary, 12-Oct-2026): built a production facility in India to mass-manufacture technology-loop skids for the 30 MW CapitaLand Mumbai project; city not stated. A search summary of a Jan-2024 dcnnmagazine item says Vertiv India's third facility joins plants at Ambernath and Pune (not verified against Vertiv). A Vertiv-run skid plant and Aeroflex as outsourced fabricator or second source are not mutually exclusive; no document says which.

Evidence against / gaps:
- No written disclosure (filing, press release, deck) links the two by name; only the oral Q2FY27 call answers above do. A web search on Aeroflex plus Vertiv plus skid customer (18-Nov-2026) returned nothing connecting them. One promotional article (indiaipo.in) asserts Aeroflex builds CDU skids for Vertiv's Indian hyperscale sites and has a second "50+ billion dollar US-listed" customer; the page returned HTTP 410 when opened, so both claims are **UNVERIFIED secondary**.
- The skids are SFN piping skids, not CDUs. Treat "CDU" in media as loose usage.
- Other candidates matching the phrase were not tested. Treat as probable, not established.

Concentration (derived; customer share not disclosed): skid revenue Rs 50.03 cr of Rs 207 cr consolidated Q2FY27 revenue = 24% (working-notes item 2), from a single customer "exclusively for India" per management.

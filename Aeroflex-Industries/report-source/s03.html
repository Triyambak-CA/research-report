Section 03

Competitive landscape and peer benchmarking

There is no listed Indian company that makes what Aeroflex makes. Its real competitors are unlisted (Witzenmann India, Senior Flexonics Pathfinder India, a tail of small Indian bellows makers) or global (Senior Flexonics, Witzenmann, BOA). In liquid cooling, no listed Indian company fabricates SFN skids; the competitor that matters may be the customer itself. The peer table below is therefore adjacent, not like-for-like, and the honest conclusion is that Aeroflex leads its Indian adjacent set on margin and growth and trades near the top of it on multiples.

Why the peer set is adjacent

Searches on 18-Nov-2026 found only unlisted Indian makers of stainless hose, bellows and expansion joints: Witzenmann India (Chennai), Flexpert Bellows, Flexatherm Expanllow, Belman Flexibles India, Ocelflex, Shivam Cast Products, Precise Engineers, LSI Mech, MB Metallic Bellows (directory listings, secondary). The comparables are grouped by what they share with Aeroflex: stainless and engineered-flow manufacturing (set a), Indian data-centre cooling exposure (set b), and global thermal and fluid-handling businesses (set c). Aeroflex Enterprises, the listed parent, is a holding company and not a peer.

Reading the gap. On growth Aeroflex is closing on the leaders quickly, but the two halves of the growth tell different stories. In FY26 export hose grew 6.2%, at market pace, and domestic sales grew 43.9%, more than half of it skids. In H1FY27 exports accelerated to about +46% and skids added Rs 82.42 cr (derived from deck mix; notes-06 A5). Scale stays an order of magnitude below Witzenmann. Peers in distress or exiting: Senior's take-private and Witzenmann's sales decline and pay cuts are the clearest openings in the core business; a private-equity owner may also price harder (inference). In liquid cooling no Indian listed peer competes directly. The competitive risk there is the customer: if the skid customer is Vertiv (inference), its own Indian skid facility is the alternative supplier.

How this report was built - read before relying on any figure

All primary documents below were downloaded and read on 18-Nov-2026 from BSE, NSE archives, SEBI and the company's investor-relations site. Scanned filings (the Sep-2026 and earlier results, the credit-rating letter, the Hyd-Air accounts) were rendered to images and read page by page, because their text layers are damaged. Figures are consolidated unless marked standalone. Inferences are labelled as inferences; secondary sources are labelled where used. The identity of the skid customer is never stated as fact: management calls it a large US multinational and has not named it. This report carries no rating and no price target, by design.

Corrections made during the research

  • An early working note said the objects of the Feb-2026 preferential issue shifted from capex to working capital at the 05-Jan-2026 corrigendum. The record shows a same-day conflict instead: the 18-Dec-2025 board outcome says the money funds capex, and the EOGM notice of the same date already says working capital and general corporate purposes. Section 7 reports the conflict as it is.
  • The results note puts the after-tax effect of the depreciation change at Rs 8.23 cr (consolidated); the P&L books deferred tax on it. This report uses its own after-tax recomputation (Section 5).

What could not be verified

  • NSE corporate-announcement, pledge and SAST APIs were not run in this session (the browser session needed for them was refused). Bulk-deal, delivery and some holder-category series are secondary.
  • ICRA monitoring reports on the IPO proceeds were not read; the statements of deviation were used instead.
  • The FY22 consolidated balance sheet and cash flow were not extracted, so the five-year tables start at FY23.
  • Nine of the fourteen investor decks were read from their text layers only; their KPI slides, which are images, were not rendered.
  • The recipient of the Rs 1.416 cr advisory fee on the preferential issue is not disclosed in any document read.
  • The skid customer's identity, contract volumes and the skid margin are not disclosed.
  • The US tariff rate on Aeroflex's main HS 8307 lines after the 2025-2026 Section 232 derivative listings could not be pinned to a single figure (Section 11).

Internal contradictions found in the company's own record

  • Annual report: "no concentration of credit risk as the customer base is widely distributed"; MD on 06-May-2026: "our biggest customer in the international market contributes about 25% to 26% of our sales" (above 10% of revenue on two of the three possible readings of "our sales"); skids, one customer, are 24% of Q2 FY27 revenue.
  • Results note 5: after-tax effect of the depreciation change Rs 8.23 cr consolidated, and a standalone after-tax effect larger than the pre-tax effect.
  • Exchange filing 16-Oct-2025: an order "under the existing global agreement"; calls: an India-only, five-year exclusive contract, with international talks starting a year later.
  • 18-Dec-2025: the board outcome says the preferential money funds capex; the EOGM notice of the same date says working capital and general corporate purposes.
  • "We don't plan to take on any debt" (29-Jan-2026); "bridge loan financing" (29-Oct-2026).
  • COO resignation: effective 30-Jun-2025 in the filing, 20-May-2025 in the FY26 annual report.
  • FY25 expense lines differ between the Dec-2025 and Mar-2026 results filings (totals agree).
  • The company website still shows the Infomerics rating that was withdrawn on 16-Oct-2023.

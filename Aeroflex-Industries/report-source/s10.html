What the scuttlebutt adds

Three things the filings do not show plainly. First, the customer most likely to be Aeroflex's skid buyer has publicly built its own Indian skid capacity, so the skid line's competitor may be its customer's plant (inference on identity, secondary on the plant). Second, the operating and finance bench is thin and turning over: a COO who lasted five months, a new company secretary, a CFO handover in the quarter of an accounting change, and a single voice on every call. Third, the hose channel visible in US shipment data is pipe-joining and hose distribution, which fits a distributor-led export business rather than OEM lock-in. None of this is verified fact; each item is a question for management.

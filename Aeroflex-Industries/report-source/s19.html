Data-aggregator insights, cross-checked

Screener's premium Insights panel (pulled from the owner's logged-in session on 18-Nov-2026) is the only place Aeroflex's operating series sits in one table: hose capacity and utilisation, export mix, SKUs, assembly stations and skid volumes. Laid against the decks and filings it cites, the capacity and skid series hold; the utilisation series mixes bases and mis-dates two statements; one assembly-station figure is a target recorded as an actual. The bigger traps are in screener's derived numbers: a trailing P/E that includes a one-off depreciation catch-up, a "free cash flow" that contains an acquisition, and an "almost debt free" tag that ignores Rs 23 cr of leases and Rs 49 cr of tax demands.

Restatements a one-document read would miss

  • Q1 FY25 deck re-filed as "Revised". The prior-year Q1 FY24 column gained an exceptional gain of Rs 0.16 cr that the original did not show; PBT went from Rs 14.54 cr to Rs 14.70 cr and PAT from Rs 10.93 cr to Rs 11.09 cr, so reported growth fell from 13.56% to 11.91% (PAT). Q1 FY25 itself did not change. The only flag is the word "Revised" on the cover letter; no explanation is given.
  • Product mix restated silently three times. H1 FY25: hoses 52%, assemblies and fittings 40%, others 8% (Q2FY25 deck p15), shown a year later as hoses 52%, assemblies and others 48% (Q2FY26 deck p18). FY25: 48/52 (Q4FY25 deck p17) became 49/51 (Q4FY26 deck p12). H1 FY26: 46/54 (Q2FY26 deck) became 47/53 (Q2FY27 deck). None is explained. The "assemblies" bucket now includes fittings, bellows and, in the narrative, skids (Section 8), so the 60-70% assemblies target is measured on a moving base.
  • Geography regrouped: H1 FY26 exports were Europe 24%, Americas 60%, Asia 13%, Africa 3% (Q2FY26 deck); a year later the same half shows Europe 23%, Americas 60%, others 17% (Q2FY27 deck). The domestic-export split has not been restated in any deck checked.
  • FY25 costs in two versions. The FY25 column in the Dec-2025 results shows materials Rs 220.06 cr, employee Rs 35.28 cr and other expenses Rs 38.11 cr; the Mar-2026 results show Rs 215.86 cr, Rs 33.79 cr and Rs 43.80 cr. Totals, PBT and PAT agree; the cost lines do not reconcile to each other or to the sum of the quarters.
  • EBITDA re-based: from the Q4 FY26 deck, EBITDA excludes other income; FY25 EBITDA moves from Rs 81.58 cr to Rs 79.05 cr, widening the reported FY26 growth to 26.18% (Section 8).

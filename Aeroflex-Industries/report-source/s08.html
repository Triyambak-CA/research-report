Section 08

Management guidance scorecard

Aeroflex has held an earnings call every quarter since listing, 13 in all, and the MD gives numbers freely. Scored against what was delivered, 11 of 32 resolvable commitments were met: a credibility ratio of 34.4%. The pattern is sharp. EBITDA growth and margin, which management controls most directly, were delivered every year. Capacity dates slip by one to six quarters as a rule. Revenue promises for anything new miss by 70-90%, with one exception, skids, where the customer sets the schedule. And the one headline profit miss, FY26, was later described as a target achieved.

The calls exist: no aggregator trap here

All 13 transcripts, Q2 FY24 (08-Nov-2023) to Q2 FY27 (29-Oct-2026), are genuine Reg 30 transcript filings, one per quarter since the 31-Aug-2023 listing (the Q3 FY24 transcript was filed twice). Each comes with an investor deck (14 decks, including a revised Q1 FY25 deck, Section 19). Screener's concall rows therefore are calls, not decks. Quotes are taken from the transcripts and cited by each transcript's own printed page number; the scorecard quotes were checked as exact substrings by script (notes-04).

! The FY26 profit miss, described as a target met

On 02-May-2025 the MD guided FY26 bottom-line growth of "at least 20% to 25% minimum" and confirmed it ("Yes, definitely. Definitely.") (Q4FY25 call p6, p9). On 29-Jul-2025, asked about "20% to 25% PAT growth", he answered only on EBITDA, cut it to "more than 20%" and added "PAT growth will be slightly lower because we have higher depreciation" (Q1FY26 call p5). FY26 PAT grew 5.75%. On 06-May-2026: "we have been able to achieve the targets that we had set out at the start of the financial year" (Q4FY26 call p4). No analyst challenged it. The depreciation that caused the miss was then switched from WDV to straight-line from 01-Apr-2026, adding about Rs 17 cr a year to PBT, announced on 29-Oct-2026 after the 157% PAT growth headline had been read out (Q2FY27 call p5; Section 6). The fair reading is that "bottom line" may have been loose speech for EBITDA, which did grow 26%. It was still never withdrawn in those words.

Definitions that moved, each time in the flattering direction

  • EBITDA: from the Q4 FY26 deck, EBITDA excludes other income, which restates FY25 from Rs 81.58 cr to Rs 79.05 cr. FY26 growth shows as 26.18% and +156 bps; on the old definition it is +23.9% and +127 bps. Both clear guidance, but the switch widened the beat.
  • Assemblies share: about 33% of sales (Q4FY24 call p20); 49% for 9M FY25, "a year ahead of schedule" (Q3FY25 call p2); by Q3 FY26 the bucket "includes the assemblies, fittings, bellows and skid assemblies" (Q3FY26 call p3). The 60-70% target is now measured on a bucket that includes a new business.
  • The 25% margin goal has been restated six times: "four to five years" (May-2024), "by FY '27" (Jan-2025), "the next couple of years" (2026), FY30 (Oct-2026); and from a hose-business ambition to "a 25% blended margin combined as a whole" (Q1FY27 call p4). The quoted segment margins (hoses 15-20%, assemblies 22-26%) have not moved in three years, so the group gain is mix.
  • A retroactive target: "last year we had set out a target of increasing our EBITDA margin by approximately 100 basis points every year" (Q3FY25 call p10). No such statement is in the five earlier transcripts; the closest is "increase our margins on a QoQ basis" (Q3FY24 call p8).
  • The profit metric: "cash PAT" appeared in the opening remarks on the four calls (Q2 FY26 to Q1 FY27) when PAT lagged EBITDA because of WDV depreciation, and was dropped on Q2 FY27, when cash profit margin fell 49 bps and reported PAT was the better number.

Who answers, and who is listening

The MD answers everything. Asad Daud gives the opening remarks and every answer on all 13 calls; the only other management voice is one line on inventory days (Q2FY24 p10). The whole-time director and CFO, Mustafa Kachwala, signs the transcript cover letters and has not spoken on any call; no COO, investor-relations head or business head has spoken. That is why the depreciation and EBITDA-definition changes got no technical explanation on any call.

Reading: attendance rose with the data-centre story, a discovery-phase signature; institutional attendance fell at the same time, which matches the register (mutual funds 4.87% to 0.92%, Section 13). The audience that now asks the questions is the one least likely to press on accounting and capital allocation.

Counter-argument, fairly stated: management has delivered the EBITDA guidance it controls every year, the skid business beat its first revenue guidance, and capacity slippage of a quarter or two is normal for a small manufacturer importing machines. The MD volunteers numbers that many small-cap managements withhold, including skid volumes and prices. The problem is not silence; it is that the numbers are restated, re-based or re-described when they miss.

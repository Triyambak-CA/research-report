Section 12

Triggers, themes and government scheme plays

The next four quarters test one guidance number and one funding plan. Management has guided FY27 revenue of Rs 715-725 cr "at the least" (+62-64%), with Rs 150-200 cr from skids, after H1 delivered Rs 352.74 cr; H2 therefore needs about Rs 362-372 cr, roughly Q2's run rate held for two quarters. In parallel, the Rs 202 cr expansion to Mar-2028 needs a funding decision that the company has said may include bridge loans and fresh equity. There is no order book to anchor either: the company has never disclosed one.

Order book and visibility

No numeric order book has ever been given for the hose business ("we specifically don't have like a specific number for an order book", Q1FY26 p9). For skids the only figure ever stated was a Rs 45 cr pipeline (29-Jan-2026), which took two quarters, not one, to ship. Firm visibility is a dispatch schedule about two months ahead and "confirmed supply over the next 3 months" (Q2FY27 p6); beyond that is the customer's forecast, and the five-year agreement fixes exclusivity, not volumes (notes-04 Part 7). Book-to-bill and execution timeline cannot be computed.

Is the market paying for these themes?

Data-centre and AI cooling: fully paid for and getting crowded. The stock trades at 72x trailing earnings (SA, 18-Nov-2026), about double Vertiv (35.6x), and at 2.7x the Rs 182.70 preferential price of Feb-2026. The skid line went from 46 to 1,475 units in four quarters; the rerating ran with it. Listed Indian cooling names (Blue Star, Voltas, KRN, Netweb) trade at 36-53x EV/EBITDA on the same theme. Export manufacturing / China+1: early and unpaid as a theme for hose; the core business grew at market pace in FY26 and accelerated only in H1FY27. Tariff relief: unpriced optionality; a signed US-India deal would help 60-66% of exports, but no schedule exists.

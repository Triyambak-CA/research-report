## Claim-by-claim

1. **Note 5 PAT effect is wrong - SURVIVES.** The consolidated effect 823.48 implies 2.1% tax. The standalone effect (769.46 > 769.02) implies negative tax. Splitting consolidated less standalone gives Hyd-Air 72.23 PBT and 54.02 PAT, which is 25.2% tax. So the error is that the parent's slice was never tax-effected. Book depreciation does not change income-tax depreciation. Under Ind AS 12 the full 841.25 therefore raises deferred tax by about 211.7. After-tax effect = 841.25 x 0.74832 = **629.5 lakh (6.30 cr)**. The deck inherits the error: H1 WDV tax of 19.97 is 20.15 less 0.18. On WDV PBT of 67.09 that implies a 29.8% rate. The deck's Q2 WDV tax of 12.87 on 41.19 implies 31.2%.
2. **"P&L books the deferred tax correctly" - SURVIVES ONLY AS "BROADLY".** Deferred tax rose to 192.45 (H1 FY26: 10.35). DTL rose from 89.79 to 282.24. Taking out about 211.7 leaves a WDV-basis H1 tax rate of 26.9%, close to H1 FY26's 27.3%. The split does not tie, though. The parent shows 151.64 against about 193.5 expected; the 150 bad-debt provision explains about 38 of the gap. Hyd-Air shows about 40.8 against about 18. Reported PAT is not overstated by the note's error, but "correctly" cannot be shown.
3. **H1 WDV PAT about 49.2 and Q2 about 30.4 - SURVIVES with nudged figures.** The marginal method gives **49.06** (H1) and **30.26** (Q2). That is growth of **+112.6%** against reported +156.9%. The 49.2 and 30.4 come from applying the 26.7% effective rate. That is wrong for a depreciation-only change, though the gap is immaterial (about 0.13 cr). Do not use the deck's 47.12 or 28.32.
4. **Clean SLM Q2 depreciation 4.1-4.3, PBT overstated 3.7-3.8 - SURVIVES.** The deck splits SLM as Q1 4.02 and Q2 4.27, both rounded. Catch-up = 784.41 - about 402 = **about 382 (3.82 cr)**. That is about 2.86 cr after tax. **Clean Q2 SLM PAT is about 33.70 cr**, not the deck's 34.00. The deck's SLM tax split is incoherent: Q2 SLM tax of 11.78 is below Q2 WDV tax of 12.87 on a higher PBT. The split rests only on the deck's rounded Q1 SLM figure.
5. **Annualised uplift about 17 cr, 23% of FY26 PBT - SURVIVES as an extrapolation only.** 2 x 8.41 = 16.83 cr, which is 22.7% of 74.08. The gap is not constant. The Q2 run-rate (8.86 - 4.27 = 4.59) gives 18.4 cr a year. WDV on old assets declines, and new capex widens the gap.

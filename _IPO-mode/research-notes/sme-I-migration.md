# I. MIGRATION TO MAINBOARD

## I.1 Regulatory layer (ICDR, verified against consolidated text amended up to 21-Mar-2026)
- Reg 277: SME-listed issuer with post-issue PAID-UP capital > Rs 10 crore and up to Rs 25 crore may migrate to main board if (a) special resolution through postal ballot, with non-promoter votes in favour >= 2x non-promoter votes against, and (b) it meets the main board exchange's eligibility criteria.
- Reg 280(2): if a further issue would take paid-up capital beyond Rs 25 crore, migration is MANDATORY - except (proviso w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025) the issuer may stay on the SME exchange if it undertakes to comply with main-board LODR in full.
- Reg 276: reverse migration (main board to SME) possible below Rs 25 crore paid-up capital with the same 2:1 special-resolution mechanics.

## I.2 NSE Emerge -> NSE main board (NSE circular NSE/CML/67671 dated 24-Apr-2025, effective 01-May-2025 - PDF saved nse-cml-67671.pdf)
- Paid-up equity capital >= Rs 10 crore AND average market capitalisation >= Rs 100 crore (price = average of weekly high/low of closing prices for 3 months preceding application x post-issue shares).
- Revenue from operations > Rs 100 crore in the last FY AND positive operating profit in >= 2 of 3 FYs.
- Listed on NSE SME platform >= 3 years.
- Public shareholders >= 500 at application date.
- Promoter + promoter group >= 20% at application; AND promoters' holding must be >= 50% of what they held ON THE DATE OF LISTING (anti-selldown condition - promoters who dumped cannot migrate).
- Other: no IBC admission (issuer or promoting company); no admitted winding-up petition; NET WORTH >= Rs 75 crore; no material regulatory action in past 3 years; no SEBI debarment; no director disqualification; no pending SCORES complaints; 2-month cooling-off from exit out of trade-to-trade/surveillance action on any exchange; no debenture/bond/FD payment defaults.

## I.3 BSE SME -> BSE main board (BSE Notice 20250820-11 dated 20-Aug-2025, criteria effective 11-Aug-2025 - PDF saved bse-mig-annexA.pdf; supersedes Notice 20231124-55 dated 24-Nov-2023)
- Paid-up capital >= Rs 10 crore.
- Average 6-month market capitalisation >= Rs 100 crore (was Rs 25 crore pre-Aug-2025).
- Market liquidity tests over the 6 months: >= 5% of weighted average listed shares traded; trading on >= 80% of days; min average daily turnover Rs 10 lakh and min daily turnover Rs 5 lakh; min average daily trades 50 and min daily trades 25.
- Operating profit (EBIDTA): average Rs 15 crore on restated consolidated basis over preceding 3 years, positive in each year, minimum Rs 10 crore in EACH of the 3 years.
- Net worth Rs 1 crore in each of preceding 3 years (restated, consolidated); net tangible assets >= Rs 3 crore in each of preceding 3 years, of which not more than 50% in monetary assets (else firm commitments to deploy).
- Promoter holding >= 20% at application (promoter group counts toward shortfall); 100% promoter holding in demat.
- LODR compliance track record 3 years with no pending non-compliance; listed >= 3 years (was 2 years); public shareholders >= 1,000 (was 250).
- Regulatory cleanliness: no continuing SEBI debarment (company/promoters/directors or their other companies); not wilful defaulter/fraudulent borrower; not fugitive economic offenders; no NCLT winding-up/CIRP admission; not suspended for non-compliance in last 12 months; NOT under ESM/ASM/GSM/T2T surveillance at application, with 2-month cooling-off after exit; no pending SCORES complaints; no pending bond/FD defaults; CRA certificate for utilisation of IPO proceeds and further issues; same line of business 3 years with >= 50% revenue from that continued activity; no continuing going-concern or materially adverse audit qualification.

## I.4 Why migration is a genuine value trigger, and the analyst checklist
- Migration unlocks: quarterly disclosure, full LODR CG regime, index/institutional eligibility (many funds cannot buy SME-platform scrips), normal lot-size trading (lot of 1), better liquidity/impact cost, research coverage. Historical migrations often re-rate.
- But the 2025 bars are HIGH: NSE demands Rs 100 cr revenue + Rs 75 cr net worth; BSE demands Rs 15 cr average EBITDA. Most SME issuers will NEVER qualify - price in permanence of the SME regime, not migration, as the base case.
- Checklist for migration-eligibility (compute at report date): paid-up capital > Rs 10 cr; years since listing vs 3-year floor; market cap vs Rs 100 cr; revenue/EBITDA/net worth vs exchange floors; public shareholder count (half-yearly pattern); promoter holding vs 50%-of-listing-date test (NSE); surveillance status; SCORES; audit qualifications; LODR compliance record.
- Watch the mandatory-migration dodge: an issuer crossing Rs 25 crore paid-up capital via bonus/rights can now stay on SME by undertaking mainboard-LODR compliance (Reg 280(2) proviso, w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025) - if it does, ask why it prefers to stay (usually: cannot meet mainboard entry norms).

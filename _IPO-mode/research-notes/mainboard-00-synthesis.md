# Synthesis: extending the 20-section deep-dive skill with an IPO mode (mainboard)

Basis: files mainboard-01-A through -L in this folder. Every hard number below is verified against the SEBI (ICDR) Regulations 2018 consolidated text as amended to 21-Mar-2026 (downloaded from sebi.gov.in on 01-Sep-2026, local: icdr-2026-03-21.pdf) or the cited circular/gazette. ## 2. NEW IPO-only sections (numbered as additions)

N1. OFFER STRUCTURE AND ELIGIBILITY ROUTE
Read the cover page and "Other Regulatory and Statutory Disclosures". State: fresh vs OFS split in Rs crore and %; eligibility route - Reg 6(1) or 6(2) - and WHICH 6(1) test failed if 6(2); allocation split that follows (50/15/35 vs 75/15/10); pre-IPO placement done or contemplated; minimum public offer tier under amended SCRR Rule 19(2)(b). Flag: 6(2) route = failed the Rs 15 crore avg-operating-profit / 3-year-positive test; OFS > 60% of issue = monetisation event. Verify the route in BOTH DRHP and RHP - it can change (ESDS changed 6(2) to 6(1)).

N2. USE-OF-PROCEEDS FORENSICS
Recompute GCP % of gross proceeds vs the 25% cap (Reg 7(2)) and GCP + unidentified-acquisition % vs 35%/25% caps (Reg 7(3)); a number sitting at the cap is a design choice - say so. For loan-repayment objects: list lenders, match to indebtedness note, flag promoter-linked debt and guarantee releases. For working-capital objects: audit the holding-norm assumptions against restated history. For capex objects: orders-placed %, land status, appraisal existence. Name the monitoring agency (mandatory if fresh issue > Rs 100 crore, Reg 41) and diarise its quarterly Schedule XI reports (45 days after each quarter).

N3. BASIS FOR ISSUE PRICE AUDIT (KPI + WACA)
Extract every KPI, its definition, its certifier, and the audit-committee confirmation; check the 3-year investor-KPI lookback was honoured; diff DRHP KPI set vs RHP KPI set and flag drops. Tabulate WACA: 18-month primary, 18-month secondary, fallback last-5 transactions, per-promoter/seller cost (cover page + abridged prospectus item 9). State floor and cap as multiples of each WACA and stress-test the issuer's (4)(d) justification against actual KPI growth. Pull the price-band advertisement (2 working days pre-open, Reg 29(4)) and quote the independent-directors' committee recommendation.

N4. POST-LISTING SUPPLY CALENDAR
Build the unlock table from the Capital Structure chapter: T+30 (50% anchor), T+90 (50% anchor), T+6m (all non-promoter pre-issue capital, adjusted for AIF-I/II/VCF/FVCI purchase-date starts and day-1-free ESOP shares), T+6m or T+12m (promoter excess), T+18m or T+36m (promoter minimum contribution; capex-majority test decides). For each row: shares, % of post-issue capital, holder identity, holder WACA multiple at cap price, x days of expected traded value. Flag any issue where >40% of capital unlocks at T+6m on >3x cost.

N5. ANCHOR BOOK AND SUBSCRIPTION QUALITY
From the exchange anchor-allocation PDF (published before opening, Sch XIII (10)(i)): compute domestic-MF share vs the 33.33% floor and insurer/pension share vs 6.67% (regime from 01-Dec-2025); count distinct decision-makers; flag AIF/NBFC/serial-flipper-dominated books; check no lead-manager associates or promoter-related QIBs (Sch XIII (10)(k)). Read day-3 subscription: QIB multiple vs NII (big/small separately) vs retail; NII-led books with weak QIB = GMP-chasing lottery money (SEBI 02-Sep-2024 study: NIIs sell 63.3% within a week). Post-listing, check bulk/block data at T+30/T+90 for anchor exits by name.

N6. DRHP-TO-RHP DIFF
Run the file-J method: eligibility route, issue size, fresh/OFS mix, financial-period roll, overlapping-year restated-number changes, risk factors added, KPI/peer-set changes, capital-structure events (placement, conversions), auditor/BRLM changes. Sixty to ninety minutes; report the deltas as "what SEBI's review surfaced and what the issuer re-engineered".

N7. INTERMEDIARY TRACK RECORD (BRLM SCORING)
From "Price information of past issues handled by the lead manager(s)" (Sch VI (14)(R)): per pricing-BRLM, median benchmark-adjusted 30-day and 180-day returns and % of issues below offer at 180 days, over the disclosed 3 FYs (max 10 issues each). Cross-check completeness against external mandate lists. State the BRLM's discount/premium base rate next to the valuation section. Check auditor peer-review status and any auditor change before filing (re-audit trigger, file I).

N8. RESTATEMENT AND LITIGATION SWEEP
Table the audited-vs-restated PAT/equity reconciliation for each of the 3 years; explain every material adjustment; flag unquantifiable qualifications carried in notes. Recompute the litigation materiality floor (lower of 2% turnover / 2% net worth / 5% of 3-yr avg |PAT|, w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025) and compare with the issuer's chosen policy threshold; sum litigation vs issuer/promoters/directors/subsidiaries as % of net worth. Note the post-2021 group-company disclosure gap (names only + top-5 website financials) and pull MCA data for the rest of the promoter group.

## 3. Known traps (IPO-specific analyst mistakes)

1. Reading the DRHP when the RHP exists (stale size/route/financials) - or reading neither and working off aggregator summaries.
2. Treating the industry chapter as independent research: it is commissioned and paid for by the issuer (standard disclosure, file H); TAM definitions are drawn to flatter.
3. Missing the eligibility route: not noticing a Reg 6(2) filing (failed profitability test, 75% QIB floor, 10% retail cap) - or missing that the route CHANGED between DRHP and RHP.
4. Valuing off the DRHP's stated P/E table without auditing the issuer-chosen peer set and the denominators (pre-issue vs post-issue, diluted vs basic, stub annualised or not).
5. Ignoring WACA: recommending at 5-10x the price insiders paid 12 months ago without addressing the mandated explanation - the reg forces the issuer to explain it; the analyst must test that explanation, not skip it.
6. Confusing OFS proceeds with company money - "raising Rs 2,000 crore" headlines when the company receives Rs 300 crore fresh; objects narratives only govern the fresh slice.
7. No supply calendar: being surprised by the T+30 anchor unlock and the T+6-month pre-IPO-investor cliff; forgetting ESOP shares and AIF purchase-date lock-in starts are earlier than 6 months.
8. Reading oversubscription as conviction: SEBI's own study - 54% of allotted shares (ex-anchor) sold within a week; NII books are lottery-and-flip money under the post-Apr-2022 draw-of-lots regime.
9. Trusting the pre-IPO margin inflection: FY(T-1) profits often carry restatement help, RPT cleanups, one-off other income, or coincide exactly with clearing the Reg 6(1)(b) threshold - check the restatement reconciliation.
10. Missing the group-company blind spot: since 13-Aug-2021 there is NO obligation to disclose loss-making or negative-net-worth group companies in the offer document - absence of bad news is not evidence.
11. Anchoring on GMP: unregulated, unverifiable, manipulable; SEBI's Nov-2025 consultation classes it with "unverified social media posts". Sentiment footnote at most, never valuation.
12. Assuming quarterly data will exist soon: the first exchange-filed quarterly results arrive only after the first full quarter-end following listing (LODR results timeline; day-count not verified here); sections 5/8/19 stay degraded for 2-3 quarters.
13. Forgetting the price band is not in the RHP: band, ratios at both ends and WACA multiples arrive via the price-band advertisement 2 working days before opening - schedule the analysis around it.
14. Treating "monitoring agency appointed" as assurance while never reading the quarterly Schedule XI reports that follow.

## 4. Verified hard numbers (copy-ready)

All ICDR references = SEBI (ICDR) Regulations 2018 as amended to 21-Mar-2026 unless stated.

ELIGIBILITY AND ROUTE
- Net tangible assets >= Rs 3 crore each of preceding 3 full years; monetary assets <= 50% (waived for pure OFS) - Reg 6(1)(a)
- Average operating profit >= Rs 15 crore over preceding 3 years, positive in EACH year - Reg 6(1)(b)
- Net worth >= Rs 1 crore in each of preceding 3 years - Reg 6(1)(c)
- Name change within 1 year: >= 50% revenue from new-name activity - Reg 6(1)(d)
- Alternative route: book-building with >= 75% of net offer to QIBs, refund if not - Reg 6(2)
- Minimum promoters' contribution 20% of post-issue capital - Reg 14(1); shortfall pluggable up to 10% of post-issue capital by AIF/FVCI/SCB/PFI/insurer/>=5% non-individual public shareholder/promoter-group entity - proviso to Reg 14(1) (extended 18-May-2024)

OFFER DOCUMENT AND PROCESS
- Restated consolidated financials: 3 years + stub; stub if latest FY older than 6 months at filing; stub end within 6 months of filing - Sch VI Part A (11)(I)(A)(i)
- Financials <= 6 months old at issue opening - Reg 24(5) (w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025)
- SEBI observations: within 30 days of latest trigger event - Reg 25(4)
- DRHP public comments: >= 21 days from public announcement; announcement within 2 working days of filing - Reg 26(1)-(2)
- Issue must open within 12 months of observations - Reg 44(1); within 18 months under pre-filing route (UDRHP-I within 16 months) - Chapter IIA
- Issue opens >= 3 working days after RHP filed with RoC - Reg 44(2)
- Price band announced >= 2 working days before opening; ratios at both ends; on exchange websites and pre-filled in forms - Reg 29(4)-(6)
- Price band width: cap <= 120% of floor price AND cap >= 105% of floor price - Reg 29(2) and proviso
- Bid period 3-10 working days; +3 working days on price-band revision; force-majeure extension minimum 1 working day (was 3; changed 18-May-2024) - Reg 46, Sch XIII (9)
- Minimum subscription 90% of offer through offer document (not for pure OFS) - Reg 45(1)
- Minimum 1,000 allottees - Reg 49(1)
- Retail minimum application value Rs 10,000-15,000 - Reg 47(2)
- Listing T+3 working days from issue close (mandatory for issues opening on/after 01-Dec-2023) - SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/TPD1/CIR/P/2023/140 dated 09-Aug-2023

USE OF PROCEEDS
- GCP <= 25% of amount raised - Reg 7(2)
- GCP + unidentified acquisition/investment objects <= 35%; unidentified acquisition objects alone <= 25% - Reg 7(3) (w.e.f. 14-Jan-2022)
- Monitoring agency (SEBI-registered credit rating agency) if fresh issue > Rs 100 crore; quarterly Schedule XI report till 100% utilised; public within 45 days of quarter end - Reg 41
- Loan-repayment object: lender-wise details + statutory-auditor utilisation certificate - Sch VI Part A (9)(A)(2)

LOCK-INS
- Promoter minimum contribution: 18 months from allotment; 3 years if majority of fresh proceeds is capex - Reg 16(1)(a)
- Promoter excess: 6 months; 1 year if capex-majority - Reg 16(1)(b); capex includes repayment of capex loans (08-Mar-2025)
- Non-promoter pre-issue capital: 6 months from allotment - Reg 17(1); VCF/AIF-I/II/FVCI: 6 months from their purchase date - proviso (c); pre-IPO ESOP shares exempt - proviso (a)
- Reg 6(2) issues: >20% (with PAC) holders capped at selling 50% of their holding; <20% holders capped at 10% of pre-issue capital; >20% holders lose AIF lock-in relaxation - Reg 8A
- Anchor: 90 days on 50% + 30 days on 50%, from allotment - Sch XIII (10)(j) (w.e.f. 01-Apr-2022)
- Pledged/encumbered pre-issue shares: depositories mark non-transferable for lock-in period - Reg 17(2) (w.e.f. 21-Mar-2026)
- OFS eligibility: 1-year holding before DRHP (CCS tacking; exceptions: govt/infra SPV, NCLT-scheme shares, qualifying bonus) - Reg 8

ALLOCATION AND ANCHOR
- Reg 6(1) route: RII >= 35%, NII >= 15%, QIB <= 50% (5% of QIB for MFs) - Reg 32(1)
- Reg 6(2) route: QIB >= 75%, NII <= 15%, RII <= 10% - Reg 32(2)
- Anchor portion <= 60% of QIB portion - Reg 32(3), Sch XIII (10)(b)
- Anchor minimum application Rs 10 crore (mainboard) - Reg 2(1)(c)
- Anchor counts (from 01-Dec-2025): up to Rs 250 crore - min 2/max 15, min allotment Rs 5 crore; above Rs 250 crore - min 5, max 15 + 15 per additional Rs 250 crore - Sch XIII (10)(c)(I)
- Anchor reservation (from 01-Dec-2025): 40% of anchor portion = 33.33% domestic MFs + 6.67% life insurers/pension funds - Sch XIII (10)(d)
- Anchor bidding 1 day before open; allocation same day; disclosed on exchange sites before opening - Sch XIII (10)(e),(g),(i)
- NII split: 1/3 for Rs 2-10 lakh applications, 2/3 for > Rs 10 lakh - Reg 32(3A) (w.e.f. 01-Apr-2022)
- NII allotment: minimum application size by draw of lots, then proportionate - Reg 49(4A) + Sch XIV Part B

BASIS FOR ISSUE PRICE
- KPI lookback: all KPIs shared with any investor in the 3 YEARS pre-DRHP/RHP - Sch VI (9)(K)(3)(h)(i)
- KPI certification: statutory auditor / peer-reviewed CA / peer-reviewed CMA; audit-committee approval + confirmation - (3)(b),(c),(h)(ii)
- KPI continuation post-listing: annually, until later of 1 year post listing or full proceeds utilisation - (3)(i)
- KPI Industry Standards (ISF/ASSOCHAM-CII-FICCI) mandatory - SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2025/28 dated 28-Feb-2025 (applicability 01-Apr-2025 per secondary summaries)
- WACA windows: 18 months (primary >= 5% fully diluted, rolling 30 days), 18 months (insider secondary >= 5%), fallback last 5 transactions within 3 years - (4)(a),(b),Note 1
- Floor/cap as multiple of primary and secondary WACA, also in price-band ad "Risks to Investors" with independent-directors committee recommendation - (4)(c),(f)
- Per-promoter/seller WACA incl. 1-year and 3-year all-shares windows: cover page + abridged prospectus item 9 (from 21-Mar-2026; pre-21-Mar-2026 in the offer document summary)

DISCLOSURE THRESHOLDS
- Litigation floor: lower of 2% turnover / 2% net worth / 5% of 3-year average absolute PAT, vs board policy - Sch VI (12)(A)(1)(v) (w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025)
- Material acquisition financials: target = 20% of turnover/net worth/PBT - Sch VI (11)(I)(B)(ii)
- Material subsidiary (website financials): 10% of turnover/net worth/PBT; unaudited in CFS <= 20% - Sch VI (11)(I)(A)(ii)
- Group companies: names + registered offices of all; 3-year financials of TOP 5 on their own websites; loss-making/negative-net-worth disclosure OMITTED since 13-Aug-2021 - Sch VI (13)
- BRLM price information: 30/90/180-day price deltas vs benchmark, 3 FYs, max 10 IPOs per banker - Sch VI (14)(R) format
- Risk factors: descending order of materiality - Sch VI (5)(F)

ADJACENT
- SCRR Rule 19(2)(b) MPO slabs - MoF gazette G.S.R. 184(E) dated 13-Mar-2026 (PRIMARY, gazette PDF verified): <= Rs 1,600 crore post-issue capital: 25%; Rs 1,600-4,000 crore: Rs 400 crore of shares; Rs 4,000-50,000 crore: 10% (MPS 25% in 3 years for these two bands); Rs 50,000-1,00,000 crore: Rs 1,000 crore + 8% (MPS 25% in 5 years); Rs 1,00,000-5,00,000 crore: Rs 6,250 crore + 2.75%; above Rs 5,00,000 crore: Rs 15,000 crore + 1%, subject to a 2.5% minimum offer floor; MPS for the top two bands: 15% in 5 years and 25% in 10 years if <15% at listing, else 25% in 5 years
- SEBI flipping study (press release 02-Sep-2024): 54% of allotted shares (ex-anchor) sold within 1 week; NII 63.3%, retail 42.7%; 70% within a year (individuals); 67.6% sold in a week when gains > 20% vs 23.3% when negative; 144 IPOs Apr-2021 to Dec-2023

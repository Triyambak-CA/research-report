# F. SME DRHP vs MAINBOARD DRHP - THE DISCLOSURE GAP

## F.1 The rule position (verified against ICDR consolidated text amended up to 21-Mar-2026)
KEY FINDING: since the ICDR (Amendment) Regulations 2022 consolidated the schedules, Chapter IX issues use THE SAME Schedule VI Part A as mainboard IPOs (Reg 245(2)(b); Schedule VI Part A applicability note cross-refers reg 246(2)(b); a proviso inserted for SME merely substitutes the words "retail individual investors" with "individual investors who applies for minimum application size"). The disclosure gap is therefore mostly a PRACTICE gap, not a rulebook gap - except where noted.

Item-by-item, de jure:
1. RESTATED FINANCIALS: 3 years restated consolidated Ind AS financials + stub (mandatory if latest FY accounts older than 6 months at filing; stub not older than 6 months) certified by peer-reviewed statutory auditor/CA - Schedule VI Part A (9)(A). Same as mainboard. Reg 245(5): financials in offer document not more than 6 months old at issue opening.
   - CAVEAT: SME issuers report under AS (Companies (AS) Rules 2021), not Ind AS, if below Ind AS applicability thresholds; in practice SME DRHP restated financials are commonly AS-based - confirm per issuer (to be grounded in the sample DRHPs, F.2).
2. BASIS FOR ISSUE PRICE + KPI REGIME: APPLIES to SME. Schedule VI Part A para 9(K)(3) (inserted by ICDR Fourth Amendment Regulations 2022, w.e.f. 21-Nov-2022, for RHPs filed on/after that date): KPIs must be approved by the AUDIT COMMITTEE, certified by peer-reviewed CA/cost accountant, certificate is a material document, KPIs disclosed to prior investors in preceding 3 years must be disclosed, peer comparison of KPIs with Indian/global listed peers "wherever available", and KPIs must continue to be disclosed at least annually until the later of 1 year post listing or full utilisation of proceeds.
3. WACA DISCLOSURE: APPLIES to SME. Schedule VI Part A para 9(K)(4): price per share of primary issuances >= 5% of capital in preceding 18 months, secondary transactions by promoters/selling shareholders/board-nomination holders >= 5% in 18 months (else last 5 transactions within 3 years), WACA vs floor/cap price multiple table, to be repeated in the price-band advertisement under "Risks to Investors" with a recommendation of the COMMITTEE OF INDEPENDENT DIRECTORS that the price band is justified.
4. COMMISSIONED INDUSTRY REPORT: NO regulation mandates a commissioned industry report for SME or mainboard; Schedule VI Part A (10)(A) requires an Industry Overview with sourced claims. In mainboard practice a paid CRISIL/F&S/D&B report is near-universal; in SME practice the industry chapter is often assembled by the LM from public sources or a cheap commissioned report - ground in sample DRHPs (F.2).
5. PEER COMPARISON: required within Basis for Issue Price (accounting ratios + industry P/E in the illustrative format; KPI peer comparison "wherever available" per 9(K)(3)(h)(v)). SME DRHPs routinely claim "no listed peers" or pick 1-2 loosely comparable names - verify in sample.
6. LITIGATION MATERIALITY: same thresholds as mainboard AFTER 08-Mar-2025 - Schedule VI Part A (14)(A)(1)(v) substituted by ICDR (Amendment) Regulations 2025: "other litigation" disclosed at the LOWER of (i) board materiality policy, or (ii) value exceeding the lowest of 2% of turnover, 2% of net worth, or 5% of average absolute PAT of last 3 years. Before 08-Mar-2025 SME issuers could rely purely on their own board-defined materiality policy. Because SME turnover/net-worth bases are tiny, the numeric thresholds bite at very small amounts - litigation chapters should be MORE complete post-2025, and an analyst should recompute the threshold and test the chapter against it.
7. ANCHOR INVESTORS: PERMITTED on SME. Definition 2(1)(c): minimum application Rs 2 crore (vs Rs 10 crore mainboard). Schedule XIII (10): anchor portion up to 60% of QIB portion; SME tiers - max 2 anchors for allocation up to Rs 2 crore; 2-15 anchors for above Rs 2 crore up to Rs 25 crore (min allotment Rs 1 crore each); above Rs 25 crore, 5-15 anchors plus 10 more per additional Rs 25 crore. Anchor lock-in: 50% for 90 days, 50% for 30 days from allotment (Schedule XIII 10(j), w.e.f. 01-Apr-2022). From 01-Dec-2025 (Third Amendment 2025): 40% of anchor portion reserved - 33.33% domestic MFs + 6.67% life insurers/pension funds. Prevalence/size in practice: see F.2.
8. LM TRACK RECORD: APPLIES - Schedule VI Part A (R): price information of past issues handled by the lead manager(s) in prescribed tabular format (30/60/90-day price vs issue price), plus (Q) objects-achievement of past issues of group/subsidiary companies. So the LM's own scorecard is INSIDE every SME DRHP - a free data table for the red-flag check.
9. NEW SME-ONLY DISCLOSURES (w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025, Reg 245(2)(c)-(e)): EPF/ESIC registration and payment details (employee-count triangulation!), LM site-visit report as material document for inspection, and LM fees disclosure.

## F.2 What actual SME DRHPs contain - grounding (three documents read, all filed AFTER 08-Mar-2025)
Documents read (PDFs saved in this folder):
1. SHANTI INORGANICS LIMITED - DRHP dated 26-Sep-2025, NSE Emerge, 100% book built. BRLM: Vivro Financial Services Pvt Ltd; Registrar: KFin Technologies. (drhp-shanti.pdf, from nsearchives.nseindia.com)
2. KUMAR COTTON MILLS LTD - DRHP dated 30-Jun-2026, BSE SME, 100% book built, 100% fresh issue (OFS: Nil). Merchant banker: Seren Capital Pvt Ltd. (drhp-kumar.pdf, from serencapital.in)
3. GABION TECHNOLOGIES INDIA LIMITED - DRHP dated 31-Mar-2025, NSE Emerge, 100% book built. (drhp3.pdf, from nsearchives.nseindia.com)

Findings across the three:
- RESTATED FINANCIALS: all three restate under INDIAN GAAP (AS), not Ind AS ("prepared in accordance with Indian GAAP and the Companies Act and restated in accordance with the SEBI (ICDR) Regulations" - Kumar Cotton; Shanti states restated info "prepared in accordance with Indian Generally Accepted Accounting Principles"). Confirms the caveat in F.1 item 1: Ind AS is NOT the SME norm; comparability with Ind AS mainboard peers is impaired (no expected-credit-loss provisioning, different lease/financial-instrument treatment).
- KPI + WACA: present in all three. Shanti's KPIs "approved by the Audit Committee" at a stated meeting; WACA tables with floor/cap multiples present (Shanti WACA Rs 90.00 vs price band [.]). The KPI/WACA regime does flow through to SME documents in practice.
- INDUSTRY REPORT: Shanti commissioned a CareEdge Research report (CARE Analytics and Advisory Pvt Ltd). Kumar Cotton and Gabion used NO commissioned report - industry chapters assembled from "publicly available industry reports, government publications, databases" with an explicit risk factor about reliance on public sources. So commissioned reports are OPTIONAL and split roughly by issuer size/LM quality.
- PEERS: Kumar Cotton tabulates 2 listed peers (Borana Weaves, Vinny Overseas) with EPS/PE/RoNW/BV sourced from their filings. Shanti states "There are no listed companies whose product portfolio... are similar" - the no-peer claim appears INSIDE Basis for Issue Price. Both patterns are common; the no-peer claim must be independently tested.
- ANCHOR INVESTORS: all three book-built DRHPs carry the full anchor machinery (up to 60% of QIB portion, 1/3 to MFs pre-Dec-2025 wording). Note: in book-built SME issues anchors are routine ON PAPER; actual anchor books are small (Rs 2 cr minimum per anchor). Fixed-price SME issues have NO anchor category at all.
- LM PAST-ISSUE PRICE TABLE: present in all three ("Price information of past issues handled by the BRLM") - free 30/90/180-day scorecard of the LM inside the document.
- EPF/ESIC: the new Reg 245(2)(c) disclosure appears in the 2025-26 filings (12-36 hits per document) - registration numbers, employee counts, amounts paid. Use for headcount triangulation vs claimed scale.
- LITIGATION: board-approved materiality policy quoted; post 08-Mar-2025 thresholds (2%/2%/5% test) referenced.
- CERTIFICATIONS: most quantitative claims (capacity, utilisation, machinery) certified by the STATUTORY AUDITOR (e.g. Kumar Cotton: "As certified by M/s Bhupendra J Shah & Associates... statutory auditor") - a single small firm certifying its own client's operating claims. Verify the auditor's other listed clients and peer-review status.

## F.3 The real gap vs mainboard (summary)
- No SEBI observation letters; exchange vetting only (Reg 246(2)).
- Same disclosure schedule on paper; enforcement of quality is by the exchange and a lead manager who is often small, conflicted (15% own-account underwriting, market-making arrangement) and has done dozens of similar filings.
- No 8-quarter history: SME DRHP gives 3 annual restated statements + possibly one stub; no quarterly series exists pre-IPO and only half-yearly after listing.
- No concalls, no analyst coverage, no institutional cross-holding record for triangulation.

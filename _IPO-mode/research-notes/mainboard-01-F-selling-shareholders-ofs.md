# F. Selling shareholders and OFS

Source: SEBI (ICDR) Regulations 2018, consolidated as amended to 21-Mar-2026 (local copy icdr-2026-03-21.pdf), read 01-Sep-2026.

## F.1 Eligibility of shares for OFS - Reg 8
- Only FULLY PAID-UP equity shares HELD FOR AT LEAST ONE YEAR prior to filing of the DRAFT offer document may be offered for sale (Reg 8).
- Tacking: for shares from conversion/exchange of fully paid-up compulsorily convertible securities (incl. depository receipts), the holding period of the convertible PLUS the resulting equity counts; conversion must be completed before RHP/prospectus filing with full disclosure of terms.
- Exceptions to the one-year rule (provisos to Reg 8):
  (a) OFS by a government company/statutory authority/corporation or their SPV in the infrastructure sector;
  (b) shares (or shares from conversion of fully paid CCS) acquired under an NCLT/High Court/Central Government scheme under ss.230-234 Companies Act 2013, in lieu of business and invested capital existing for more than one year before scheme approval (clause substituted w.e.f. 09-Sep-2025 to cover CCS-derived shares);
  (c) bonus shares on securities held at least one year, provided the bonus was issued out of free reserves and share premium existing at the end of the preceding FY, and NOT out of revaluation reserves or unrealised profits.

## F.2 OFS caps in a Reg 6(2) (loss-maker route) issue - Reg 8A (inserted 14-Jan-2022)
- Shareholders holding (with PACs) MORE THAN 20% of pre-issue capital (fully diluted): may sell at most 50 PER CENT of their pre-issue holding.
- Shareholders holding LESS THAN 20%: may sell at most 10 PER CENT OF PRE-ISSUE SHARE CAPITAL OF THE ISSUER (fully diluted).
- >20% holders lose the Reg 17(c) VCF/AIF lock-in relaxation (Reg 8A(c)).
- Explanation (added subsequently): limits computed on shareholding as at DRHP filing and apply CUMULATIVELY to the OFS plus any pre-issue secondary sales.
- There is NO equivalent hard percentage cap on promoter selldown in a Reg 6(1) mainboard issue in the current text - the constraints are the one-year holding rule, the 20% minimum promoter contribution that must remain locked 18 months (Reg 14/16), and the Reg 8A caps only in 6(2) issues. (Minimum public offer/dilution percentages come from SCRR Rule 19(2)(b), outside ICDR.)

## F.3 What the offer document must disclose about each selling shareholder
- Front outside cover page: details of selling shareholders IN TABULAR FORMAT with their AVERAGE COST OF ACQUISITION and OFS details (Sch VI Part A (1)(a)(1), substituted 14-Jan-2022).
- Price band advertisement (Sch X): name of selling shareholder, type (promoter selling shareholder, investor etc.), number of shares offered, AGGREGATE PROCEEDS from offered shares, weighted average cost of acquisition per share.
- For DRHPs filed BEFORE 21-Mar-2026: "Summary of offer document" items (M) weighted average acquisition price of each promoter/selling shareholder in the last one year and (N) their average cost of acquisition. This summary section was OMITTED w.e.f. 21-Mar-2026 (ICDR Amendment Regulations 2026, footnote 655).
- For filings from 21-Mar-2026: the new draft abridged prospectus / abridged prospectus (Sch VI Part E + Annexure I, substituted 21-Mar-2026) item 9 - per-promoter and per-selling-shareholder WACA table plus 1-year and 3-year all-shares-transacted WACA; item 5 - pre and post offer shareholding of each promoter, promoter-group member and top-10 public shareholders at floor and cap price.
- Selling shareholders take responsibility only for statements specifically pertaining to themselves and their offered shares (cover-page "Issuer's Absolute Responsibility" clause, Sch VI Part A (1)(a)(2)(k)).
- Capital structure chapter gives the full acquisition history (date, price, nature of consideration) from which per-seller cost can be recomputed.

## F.4 Analyst checks
- Build a seller table: name, category, shares offered, % of their holding being sold, average cost, implied realised multiple at cap price, remaining stake and its unlock date. A promoter selling a large slice at 30-50x cost while the fresh issue is small is a monetisation event, not a capital raise - frame the report accordingly.
- Check for pre-DRHP secondary sales in the capital-structure chapter (last 18 months): insiders who sold BELOW the IPO band recently undermine the band; insiders who bought recently support it.
- Confirm whether any selling shareholder has board nomination rights or veto rights (these trigger the WACA secondary-transaction disclosure and the anchor-exclusion test).

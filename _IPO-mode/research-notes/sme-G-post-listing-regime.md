# G. POST-LISTING DISCLOSURE REGIME - WHY SME COMPANIES GO DARK

Source: SEBI (LODR) Regulations 2015, consolidated text "Amended up to January 22, 2026" (downloaded from sebi.gov.in, saved as lodr-2026-01-22.pdf). All cites verified against this text.

## G.1 Half-yearly, not quarterly - VERIFIED
- LODR Reg 33(5): for SME-exchange-listed entities, every reference to "quarterly/quarter" in Reg 33 is read as "half yearly/half year"; the year-to-date results requirement does NOT apply.
- LODR Reg 32(8): same quarterly->half-yearly reading for statements of deviation/variation in use of issue proceeds.
- LODR Reg 31(1) proviso: shareholding pattern HALF-YEARLY, within 21 days of each half-year end (vs quarterly for mainboard).
- LODR Reg 47(4) proviso: newspaper publication of results NOT applicable to SME-listed entities.
- Tension with ICDR: ICDR Reg 262(5)-(6) (w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025) requires auditor utilisation certificates "while filing the quarterly financial results". Since SME entities file half-yearly under LODR 33(5), in practice these certificates ride on the half-yearly filings; the ICDR wording appears not to have been conformed. Flag: interpret as "with each periodic results filing" - UNVERIFIED whether SEBI has clarified this by circular.

## G.2 Corporate governance provisions disapplied - VERIFIED
- LODR Reg 15(2)(b): Regulations 17 to 27, clauses (b) to (i) and (t) of Reg 46(2) (website disclosures), and paras C, D, E of Schedule V (annual-report CG disclosures) DO NOT APPLY to entities listed on SME Exchange. That removes, inter alia: Reg 17 board composition (incl. independent directors, woman director), Reg 18 audit committee, Reg 19 NRC, Reg 20 stakeholders relationship committee, Reg 21 risk management committee, Reg 24 subsidiary governance, Reg 24A secretarial audit report, Reg 25 independent director obligations, Reg 27 CG compliance report.
- EXCEPTION carved back in (proviso inserted w.e.f. 01-Apr-2025, following SEBI Board PR 37/2024 item 2.9): Reg 23 (RPT) APPLIES to an SME-listed entity with paid-up equity capital > Rs 10 crore OR net worth > Rs 25 crore as on the last day of the previous FY; 6 months to comply once triggered; remains applicable until capital AND net worth stay below threshold for 3 consecutive FYs.
- LODR Reg 23(1) proviso (w.e.f. 01-Apr-2025): SME RPT materiality = Rs 50 crore OR 10% of annual consolidated turnover, WHICHEVER IS LOWER (mainboard uses Schedule XII thresholds).
- Companies Act 2013 baseline still applies (board, s.177/178 committees where triggered, s.188 RPT, statutory audit) - but the LODR overlay that analysts rely on (independent-director-majority audit committee approving RPTs, CG report) is absent for most SME issuers below the Reg 23 threshold.
- BRSR: voluntary only for SME (Reg 34(2)(f) third proviso).
- NOTE (contrast with 15(2)(a)): mainboard small companies are exempt only if paid-up capital <= Rs 10 crore AND net worth <= Rs 25 crore; the SME exemption under 15(2)(b) has no size test at all (except the Reg 23 carve-in).

## G.3 Earnings calls in practice
- No LODR obligation to hold earnings calls for any issuer; mainboard Reg 30/Schedule III requires disclosure of call schedules/transcripts IF held. SME issuers overwhelmingly do NOT hold earnings calls; most have no investor-relations function. UNVERIFIED as a quantified statistic - no official study found counting SME concalls; treat as a well-supported practice observation (cross-checked: prior deep-dive work on SOUTHWEST, an NSE-Emerge-migrated issuer, found only 3 concalls ever).

## G.4 Consequences for the existing skill's sections
- Section 8 (guidance scorecard from 8 quarters of concalls): CANNOT RUN as designed. No concalls, no quarterly cadence. At IPO: replace with "promise ledger" built from the offer document (objects, capacity, timelines) to be tracked against half-yearly results + monitoring-agency/auditor utilisation certificates. Post-listing: at most 2 data points per year.
- Section 13 (shareholding/positioning/flows): DEGRADED. Shareholding pattern only half-yearly (Reg 31 proviso); most SME counters have no FII/DII of note, no bulk/block-deal depth, no F&O, no delivery-quality data on NSE Emerge equivalent to mainboard. What survives: half-yearly pattern deltas, promoter pledge disclosures (Reg 31(2)/SAST), post-lock-in expiry pattern changes, market-maker activity.
- Section 19 (data-aggregator operating-series cross-check): DEGRADED to half-yearly frequency, and screener-type aggregators often carry SME data late or not at all. GST/EPFO-type external series become MORE important than exchange data (EPF/ESIC counts are now in the DRHP itself per ICDR Reg 245(2)(c)).
- Section 5 (8 quarters + 5 years): 8 quarters DO NOT EXIST. Best case: 3 years restated + stub from the offer document + half-yearly results after listing. 5-year view requires unrestated RoC/MCA filings (AOC-4) which are NOT restated and may not reconcile - a check in itself.

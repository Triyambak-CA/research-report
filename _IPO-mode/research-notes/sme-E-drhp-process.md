# E. DRHP VETTING AND PUBLIC-COMMENT PROCESS (SME)

Source: SEBI ICDR 2018 consolidated text amended up to 21-Mar-2026, Regs 246-247.

## E.1 Exchange vetting, not SEBI - VERIFIED
- Reg 246(1): issuer files the offer document with SEBI (through lead managers) only AFTER filing with RoC, i.e. for record.
- Reg 246(2): "The Board shall not issue any observation on the offer document." SEBI does not vet SME offer documents.
- Reg 246(3) (substituted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): lead manager submits due-diligence certificate (Form A, Schedule V) WITH THE SITE VISIT REPORT ANNEXED, plus Form G confirmations, along with the DRAFT offer document TO THE SME EXCHANGE(S) where listing is proposed. In-principle approval is issued by the SME exchange (NSE Emerge / BSE SME) under its own review process (Schedule XIX read with Reg 230(1)(a)); the exchange is the vetting authority.
- 21-Mar-2026 amendment (SEBI (ICDR) (Amendment) Regulations 2026, w.e.f. 21-Mar-2026): draft abridged prospectus (Part E of Schedule VI) also filed and hosted.

## E.2 21-day public comment period - VERIFIED
- Reg 247(1) (inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): DRHP filed with the SME exchange "shall be made public for comments, if any, for a period of at least twenty one days from the date of filing", hosted on the websites of the ISSUER, the SME EXCHANGE and the LEAD MANAGER.
- Reg 247(2): within 2 working days of filing, public announcement in one English, one Hindi and one regional-language newspaper inviting public comments to the exchange, issuer or lead manager.
- Reg 247(3): after the 21 days, lead manager files with the exchange the comments received and consequential changes.
- Before 08-Mar-2025 there was NO public-comment window for SME DRHPs at all.

## E.3 Why this is an analytical opportunity
- The 21-day window is the only point where an outside analyst can influence or stress-test disclosure: comments go on record with the exchange and the LM must report them and consequential changes (Reg 247(3)).
- For a report writer: (a) the DRHP is guaranteed to be on three websites simultaneously - pull it early; (b) compare DRHP vs final prospectus for changes made after comments - the diff reveals what the exchange or commenters forced out; (c) Reg 274 gives a live feed of promoter/promoter-group dealings and pre-IPO placements between DRHP and issue close (24-hour reporting to the exchange).
- Caveat: exchange vetting is lighter than SEBI vetting in practice; no SEBI observations exist to read (unlike mainboard where SEBI observation letters and issuer replies improve successive filings).

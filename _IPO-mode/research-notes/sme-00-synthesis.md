## 2. MAINBOARD vs SME - MATERIAL DIFFERENCES TABLE
(SME side verified by me against primary sources; mainboard side stated only where needed for contrast - parallel agent to confirm mainboard figures.)

| Dimension | Mainboard | SME (verified) | Why it changes the analysis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who vets the DRHP | SEBI observations | Exchange only; "The Board shall not issue any observation" (ICDR 246(2)) | No SEBI letter to read; diligence quality = exchange + LM quality; read DRHP-vs-prospectus diff instead |
| Public comment | On SEBI site | 21 days on issuer + exchange + LM websites (ICDR 247(1), w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025) | Analyst can file comments; LM must report them and changes (247(3)) |
| Profitability gate | None (QIB route exists) | Rs 1 cr operating profit (EBIDT) from operations in 2 of 3 prior FYs (ICDR 229(6)) + exchange tests (NSE: positive net worth + positive FCFE 2/3 yrs; BSE: NW Rs 1 cr x2yrs, NTA Rs 3 cr, leverage <=3:1, EBIDT positive in latest yr) | Creates bright-line earnings-management incentives exactly at the thresholds |
| Size band | No cap | Post-issue paid-up capital <= Rs 25 cr (Chapter IX ceiling; <= Rs 10 cr as-of-right, 229(1)-(2)) | Tiny equity bases; EPS optics; bonus-issue games |
| OFS | Uncapped | <= 20% of issue size; each seller <= 50% of holding (230(1)(f)-(g)) | Promoter exit shifts to post-lock-in window; build supply calendar |
| GCP | <= 25% (no Rs cap) | <= 15% or Rs 10 cr, whichever LOWER (230(2)) | Less slush; watch for objects gamed to sit at caps |
| Promoter-loan repayment from proceeds | Permitted with disclosure | PROHIBITED directly or indirectly (230(1)(h)) | Must test indirect routes (see K-5) |
| Monitoring agency | Issue > Rs 100 cr | Issue (ex-OFS) > Rs 50 cr (262(1)); below that, statutory-auditor certificates with each results filing (262(5)-(6)) | Different verification artefacts to collect post-listing |
| Promoter lock-in | MPC 18 months (typ.) | MPC 3 YEARS; excess: 50% 1yr + 50% 2yrs (238) | Longer runway but a defined supply cliff at yr 1/2/3 |
| Anchor | Rs 10 cr min ticket | Rs 2 cr min ticket; same 90/30-day lock-in; only in BOOK-BUILT issues (2(1)(c), Sch XIII(10)) | Anchor names are weaker signals; fixed-price issues have none |
| Min allottees / application | 1,000 allottees; app ~Rs 15k | 200 allottees (268(1)); min application 2 lots and > Rs 2 LAKH (267(2)) | Retail excluded by design; 200-name books are concentratable |
| Underwriting | Not mandatory | 100% underwritten; LM min 15% own account (260) | LM is conflicted principal, not agent; LM economics disclosure (245(2)(e)) matters |
| Market making | None | Compulsory 3 years; 5% inventory (261) | Quote != depth; cliff when it ends at yr 3 |
| Results cadence | Quarterly | HALF-YEARLY; no YTD; no newspaper publication (LODR 33(5), 47(4)) | Half the data points; stale prices vs fundamentals |
| Shareholding pattern | Quarterly | Half-yearly (LODR 31(1) proviso) | Flow analysis degraded |
| CG regime | Regs 17-27 apply | DISAPPLIED (LODR 15(2)(b)) except Reg 23 RPT above Rs 10 cr capital / Rs 25 cr NW; SME RPT materiality = lower of Rs 50 cr or 10% turnover (w.e.f. 01-Apr-2025) | No independent-director enforcement, no CG report, no mandatory RMC/NRC |
| Listing-day price | No cap (mainboard) | +90% cap over issue price in special pre-open (NSE/SME/62757, 04-Jul-2024; BSE equivalent) | Listing pops truncated; day-2 behaviour more informative |
| Accounting basis | Ind AS restated | Usually INDIAN GAAP (AS) restated (all 3 sample DRHPs) | Not comparable line-by-line with Ind AS peers |
| Concalls/coverage | Standard | Essentially none | Section 8 impossible as designed |
| Migration | n/a | A defined, hard-gated upside trigger (Reg 277 + NSE/BSE 2025 norms) | New checkable catalyst |

## 3. SME-ONLY SECTIONS / CHECKS TO ADD

1. ELIGIBILITY-GAMING AUDIT. Recompute every eligibility metric from the restated financials: EBITDA (ex-other-income) per year vs the Rs 1 cr / 2-of-3 test; NSE FCFE per the 22-Aug-2024 formula AND ex-capital-issuance (the 20-Apr-2026 formula counts share issues as cash flow); BSE net worth/NTA/leverage. Flag any metric that clears its threshold by <25% or clears only in the qualifying years. State which exchange the issuer chose and whether it would fail the other's test.
2. USE-OF-PROCEEDS FORENSICS. Table every object vs the caps (GCP min(15%, Rs 10 cr); unidentified acquisitions <= 25%; combined <= 35%). Trace promoter/related-party loans in the borrowings note and stub-period RPTs against Reg 230(1)(h). Verify capex vendors and quotation-givers on MCA (existence, financials, incorporation date). Name the monitoring artefact that will exist post-listing (CRA agency if ex-OFS size > Rs 50 cr, else auditor certificates) and diarise it.
3. POST-LISTING SUPPLY CALENDAR. Build the D-file template: anchor 30/90 days (book-built only); non-promoter pre-IPO capital + 50% promoter excess at 1 year; remaining promoter excess at 2 years; MPC and market-making expiry at 3 years. Overlay pre-IPO placement allottees with their WACA vs issue price. State each tranche as % of post-issue capital and vs average daily turnover.
4. LEAD MANAGER DOSSIER. From inside the DRHP: past-issue price table (Sch VI Part A (R)) and LM fees (245(2)(e)). Outside: NSE merchant-banker application list (returned/withdrawn filings), SEBI enforcement search (FOCL/Inventure precedents), count of concurrent SME mandates. The LM underwrites 15% and arranges market making - treat the LM as a principal whose incentives must be analysed, and say so in the report.
5. LIQUIDITY AND EXIT-ABILITY NOTE. Trailing 6-month ADV, traded-days ratio, position exitable at 10% of ADV; surveillance status (ESM/GSM/ASM/T2T) and history; lot-size (can't sell < 1 lot except to market maker); what happens to the position if the counter enters ESM (2% T2T band) or GSM stage III (weekly trading). Benchmark against BSE migration liquidity floors (Rs 10 lakh ADV, 80% traded days) as "minimum healthy".
6. MIGRATION SCORECARD. Compute distance to every NSE/BSE 2025 migration gate (I-file checklist). Classify: realistic within 3 years / plausible / never. If "never", the terminal state is a half-yearly-reporting illiquid counter - value it that way.
7. DRHP COMMENT-WINDOW DIFF. If covering at IPO: pull DRHP on day 1 of the 21-day window; diff DRHP vs RHP/prospectus at filing with RoC; every change is signal (exchange pushback or public comments). Reg 274 feed: promoter dealings and pre-IPO placements reported within 24 hours during the issue window.
8. GOVERNANCE-CLIFF DISCLOSURE. One paragraph in every SME report stating plainly: after listing this company must publish only half-yearly results and a half-yearly shareholding pattern; no audit-committee-of-IDs RPT approval unless above the Reg 23 threshold; no CG report; earnings calls voluntary and unlikely. Conviction must be discounted for a 6-month information vacuum.

## 4. SME-SPECIFIC KNOWN TRAPS

- Assuming quarterly data exists. It does not (LODR 33(5)). Any "8-quarter" framing fails.
- Assuming SEBI vetted the DRHP. It did not (ICDR 246(2)). Exchange in-principle approval is a weaker filter.
- Reading "oversubscribed 200x" as demand quality. Applicant inflation hit 245X average in FY24 (SEBI CP 19-Nov-2024); it is a coordination artefact.
- Treating the 90% listing-day cap as price discovery. The cap (NSE/SME/62757) truncates the open; a cap-hit listing says demand exceeded 1.9x price, nothing more.
- Comparing AS-restated SME financials line-by-line with Ind AS mainboard peers (leases, financial instruments, ECL all differ).
- Trusting "no listed peers" in Basis for Issue Price (Shanti pattern) - it is a pricing dodge, not a fact.
- Missing that the KPI/WACA regime DOES apply to SME (Sch VI Part A 9(K)(3)-(4)) - if absent or perfunctory in a DRHP, that is a compliance red flag, not a regime difference.
- Forgetting fixed-price issues: no anchors, no QIB/NII split, allocation per Reg 253(3) (min 50% to minimum-application individuals). Check issue type before applying book-building mechanics.
- Using pre-08-Mar-2025 rules (Rs 1 lakh application, 50 allottees, 25% GCP, 1-year excess lock-in, 100 cr monitoring threshold) - all stale.
- Treating the NSE FCFE screen as a quality signal after 20-Apr-2026: capital raises now count INTO FCFE (NSE/SME/73818).
- Assuming promoter selldown shows up in the IPO. It cannot (20%/50% caps); it shows up at lock-in expiries and via pre-IPO placements.
- Assuming the market maker provides exit depth. Obligation is a 3-year two-way quote with 5% inventory, not liquidity; and it lapses at year 3.
- Expecting a monitoring agency. Most SME issues are < Rs 50 cr ex-OFS; you get auditor certificates instead - different document, different reliability.

## 5. VERIFIED HARD NUMBERS (copy-ready)

ICDR Chapter IX (consolidated text amended up to 21-Mar-2026; 2025 changes per SEBI (ICDR) (Amendment) Regulations 2025 w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025, approved at SEBI Board 18-Dec-2024, PR 37/2024):
- Operating profit (EBIDT) from operations >= Rs 1 crore in >= 2 of 3 previous FYs - Reg 229(6).
- Post-issue paid-up capital: <= Rs 10 cr as of right; > Rs 10 cr to <= Rs 25 cr permitted under Chapter IX - Reg 229(1)-(2).
- Converted proprietorship/partnership/LLP: >= 1 full FY as a company before DRHP - Reg 229(4).
- Promoter change > 50%: 1-year wait before DRHP - Reg 229(5).
- OFS <= 20% of total issue size - Reg 230(1)(f); per-seller <= 50% of pre-issue holding (fully diluted) - Reg 230(1)(g).
- No repayment of promoter/promoter-group/related-party loans from proceeds, directly or indirectly - Reg 230(1)(h).
- GCP <= 15% of amount raised or Rs 10 crore, whichever less - Reg 230(2).
- GCP + unidentified acquisitions <= 35%; unidentified acquisitions alone <= 25% - Reg 230(3) (w.e.f. 14-Jan-2022).
- Firm financing arrangements for 75% of stated means of finance (ex-issue/internal accruals) - Reg 230(1)(e).
- Minimum promoters' contribution 20% of post-issue capital - Reg 236(1).
- Lock-in: MPC 3 years (from allotment or commercial production, later) - Reg 238(a); excess: 50% for 2 years + 50% for 1 year - Reg 238(b) (w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025); non-promoter pre-issue capital 1 year - Reg 239.
- Anchor (book-built only): min application Rs 2 crore - Reg 2(1)(c); anchor portion <= 60% of QIB portion; SME tiers: <= 2 anchors up to Rs 2 cr allocation; 2-15 for > Rs 2 cr to Rs 25 cr (min Rs 1 cr each); above Rs 25 cr: 5-15 + 10 per additional Rs 25 cr - Sch XIII (10)(c)(II); lock-in 50% 90 days + 50% 30 days - Sch XIII (10)(j); from 01-Dec-2025, 40% of anchor portion reserved (33.33% MFs, 6.67% life insurers/pension funds) - Sch XIII (10)(d) (Third Amendment 2025).
- Allocation (book-built): >= 35% individuals at min application, >= 15% NII, <= 50% QIB (5% MF) - Reg 253(1); NII split 1/3 (2 lots-Rs 10 lakh) / 2/3 (> Rs 10 lakh) - Reg 253(2). Fixed price: >= 50% to min-application individuals - Reg 253(3).
- Min application: 2 lots AND > Rs 2 lakh - Reg 267(2). Min allottees: 200 - Reg 268(1).
- Underwriting: 100%; LM >= 15% on own account - Reg 260(1)-(2).
- Market making: compulsory 3 years; >= 5% inventory at allotment - Reg 261(1),(4).
- Monitoring agency (CRA): issue ex-OFS > Rs 50 crore - Reg 262(1); else auditor certificate with each results filing till full utilisation - Reg 262(5); working-capital object > Rs 5 cr: auditor certificate in offer-document format - Reg 262(6).
- SEBI issues no observations - Reg 246(2); DRHP public comments >= 21 days on issuer/exchange/LM websites - Reg 247(1); newspaper announcement within 2 working days - Reg 247(2).
- Issue period 3-10 working days - Reg 266(1). Refund on listing failure: 4 days, 15% p.a. interest - Reg 272.
- Promoter/promoter-group dealings and pre-IPO placements reported to exchange within 24 hours (DRHP filing to issue close) - Reg 274.
- Migration to main board: paid-up > Rs 10 cr to Rs 25 cr + special resolution (non-promoter 2:1) + main-board criteria - Reg 277; mandatory migration beyond Rs 25 cr unless mainboard-LODR undertaking - Reg 280(2) + proviso.
- Litigation "other matters" threshold: lower of board policy or lowest of 2% turnover / 2% net worth / 5% avg absolute PAT (3 yrs) - Sch VI Part A (14)(A)(1)(v) (w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025).
- KPI regime (audit committee approval, peer-reviewed certification, 3-yr prior-investor KPIs, annual continuation >= 1 yr post listing) - Sch VI Part A 9(K)(3) (w.e.f. 21-Nov-2022). WACA disclosure (18-month primary/secondary, 5% thresholds, floor/cap multiples, ID-committee recommendation) - Sch VI Part A 9(K)(4).
- EPF/ESIC disclosure, LM site-visit report as material document, LM fees disclosure - Reg 245(2)(c)-(e) (w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025).
- Restated financials: 3 years + stub (stub mandatory if latest FY > 6 months old; financials < 6 months old at issue opening) - Sch VI Part A 9(A); Reg 245(5).

LODR (consolidated text amended up to 22-Jan-2026):
- Results half-yearly; no YTD - Reg 33(5). Deviation statements half-yearly - Reg 32(8). Shareholding pattern half-yearly within 21 days - Reg 31(1) proviso. No newspaper publication - Reg 47(4) proviso.

## 6. MINIMUM VIABLE REPORT - WHAT CANNOT BE FILLED

For a typical SME issuer (sub-Rs 50 cr issue, no concalls, half-yearly filings), the honest report must say "insufficient data for conviction" rather than pad, in:
- Section 8 (guidance scorecard): NO guidance history exists at IPO and no concalls after. Replaceable only by the promise ledger (Section 3 item 8 above); as designed, IMPOSSIBLE.
- Section 5 (8 quarters): quarterly series does not exist and never will while SME-listed. Best honest output: 3 restated years + stub + half-yearly series post-listing.
- Section 13 (flows): no institutional flow data, half-yearly pattern only. Honest output: lock-in supply calendar + pledge + pattern deltas; anything more is fabrication.
- Section 19 (aggregator cross-check): aggregator coverage unreliable; degrade to official external series or state the gap.
- Section 18 (order book): usually unverifiable; state the certification chain (who certified, their independence) and stop there.
- Section 3 (peers): where genuinely no listed peer exists, present the analyst-built unlisted-peer table (MCA data) with its limitations, or say so.
A conforming SME report therefore has a mandatory "DATA LIMITS" box up front: results frequency, last filing date, ADV, surveillance status, and the list of sections running on degraded inputs. The scepticism prior is justified quantitatively by J: SEBI's own paper attributes the 2025 tightening to diversion and circular-revenue cases, and four named 2024-25 orders involved 40-71% of IPO proceeds being diverted.

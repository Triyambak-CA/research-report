# K. RED FLAGS SPECIFIC TO SME ISSUERS - CHECKLIST WITH DOCUMENT LOCATIONS

For each flag: WHERE to find the evidence in the offer document (chapter names per Schedule VI Part A structure, which SME DRHPs follow) and outside it.

1. CIRCULAR / RELATED-PARTY REVENUE
- Where: Restated Financial Information - Related Party Transactions annexure; Risk Factors; MCA filings of the counterparties (director/address overlap); GST turnover vs reported revenue if obtainable. Compare top-customer names (Business chapter) against promoter group entities list (Our Promoter Group / Group Companies chapter).
- Precedent: Debock (23-Aug-2024 interim), Add-Shop (May-2024). SEBI CP 19-Nov-2024 para 21-22: 1 in 2 top-50 SME entities had RPTs > 10% of turnover.
2. SINGLE DOMINANT CUSTOMER / SUPPLIER CONCENTRATION
- Where: Risk Factors (usually top-3/5/10 customer concentration table - SME DRHPs often anonymise customers as "Customer A"); Business chapter. If customers unnamed, treat as elevated risk; triangulate via EPF/ESIC counts (Reg 245(2)(c) data) and site-visit report reference.
3. RECEIVABLE AND INVENTORY BUILD BEFORE FILING
- Where: Restated Financials - trade receivables/inventory schedules across 3 years + stub; MD&A. Compute debtor days and inventory days per year; a jump in the DRHP year, especially with working-capital as an object, indicates revenue pull-forward. Cross-check the working-capital computation table in Objects of the Issue (holding-period assumptions are stated there - test them against history).
4. SUDDEN MARGIN EXPANSION IN THE PRE-IPO YEAR
- Where: Restated Financials + Basis for Issue Price (RoNW/EPS trajectory) + KPI section. The Rs 1 crore EBITDA eligibility test (ICDR 229(6)) creates a bright-line incentive: check whether EBITDA crossed Rs 1 crore only in the 2 years used for eligibility. Also check BSE's requirement of operating profit in the single preceding year. Compare margins with the peer table (or construct one if the DRHP claims "no listed peers" - Shanti Inorganics pattern).
5. PROMOTER LOANS REPAID FROM PROCEEDS VIA INDIRECT ROUTES (prohibited: ICDR 230(1)(h))
- Where: Objects of the Issue; Restated Financials - borrowings note (unsecured loans from directors/related parties); Financial Indebtedness chapter; RPT annexure (loans taken/repaid in the stub period). Red route: promoter loans repaid JUST BEFORE filing out of fresh bank borrowing, which the IPO then repays; or "working capital" object sized suspiciously close to promoter-loan balances that vanish in the stub.
6. STATUTORY AUDITOR CHANGES IN THE LOOKBACK WINDOW
- Where: General Information chapter - "Changes in auditors during the last three years" (mandatory disclosure with reasons, Schedule VI Part A); cross-check MCA form ADT-3/AOC-4. A change into a friendlier firm in the restatement window is a classic precursor.
7. AUDITOR WITH NO OTHER LISTED CLIENTS / BARE PEER-REVIEW COVER
- Where: General Information (auditor name, firm registration number, PEER REVIEW CERTIFICATE NUMBER - now mandatory in the document); NFRA/ICAI disciplinary databases; search the firm name across other DRHPs. Note (F.2 finding): operating claims (capacity, utilisation) are typically "certified by the statutory auditor" - the same small firm; weight accordingly.
8. THIN / NOMINAL INDEPENDENT DIRECTORS
- Where: Our Management chapter - ID bios, other directorships, DIN (run DIN through MCA for their other companies; serial SME-IPO IDs sit on many unrelated boards). Remember LODR Regs 17-27 DO NOT APPLY post-listing (Reg 15(2)(b)) - the board you see at IPO faces almost no post-listing governance enforcement, so quality at filing is all you get.
9. GROUP COMPANIES WITH NEGATIVE NET WORTH / OVERLAPPING BUSINESS
- Where: Group Companies chapter (financial summaries mandatory: reserves, sales, PAT of top group companies); Risk Factors (common pursuits/conflict-of-interest disclosure); RPT annexure for transactions with them. Negative-net-worth group entities transacting with the issuer = diversion channel (Debock pattern: Impex Agrotech).
10. UNUSUALLY HIGH SUBSCRIPTION MULTIPLES, ESPECIALLY NII
- Where: post-issue - exchange subscription data and basis-of-allotment advertisement (Reg 270). Context: SEBI CP shows applicant inflation of 245X in FY24; NII splitting is now curbed by the two-sub-bucket rule (Reg 253(2)) and Rs 2 lakh minimum. Treat >100x NII subscription as a coordination signal, not a quality signal; check grey-market-premium chatter in scuttlebutt but never cite it as evidence.
11. LEAD MANAGER WITH A POOR TRACK RECORD
- Where: INSIDE the DRHP - "Price information of past issues handled by the lead manager(s)" (Schedule VI Part A (R): 30/60/90-day price performance table) and objects-achievement of past issues; NSE's published "Merchant Banker wise list of applications in past 6 months" (approved/withdrawn/returned); SEBI enforcement orders (FOCL, Inventure precedents); count of the LM's SME mandates per year (mill-scale volume = thin diligence). Also now disclosed: LM FEES (Reg 245(2)(e)) - an outsized fee percentage is itself a flag (Synoptics: diversion disguised as issue expenses; issue expenses > 12-15% of proceeds is extreme).
12. PRE-IPO PLACEMENT JUST BEFORE THE DRHP
- Where: Capital Structure chapter (allotments in last 1 year / 18 months with names and prices - feeds the WACA table); Reg 274(2) 24-hour reporting of pre-IPO placements. Allottees at a fraction of the band weeks before filing = exit-motivated float creation unlocking at T0+1yr; also note the NSE FCFE dodge (post Apr-2026 formula counts capital raises as FCFE).
13. OBJECTS-OF-ISSUE SHAPE
- Where: Objects of the Issue. Flags: GCP at exactly the min(15%, Rs 10 cr) cap; unidentified acquisitions near the 25% cap; "working capital" > 60-70% of proceeds with aggressive holding-period assumptions; capex from unrelated third-party vendors with quotations from entities with no track record (Trafiksol pattern - verify vendor existence via MCA); issue expenses percentage.
14. CONVERSION / PROMOTER-CHANGE TIMING
- Where: History and Corporate Structure chapter. Just-in-time conversion from proprietorship/LLP (ICDR 229(4): only 1 full FY needed as a company) means the restated "company" history is largely the firm's; check BSE's requirement that the FIRM had the net worth. Promoter change within 1 year bars filing (229(5)/BSE norm) - check for de-facto control changes dressed as "professionalisation".
15. EPF/ESIC vs CLAIMED SCALE
- Where: the new EPF/ESIC disclosure (Reg 245(2)(c), in the 2025-26 DRHPs read for F.2): employees registered and amounts paid. Revenue-per-registered-employee wildly above industry norms = manpower doesn't support claimed operations.

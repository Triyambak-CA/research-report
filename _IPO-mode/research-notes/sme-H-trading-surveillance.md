# H. TRADING, LIQUIDITY AND SURVEILLANCE ON SME COUNTERS

## H.1 Application and lot size - VERIFIED (mixed sources)
- IPO minimum application: TWO LOTS per application AND minimum application size must be ABOVE Rs 2 LAKH - ICDR Reg 267(2) + proviso, w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025 (previously Rs 1 lakh). Applications in multiples of the lot size (Reg 267(3)).
- Secondary-market trading lot: governed by SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DSA/06/2012 dated 21-Feb-2012 (standardised lot-size bands by price; exchanges may review lot size every 6 months with at least 1 month notice). Exchanges periodically revise market lots of listed SME companies under this framework. Whether SEBI has raised the SECONDARY market lot floor to match the new Rs 2 lakh IPO application floor: nothing verified - no circular found; the 2012 framework (approximately Rs 1 lakh lot value at IPO calibration) still appears to govern post-listing lots. Practical effect: an investor cannot sell less than one lot except through the market maker (ICDR Reg 261(5): market maker must buy an entire holding below one contract size in one lot).

## H.2 Listing-day price control - VERIFIED
- NSE circular NSE/SME/62757 dated 04-Jul-2024 (immediate effect; PDF saved): overall cap of 90% OVER THE ISSUE PRICE on the opening/equilibrium price discovered in the special pre-open session for SME IPOs on NSE Emerge. Circular states it is to standardise price discovery across exchanges; applies only to SME, not mainboard. (BSE applies the equivalent cap; BSE's own notice not separately pulled - the NSE circular's "standardize across exchanges" language and press coverage of 04-Jul-2024 support it. Mark BSE-side citation as secondary.)
- Post-listing, normal exchange price bands apply (5% / 10% / 20% as applicable per exchange surveillance settings for the scrip; call-auction mechanisms for illiquid scrips).

## H.3 Surveillance frameworks - VERIFIED (exchange FAQs/circulars)
- ESM (Enhanced Surveillance Measure): per BSE ESM FAQ (downloaded, bse-esm-faq.pdf), SEBI and exchanges apply ESM to "mainboard companies AND stocks listed under SME segment" with market cap below Rs 1,000 crore (framework introduced by BSE Notice 20230602-44 dated 02-Jun-2023 and joint surveillance meetings; extension to mainboard < Rs 1,000 cr per joint surveillance meeting 09-Aug-2024). ESM stages push a scrip to trade-to-trade with 2% price band and periodic call auction.
- GSM (Graded Surveillance Measure) and ASM (Additional Surveillance Measure) frameworks also apply to SME scrips (exchange FAQs; GSM stages escalate to 100% additional surveillance deposit and once-a-week trading at Stage III+).
- Joint surveillance meeting of exchanges and SEBI dated 04-Oct-2024: mandatory pop-up alert messages introduced for ALL SME scrips at order entry (secondary source: exchange bulletins reproduced by brokers; primary circular not pulled - treat citation as secondary).
- BSE migration norms (Notice 20250820-11) explicitly bar migration while under ESM/ASM/GSM/T2T and impose a 2-month cooling-off - surveillance status is now a migration gate.

## H.4 Market making - VERIFIED (ICDR)
- Reg 261(1): compulsory market making for a MINIMUM of 3 YEARS from listing (or from migration to SME exchange), arranged by the lead manager through exchange-registered market makers appointed by the ISSUER. The issuer/LM bear the cost; fees are negotiated (now disclosable: LM fees under Reg 245(2)(e)).
- Reg 261(4) proviso: market-maker inventory at allotment must be at least 5% of listed securities. Reg 261(5): must buy odd holdings below one lot in one lot. Reg 261(6)-(7): cannot buy from promoters/promoter group; promoter holdings cannot be offered to the market maker during the compulsory period.
- Analyst note: two-way quotes are typically thin, spreads wide; the market maker obligation guarantees a QUOTE, not depth.

## H.5 Realistic liquidity - what the report must say
- Typical SME free float is tiny: public issue is >= 25% of post-issue capital (SCRR 19(2)(b) via Reg 252), of which retail/NII halves are dispersed in Rs 2-lakh-plus lots to a minimum of only 200 allottees (Reg 268(1)). Daily traded value on most SME counters is a few lakh rupees; BSE's own migration liquidity floor (Rs 10 lakh average daily turnover) is set at a level many SME counters fail - use that as the benchmark.
- Exit-ability statement for every SME report: state (a) average daily turnover over trailing 6 months, (b) number of trading days with any volume, (c) position size that could be exited within 10% of ADV over 30 days, (d) what happens under ESM/GSM (2% T2T band or weekly trading can trap a position), (e) the cliff at year 3 when compulsory market making ends.
- Impact cost is not published for SME scrips (no index inclusion); compute a proxy from bid-ask snapshots or high-low ranges.

# K. Regulatory changes 2024-2026 (mainboard IPO relevant)

Primary evidence: amendment footnotes in the ICDR consolidated text of 21-Mar-2026 (each item below carries its w.e.f. date from those footnotes unless otherwise cited); SEBI circulars/press releases and MoF gazette as cited.

## K.1 ICDR amendments in the window
1. ICDR (Amendment) Regulations 2024, w.e.f. 18-MAY-2024:
   - Reg 14(1)/16(1)(a): widened who may plug the promoter minimum-contribution shortfall - any non-individual public shareholder holding >= 5% of post-issue capital, or any promoter-group entity, without being tagged promoter (footnotes 39/53).
   - Reg 46(3)/Sch XIII (9)(ii): force-majeure extension of the bid period cut from minimum 3 working days to minimum 1 WORKING DAY (footnotes 108-109, 791-792).
2. ICDR (Amendment) Regulations 2025, w.e.f. 08-MAR-2025 (some Schedule VI items 08-Apr-2025):
   - Reg 24(5): financials in the offer document not more than 6 months old at issue opening.
   - Litigation disclosure floor: "lower of 2% turnover / 2% net worth / 5% of 3-year average absolute PAT" threshold alternative to the board materiality policy; KMP/senior-management criminal and regulatory proceedings added (footnotes 711-712).
   - Capex definition for extended promoter lock-in now includes repayment of loans taken for capex (footnote 59).
   - SAR-scheme shares added to lock-in exemptions and ineligibility computations; peer-reviewed-CA certification alternatives for loan-utilisation certificates; working-capital object disclosures on audited standalone basis; KPI illustrative-table due-diligence note (footnote 677).
3. ICDR (Second Amendment) Regulations 2025:
   - w.e.f. 09-SEP-2025: Reg 8 OFS exception (b) extended to shares arising from conversion of fully paid CCS acquired under NCLT/court schemes (footnote 36).
   - w.e.f. 08-OCT-2025: Reg 7(1)(c) recast - ALL specified securities of promoters, promoter group, selling shareholders, directors, KMP, senior management, QIBs, employees, SR shareholders, financial-sector-regulated entities must be in DEMAT before DRHP filing (previously promoters only) (footnote 33).
4. ICDR (Third Amendment) Regulations 2025, w.e.f. 01-DEC-2025 (board approval 12-Sep-2025):
   - Sch XIII (10)(c)(I): anchor bucket recast - min 2/max 15 anchors up to Rs 250 crore (min allotment Rs 5 crore); above Rs 250 crore, +15 (was +10) additional anchors per additional Rs 250 crore; the old "max 2 anchors up to Rs 10 crore" bucket deleted (footnote 793).
   - Sch XIII (10)(d): anchor reservation raised from one-third (domestic MFs) to 40% of the anchor portion - 33.33% domestic MFs + 6.67% life insurers and PFRDA pension funds, with insurer/pension undersubscription flowing to MFs (footnote 794).
5. ICDR (Amendment) Regulations 2026, gazette 18-Mar-2026, w.e.f. 21-MAR-2026:
   - DRAFT ABRIDGED PROSPECTUS introduced: filed with the DRHP (Reg 25(2)(d)), hosted with it (Reg 26(1)); abridged prospectus filed with the offer document (Reg 25(7)-(8)).
   - Sch VI Part A (4) "Offer Document Summary" OMITTED (footnote 655); Part E/Annexure I abridged-prospectus template substituted with a 12-item format (business summary 250 words, industry summary, promoters, objects summaries, pre/post-offer shareholding of promoters + top-10 public shareholders at floor and cap price, summary restated financials incl. EBITDA and the three cash-flow lines, KPI summary, top-10 internal risk factors, promoter/selling-shareholder WACA incl. 1-year and 3-year windows, board/KMP, auditor-qualification cross-reference, litigation summary).
   - Reg 17(2): where lock-in cannot be created on pre-issue shares (encumbered/pledged), depositories record them as NON-TRANSFERABLE for the lock-in period - unblocks IPOs of companies with wide, partly-pledged public registers (Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas client alert, 24-Mar-2026, secondary).

## K.2 Adjacent rule changes
- Securities Contracts (Regulation) Amendment Rules 2026, MoF gazette notification G.S.R. 184(E) dated 13-MAR-2026, in force from publication (PRIMARY - gazette PDF downloaded from sebi.gov.in, local scrr-amend-2026.pdf). Rule 19(2)(b) substituted with a post-issue-capital-tiered minimum public offer (post-issue capital calculated at offer price):
  (i) <= Rs 1,600 crore: at least 25%;
  (ii) > Rs 1,600 crore to Rs 4,000 crore: shares worth at least Rs 400 crore;
  (iii) > Rs 4,000 crore to Rs 50,000 crore: at least 10%; [(ii) and (iii): MPS 25% within 3 years of listing]
  (iv) > Rs 50,000 crore to Rs 1,00,000 crore: shares worth Rs 1,000 crore AND at least 8%; [MPS 25% within 5 years]
  (v) > Rs 1,00,000 crore to Rs 5,00,000 crore: shares worth Rs 6,250 crore AND at least 2.75%;
  (vi) > Rs 5,00,000 crore: shares worth Rs 15,000 crore AND at least 1%;
  (vii) floor for (vi): in any case at least 2.5% offered to the public.
  MPS glide for (v)/(vi): if public shareholding < 15% at listing - 15% within 5 years and 25% within 10 years; if >= 15% at listing - 25% within 5 years. The extended timelines also apply to companies already listed on/before commencement. Consequence for analysts: mega-IPO free floats at listing are now very small - factor into index-inclusion and supply math.
- T+3 LISTING: SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/TPD1/CIR/P/2023/140 dated 09-Aug-2023 - listing timeline cut from T+6 to T+3 WORKING DAYS (T = issue close); voluntary for issues opening on/after 01-Sep-2023, MANDATORY on/after 01-Dec-2023. Allotment ~T+1, refunds/unblocking and demat credit T+2, listing T+3.
- UPI/ASBA mechanics: no 2024-2026 change to the core rule verified; individual-investor UPI and ASBA-only regime predates the window. NOTHING FURTHER VERIFIED.

## K.3 SEBI research in the window
- SEBI study "SEBI study shows 54% of IPO shares allotted to investors (excluding anchor investors) are sold within a week", press release 02-Sep-2024 (sebi.gov.in press release page 86387): sample = 144 mainboard IPOs listed Apr-2021 to Dec-2023. Findings: 54% of allotted shares (by value, ex-anchor) sold within 1 week; NIIs sold 63.3%, retail 42.7% within a week; individual investors sold 70% by value within a year; strong disposition effect - 67.6% of shares sold within a week when listing gains > 20% vs 23.3% when returns negative; about half the demat accounts used for IPO applications were opened 2021-2023. Use: baseline for modelling day-1 to week-1 float churn and for interpreting oversubscription as flipping demand, not holding demand.
- Dec-2025 SEBI board agenda (17-Dec-2025) covered the encumbered-shares lock-in and abridged-prospectus rationalisation that became the Mar-2026 amendment (per CAM alert, secondary).

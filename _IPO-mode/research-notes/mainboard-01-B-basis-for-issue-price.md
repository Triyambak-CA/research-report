# B. The "Basis for Issue Price" chapter - KPI and WACA rules

Primary source: SEBI (ICDR) Regulations 2018, consolidated text as last amended on 21-Mar-2026, downloaded from sebi.gov.in on 01-Sep-2026 (local copy: icdr-2026-03-21.pdf). All references below are to Schedule VI, Part A, item (9)(K) "Basis for Issue Price" unless stated. The KPI/WACA block (paras (3) and (4) under (9)(K)) was inserted by the SEBI (ICDR) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations 2022, w.e.f. 21-Nov-2022, applicable to all issues where the RHP is filed with the RoC on or after 21-Nov-2022 (footnote 678 of the consolidated text).

## B.1 Baseline quantitative disclosures - Sch VI Part A (9)(K)(1)
- EPS and diluted EPS, pre-issue, for the last 3 years (adjusted for changes in capital), on a consolidated basis, after effect of any bonus/split after the last balance sheet date.
- P/E pre-issue; industry P/E (highest, lowest, average, with source).
- Average Return on Net Worth for the last 3 years.
- NAV per share on last balance sheet AND NAV per share after the issue, compared with the issue price.
- (9)(K)(1)(g): comparison of these accounting ratios with industry average AND with the peer group (companies of comparable size in the same industry), consolidated-vs-consolidated, and peer numbers must be extracted from the peers' regulatory filings.
- 08-Mar-2025 amendment added a note to the illustrative table: lead managers shall exercise due diligence before assigning weights (footnote 677).

## B.2 KPI disclosure - Sch VI Part A (9)(K)(3)
- (3)(a): every KPI and every term inside a KPI must be defined in the "Definitions and Abbreviations" section in simple English.
- (3)(b): KPIs disclosed in the offer document shall be APPROVED BY THE AUDIT COMMITTEE.
- (3)(c): KPIs shall be CERTIFIED by the statutory auditor(s), or a CA/CA firm holding a valid ICAI Peer Review Board certificate, or a Cost Accountant holding a valid ICMAI Peer Review Board certificate.
- (3)(d): the KPI certificate must be included in the list of material documents for inspection (so an analyst can inspect it).
- (3)(e): each KPI must be given for a period CO-TERMINUS with the restated financial information (i.e. 3 years + stub).
- (3)(f): explanation of how management has historically used each KPI to track the business.
- (3)(g): KPI comparisons over time must be explained for acquisitions/dispositions.
- (3)(h)(i): THE LOOKBACK - disclosure of ALL KPIs pertaining to the issuer that have been disclosed to ITS INVESTORS AT ANY POINT OF TIME DURING THE THREE YEARS PRECEDING the date of filing of the DRHP/RHP. Exact period: 3 years before DRHP/RHP filing date.
- (3)(h)(ii): Audit Committee must CONFIRM that verified and audited details of all KPIs shared with earlier investors in that 3-year window are disclosed in the Basis for Issue Price section.
- (3)(h)(v): KPIs disclosed under Basis for Issue Price must be COMPARED WITH INDIAN LISTED PEERS and/or GLOBAL LISTED PEERS (wherever available); peer set = comparable size, same industry, similar business model; if one-to-one comparison impossible, explanatory notes required.
- (3)(i): POST-LISTING CONTINUATION - the issuer must keep disclosing the same KPIs at least once a year for a duration that is at least the LATER of (i) one year after listing (or period specified by SEBI) or (ii) till full utilisation of issue proceeds; changes must be explained; ongoing KPIs must continue to be certified per (3)(c). Analyst use: this creates the only "guidance scorecard" raw material an IPO company will have - log the DRHP KPIs and audit the first annual re-disclosure against them.

## B.3 WACA disclosure - Sch VI Part A (9)(K)(4)
Three distinct windows exist:
1. (4)(a) PRIMARY: price per share in primary/new issuances (equity or convertibles, excluding ESOP/bonus) during the 18 MONTHS preceding DRHP/RHP filing, where the issuance is >= 5 per cent of fully diluted pre-issue capital in a single transaction or multiple transactions over a ROLLING 30-DAY span.
2. (4)(b) SECONDARY: price per share in secondary sale/acquisition (excluding gifts) during the 18 MONTHS preceding DRHP/RHP filing, where promoter/promoter group entities, OFS selling shareholders, or shareholders with board nomination rights are a party, and the transaction is >= 5 per cent of fully diluted pre-issue capital (same rolling 30-day aggregation).
3. Note 1 FALLBACK: if there are no transactions under (a) and (b), disclose the LAST 5 primary or secondary transactions (secondary limited to the same insider parties), NOT OLDER THAN 3 YEARS before DRHP/RHP filing, irrespective of size.
- Primary and secondary WACA are computed and tabulated SEPARATELY - the mandated table has one row "WACA of Primary issuance" and one row "WACA of Secondary transactions".
- Prices must be adjusted for corporate actions (split, bonus) - Note 2.
- (4)(c): FLOOR PRICE AND CAP PRICE must each be disclosed AS [x] TIMES THE WACA of primary and of secondary transactions, in the prescribed table.
- (4)(d): detailed explanation of why the offer/cap price is [x] times WACA, along with a comparison of the issuer's KPIs and financial ratios for the last 3 full years plus stub.
- (4)(e): explanation of external factors that influenced pricing, if any.
- (4)(f): the WACA-multiple table must ALSO appear in the PRICE BAND ADVERTISEMENT under the "Risks to Investors" section, together with a RECOMMENDATION OF A COMMITTEE OF INDEPENDENT DIRECTORS stating that the price band is justified based on the quantitative factors/KPIs vis-a-vis the WACA. Analyst use: the price band ad (published minimum 2 working days before opening; Reg 29) is the fastest single-page source for the WACA multiple.

## B.4 Other cost-of-acquisition disclosures elsewhere in the document (do not confuse with B.3)
- Front outside cover page: selling shareholders in tabular format WITH THEIR AVERAGE COST OF ACQUISITION - Sch VI Part A (1)(a)(1), as substituted w.e.f. 14-Jan-2022.
- TRANSITION NOTE: the old "Offer Document Summary" section (former Sch VI Part A (4)) - which carried (M) weighted average acquisition price of each promoter/selling shareholder in the last 1 year and (N) their average cost of acquisition - was OMITTED by the ICDR (Amendment) Regulations 2026 w.e.f. 21-Mar-2026 (footnote 655). DRHPs filed before 21-Mar-2026 still contain it; later filings do not.
- Its replacement: the revamped ABRIDGED PROSPECTUS and new DRAFT ABRIDGED PROSPECTUS (Sch VI Part E + Annexure I, substituted w.e.f. 21-Mar-2026), a 12-item template whose item 9 requires a per-promoter and per-selling-shareholder WACA table (shares held, WACA per share, WACA of shares acquired in last 1 year) PLUS "weighted average cost of acquisition of all shares transacted in the ONE YEAR AND THREE YEARS preceding the date of the draft offer document / offer document". Item 7 requires a summary of the KPIs used in Basis for Offer Price for 3 years + stub. The draft abridged prospectus is filed WITH the DRHP and hosted alongside it (Reg 25(2)(d), Reg 26(1) as amended w.e.f. 21-Mar-2026), so this summary now exists from DRHP stage.
So the full set of lookback windows an analyst can extract: since-inception average cost, 1-year weighted average price, 18-month primary WACA, 18-month secondary WACA, 3-year last-5-transactions fallback, and 1-year and 3-year all-shares-transacted WACA in the abridged prospectus.

## B.4A Operationalising circular - KPI Industry Standards
- SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2025/28 dated 28-Feb-2025, "Industry Standards on Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Disclosures in the draft Offer Document and Offer Document" (title, number and date verified from the SEBI circular page itself): mandates that issuers and merchant bankers follow the KPI industry standards formulated by the Industry Standards Forum (ASSOCHAM, CII, FICCI) in consultation with SEBI, published on the industry associations' and stock exchanges' websites. Applicability: draft offer documents / offer documents filed on or after 01-Apr-2025 (applicability date per multiple secondary summaries of the circular - taxmann/lexology/CAM blog Mar-2025; treat as reliable but SECONDARY). Practical effect: KPI identification/disclosure in post-Apr-2025 DRHPs follows a standardised ISF template - pull the ISF standards document from the BSE/NSE website when auditing a KPI set.

## B.5 Analyst checks this enables (concrete)
- Compute cap price / 18-month primary WACA and cap price / most recent pre-IPO round price. A multiple > 2x within 18 months demands the (4)(d) explanation be tested against actual KPI growth over the same window.
- Diff the KPI list in the DRHP against the KPI list in the RHP: KPIs quietly dropped between DRHP and RHP were probably deteriorating.
- Check the (3)(h)(i) investor-KPI list against the KPIs the company markets publicly - a KPI shown to Series-C investors but absent from Basis for Issue Price is a red flag and arguably a compliance breach.
- Pull the KPI certificate from material documents for inspection; note who certified (statutory auditor vs an unrelated peer-reviewed CA).
- One year after listing, score B.2(3)(i) compliance: are the same KPIs re-disclosed, and how did they move vs the offer-document narrative.

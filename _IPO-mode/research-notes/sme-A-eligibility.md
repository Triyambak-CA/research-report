# A. ELIGIBILITY - SME IPO (Chapter IX, SEBI ICDR 2018 as amended up to 21-Mar-2026)

Source: SEBI (ICDR) Regulations 2018, consolidated text "Amended upto March 21, 2026", Chapter IX (Regs 227-281A). All regulation numbers below verified against this text. The 2024-25 tightening was effected by the SEBI (ICDR) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025, w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025 (per footnotes in the consolidated text), following the SEBI Board meeting of 18-Dec-2024 (board meeting date to be cross-checked - see A.7).

## A.1 Operating profit / EBITDA test - VERIFIED EXACTLY
Reg 229(6) (inserted by ICDR (Amendment) Regulations 2025, w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025):
"An issuer may make an initial public offer, only if the issuer had minimum operating profits (earnings before interest, depreciation and tax) of Rs 1 crore from operations for at least two out of the three previous financial years."
- Threshold: Rs 1 crore operating profit (EBIDT) FROM OPERATIONS.
- Frequency: at least 2 out of the 3 previous financial years.
- Note the wording "from operations" - other income does not count. An analyst must recompute EBITDA excluding other income from the restated financials.

## A.2 Post-issue paid-up capital band - VERIFIED
- Reg 229(1): eligible only if post-issue paid-up capital <= Rs 10 crore.
- Reg 229(2): issuer with post-issue paid-up capital > Rs 10 crore and up to Rs 25 crore MAY also use Chapter IX.
- The word "paid-up" was substituted for "face value" by the ICDR (Amendment) Regulations 2025 w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025 (footnote 411). Practical ceiling for the SME segment: Rs 25 crore post-issue paid-up capital. Above that, mainboard.
- Reg 280(2): if a further issue (rights/preferential/bonus) would push paid-up capital beyond Rs 25 crore, the issuer must migrate to the main board - BUT a proviso inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025 permits staying on the SME exchange if the issuer undertakes to comply with main-board LODR provisions.

## A.3 Track record and conversion/promoter-change conditions - VERIFIED
- Reg 229(3): issuer must satisfy track record / other eligibility conditions OF THE SME EXCHANGE(S) - exchange norms are load-bearing (see exchange-norms file).
- Reg 229(4) (inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): if converted from proprietorship/partnership/LLP, the company must have existed at least ONE FULL FINANCIAL YEAR before filing the draft offer document; post-conversion restated financials per Schedule III of Companies Act 2013.
- Reg 229(5) (inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): if there is a complete change of promoter, or new promoter(s) acquired more than 50% of shareholding, DRHP may be filed only after ONE YEAR from the final change.

## A.4 Minimum allottees and application size - VERIFIED
- Reg 268(1): allotment shall NOT be made if number of allottees < 200 (substituted for "fifty" w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025).
- Reg 267(2): minimum application size = TWO LOTS per application; proviso: "the minimum application size shall be above Rs 2 lakhs" (substituted for "one lakh rupees" w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025).
- Minimum ISSUE size: no minimum issue size found in Chapter IX of ICDR. NOTHING VERIFIED in ICDR for a minimum issue size; check exchange norms (NSE Emerge / BSE SME) separately.

## A.5 Disqualifications - Reg 228 - VERIFIED
Issuer NOT eligible if:
(a) issuer, promoters, promoter group, directors or selling shareholders debarred from capital market by SEBI;
(b) any promoter/director is promoter/director of another company so debarred;
(c) issuer or any promoter/director is a wilful defaulter or fraudulent borrower;
(d) any promoter/director is a fugitive economic offender;
(e) (inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025) any outstanding convertible securities or rights entitling any person to equity shares - EXCEPT ESOPs compliant with Companies Act 2013, and fully paid-up convertibles required to convert before RHP/prospectus filing.
Explanation: past debarment whose period has expired does not disqualify.

## A.6 Other 08-Mar-2025 issue-mechanics changes (verified in Chapter IX text)
- Reg 253(1): allocation in net offer (book built): >=35% individual investors applying minimum application size; >=15% NII; <=50% QIB (5% of QIB to MFs). "Retail" nomenclature removed; category is "individual investors who apply for minimum application size".
- Reg 253(2) (inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): NII bucket split - 1/3 for applications > 2 lots and up to Rs 10 lakh; 2/3 for > Rs 10 lakh.
- Reg 253(3) fixed price issues: min 50% to individual investors applying minimum application size.
- Reg 260: 100% underwriting mandatory; lead manager must underwrite at least 15% of issue size on own account.
- Reg 265: issue opens at least 3 working days after prospectus filed with RoC. Reg 266: open 3-10 working days.
- Reg 274 (substituted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): promoter/promoter-group transactions between DRHP filing and issue closure, and any pre-IPO placement, reportable to exchange within 24 hours.
- Reg 245(2)(c)-(e) (inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): offer document must carry EPF/ESIC registration and payment details; the LEAD MANAGER'S SITE VISIT REPORT is a material document for inspection; lead manager fees must be disclosed.
- Definition 2(1)(c): anchor investor in a Chapter IX issue = QIB applying for at least Rs 2 crore (vs Rs 10 crore mainboard).

## A.7 Which board meeting / notification - PARTIALLY VERIFIED
- Amendment instrument: SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) (Amendment) Regulations, 2025, w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025 - VERIFIED via footnotes 409-412, 416-418, 489-493 etc. in the consolidated text. All the tightening items (EBITDA test, OFS caps, GCP cap, staggered lock-in, 200 allottees, Rs 2 lakh min application, 21-day public comment, monitoring agency at Rs 50 crore) carry this citation.
- Approving board meeting: VERIFIED - SEBI Board meeting of 18-Dec-2024, Press Release No. 37/2024 dated 18-Dec-2024 (item 2, "Review of SME framework under SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018, and applicability of corporate governance provisions under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 on SME companies"). PR items 2.1-2.9 match the notified amendments: Rs 1 crore operating profit in any 2 of 3 previous FYs at DRHP filing; OFS <= 20% of issue size and selling shareholders <= 50% of their holding; phased release of promoter excess-over-MPC (50% after 1 year, 50% after 2 years); NII allocation aligned to mainboard; GCP capped at 15% or Rs 10 crore whichever is lower; no repayment of promoter/promoter-group/related-party loans directly or indirectly; 21-day public comment on DRHP with newspaper announcement and QR code; further issues without mainboard migration subject to mainboard-LODR compliance undertaking; RPT norms extended to SME listed entities with materiality threshold of 10% of annual consolidated turnover or Rs 50 crore, whichever is LOWER. (PDF saved: sebi-pr-37-2024.pdf)
- Prospective application: the amendments took effect 08-Mar-2025; DRHPs already filed before that date were grandfathered under the old regime - UNVERIFIED as a general statement; the regulations themselves state effectivity from 08-Mar-2025 with no transition clause visible in Chapter IX.

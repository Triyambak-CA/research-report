# D. LOCK-IN - SME-SPECIFIC

Source: SEBI ICDR 2018 consolidated text amended up to 21-Mar-2026, Regs 236-243.

## D.1 Minimum promoters' contribution (MPC) - VERIFIED
- Reg 236(1): promoters must hold at least 20% of post-issue capital. Shortfall (where promoter post-issue holding < 20%) may be met by AIFs, FVCIs, scheduled commercial banks, PFIs, IRDAI-registered insurers, any non-individual public shareholder holding >= 5% post-issue, or promoter-group entities (the last two inserted w.e.f. 18-May-2024), capped at 10% of post-issue capital, without being named promoters. MPC requirement does not apply if no identifiable promoter.
- Reg 237: ineligible securities for MPC - shares acquired in preceding 3 years for non-cash consideration involving revaluation/intangibles capitalisation; bonus out of revaluation reserves/unrealised profits; shares acquired in preceding 1 year below the IPO price (unless difference paid in); pledged shares.

## D.2 Lock-in durations - VERIFIED
- Reg 238(a): MPC locked in for 3 YEARS from date of commencement of commercial production or date of allotment in the IPO, whichever is LATER.
- Reg 238(b) (substituted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025; earlier: 1 year for the whole excess): promoter holding IN EXCESS of MPC:
  (i) 50% of the excess locked in for 2 YEARS from allotment;
  (ii) remaining 50% locked in for 1 YEAR from allotment.
- Reg 239: entire pre-issue capital of NON-promoters locked in 1 YEAR from allotment. Exemptions: ESOP shares (per SBEB Regs), VCF/AIF Cat I-II/FVCI shares (locked 1 year from their purchase date, with holding period of compulsorily convertibles counted).
- Anchor investors - VERIFIED: Schedule XIII para (10)(j) (substituted w.e.f. 01-Apr-2022) applies to Chapter IX issues too: lock-in of 90 days on 50% of shares allotted to anchor investors and 30 days on the remaining 50%, from date of allotment. Applies only in BOOK-BUILT SME issues; fixed-price SME issues have no anchor category.
- Contrast mainboard: mainboard MPC lock-in was REDUCED to 18 months (where objects are not capex-heavy) by the 2021 amendment; SME retains the full 3 years. Excess-over-MPC on mainboard is 6 months; SME is 1-2 years staggered. SME lock-ins are materially LONGER.

## D.3 Pledge and inter-se transfer - VERIFIED
- Reg 242: locked-in promoter shares may be pledged only to SCBs/PFIs/SI-NBFCs/HFCs and (for MPC shares) only where the loan is to the issuer/subsidiary for financing objects of the issue and pledge is a sanction term. Lock-in survives invocation.
- Reg 243: inter-se transfer among promoters/promoter group permitted; lock-in continues in transferee's hands.
- Reg 261(7): promoter holding is NOT eligible for offer to the market maker during the compulsory market-making period; unlocked promoter shares tradable only with prior SME-exchange permission.

## D.4 Post-listing supply calendar template (build for every SME issue)
T0 = date of allotment. For each row compute shares and % of post-issue capital:
- T0+30 days: 50% of anchor allocation released (Schedule XIII 10(j)) - book-built issues only.
- T0+90 days: remaining 50% of anchor allocation released (Schedule XIII 10(j)) - book-built issues only.
- T0+1 year: (a) all NON-promoter pre-issue capital (less VC/AIF shares whose own 1-year-from-purchase runs later); (b) 50% of promoter excess-over-MPC.
- T0+2 years: remaining 50% of promoter excess-over-MPC.
- T0+3 years (or 3 years from commercial production if later): MPC (20% of post-issue capital).
- T0+3 years: compulsory market making ends (Reg 261(1)) - liquidity support disappears at the same time the last promoter block unlocks.
- Overlay: pre-IPO placement investors (who came in just before DRHP) sit in the non-promoter 1-year bucket - identify them by name and cost from the Capital Structure chapter; their cost basis vs IPO price tells you the T0+1yr overhang incentive.
- Overlay: migration to mainboard typically ~2-3 years out (see file I); check whether unlock dates cluster around the migration window.

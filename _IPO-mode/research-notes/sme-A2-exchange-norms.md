# A2. EXCHANGE-LEVEL ELIGIBILITY NORMS (load-bearing via ICDR Reg 229(3))

## NSE Emerge (source: nseindia.com static page "Eligibility Criteria - Public Issues Emerge", accessed 01-Sep-2026; plus NSE circulars cited)
- Incorporation: company incorporated in India under Companies Act 1956/2013.
- Post-issue paid-up capital (face value) <= Rs 25 crore.
- Track record: at least 3 years of (i) the applicant, or (ii) the promoters/promoting company (Indian or foreign), or (iii) the erstwhile proprietorship/partnership firm since converted. "Promoters" for this purpose = person(s) with minimum 3 years experience in the SAME LINE OF BUSINESS holding at least 20% of post-issue capital (individually or severally).
- Financial: operating profit (EBIDT) of Rs 1 crore from operations in any 2 of 3 previous FYs (mirrors ICDR 229(6)); POSITIVE NET WORTH; positive Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE) in at least 2 of 3 FYs preceding the application.
  - FCFE test introduced by NSE circular NSE/SME/63532 dated 22-Aug-2024, applicable to all DRHPs filed on/after 01-Sep-2024. Original formula: FCFE = Cash flow from operations - purchase of fixed assets + net borrowings - interest x (1-T), all from audited statements filed with offer documents.
  - REVISED by NSE circular NSE/SME/73818 dated 20-Apr-2026 (immediate effect): FCFE now ADDS "Proceeds from issuance of Capital" (equity + preference + securities premium received in cash). Analyst note: this revision makes the test much easier to pass - a pre-IPO placement can manufacture positive FCFE; recompute FCFE ex-capital-raises yourself.
- OFS: <= 20% of issue size; selling shareholders <= 50% of their holding (mirrors ICDR).
- Other conditions: no BIFR reference / no IBC proceedings admitted against issuer or promoting companies; no winding-up petition admitted by NCLT/court; no material regulatory or disciplinary action by an exchange/regulator against the applicant in past 3 years; objects must not include repayment of promoter/promoter-group/related-party loans directly or indirectly.
- Merchant banker condition: none of the merchant bankers responsible for due diligence/drafting may have had an IPO draft offer document RETURNED by the Exchange in the past 6 months. NSE publishes a "Merchant Banker wise list of applications in past 6 months" (approved/withdrawn/returned) - a direct data source for the lead-manager red-flag check.
- Mandatory offer-document disclosures required by NSE: regulatory/disciplinary actions against promoter group and group companies in past 1 year; payment defaults to debenture/bond/FD holders, banks, FIs in past 3 years; litigation record; criminal cases/investigations against directors.
- Minimum issue size: NONE stated on the NSE criteria page and none in ICDR Chapter IX. nothing verified for any exchange-level minimum issue size at NSE Emerge.

## BSE SME (source: bsesme.com "Criteria for New Listing" page, accessed 01-Sep-2026)
- Post-issue paid-up capital: not more than Rs 25 crore.
- Net worth: at least Rs 1 crore for the 2 preceding full FYs (converted firms: the firm/LLP must show the same).
- Net tangible assets: Rs 3 crore in the last preceding full FY.
- Track record: 3 years (applicant, or together with the taken-over proprietorship/partnership/LLP); the applicant COMPANY must have at least 1 full FY of operations and audited results. ALTERNATIVE ROUTE: if no 3-year track record, project must be appraised and funded by NABARD/SIDBI/banks (non-cooperative)/FIs, again with 1 full FY of operations.
- Operating profit: Rs 1 crore EBIDT from operations in 2 of 3 latest FYs preceding application, AND operating profit in the one full FY preceding application. Appraised-project route: positive EBIDT in one full preceding FY.
- Leverage ratio: not more than 3:1 (relaxation possible for finance companies).
- Disciplinary/default: no suspension actions against promoters or their companies; promoters/directors not promoters/directors of compulsorily delisted or suspended companies; directors not disqualified/debarred; no pending defaults to debenture/bond/FD holders by issuer, promoters, subsidiaries.
- Name change in last 1 year: activity under the new name must have contributed >= 50% of restated consolidated revenue of the preceding full FY.
- Additional sector criteria: broking companies (net worth Rs 5 cr + PBT Rs 5 cr in 2 of 3 years, OR net worth Rs 25 cr in 3 of 5 years; NTA Rs 3 cr; post-issue paid-up capital >= Rs 3 cr); microfinance companies (AUM >= Rs 100 cr, >= 10,000 clients, no public deposits).
- Other: functional website mandatory; 100% promoter holding in demat; both depositories; NO CHANGE IN PROMOTERS in preceding 1 year from application; board composition per Companies Act 2013 at in-principle approval; not referred to NCLT under IBC; no admitted winding-up petition.
- Cooling-off: 6-month gap after withdrawal/rejection of an issue by SEBI/exchanges.
- Minimum issue size: none stated. nothing verified.

## Deltas between the two exchanges an analyst should note
- BSE requires positive net worth Rs 1 cr for 2 years + NTA Rs 3 cr + leverage <= 3:1 + EBIDT positive in the immediately preceding year; NSE instead requires positive FCFE 2 of 3 years. A company can qualify on one exchange and fail the other; WHERE the issuer chose to file is itself a signal.
- NSE bars promoter-change in the ICDR way (Reg 229(5), 1 year); BSE bars any promoter change in the preceding 1 year at exchange level too.

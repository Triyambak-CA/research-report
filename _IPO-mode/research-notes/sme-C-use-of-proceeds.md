# C. USE OF PROCEEDS - SME-SPECIFIC RESTRICTIONS

Source: SEBI ICDR 2018 consolidated text amended up to 21-Mar-2026, Chapter IX.

## C.1 General Corporate Purposes cap - VERIFIED
Reg 230(2) (as amended w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): GCP amount "shall not exceed fifteen per cent of the amount being raised by the issuer or Rs 10 crores, whichever is less".
- Previous position: 25% with no absolute ceiling. "fifteen" substituted for "twenty five" and the Rs 10 crore ceiling inserted, both w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025 (footnotes 417-418).
- Contrast mainboard: GCP cap is 25% (and combined GCP + unidentified acquisition 35%) with no absolute rupee ceiling - mainboard figures are the parallel agent's scope.

## C.2 Prohibition on repaying promoter/related-party loans - VERIFIED
Reg 230(1)(h) (inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): objects "should not consist of repayment of loan taken from promoter, promoter group or any related party, from the issue proceeds, directly or indirectly".
- Note "directly or indirectly" - the analyst must still test indirect routes: proceeds used for "working capital" that replaces promoter loans repaid contemporaneously; repayment of a bank loan personally guaranteed or collateralised by promoter assets that then releases the promoter; payments to related-party vendors. Check DRHP chapters: Objects of the Issue, Restated Financials (borrowings note), Related Party Transactions, and Financial Indebtedness.

## C.3 Monitoring agency - VERIFIED
Reg 262(1): monitoring agency (a SEBI-registered CREDIT RATING AGENCY) mandatory if issue size EXCLUDING OFS exceeds Rs 50 crore ("Rs 50 crores" substituted for "one hundred crore rupees" w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025).
- Reg 262(2)-(4): quarterly monitoring reports until 100% utilisation; issuer must publicly disseminate within 45 days of quarter end (website + exchange).
- Reg 262(5) (inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): where no monitoring agency is required, a STATUTORY AUDITOR'S certificate on utilisation must accompany quarterly financial results filed with the exchange until full utilisation.
- Reg 262(6) (inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): if working capital object > Rs 5 crore, statutory auditor certificate on use of funds as working capital, in the same format as disclosed in the offer document, with quarterly results, until fully utilised.
- Practical note: most SME issues are below Rs 50 crore, so the auditor-certificate route (262(5)-(6)) is the norm; these certificates are a post-listing verification artefact the analyst should pull from exchange filings.

## C.4 Unidentified / inorganic acquisitions - VERIFIED
Reg 230(3) (inserted by ICDR (Amendment) Regulations 2022 w.e.f. 14-Jan-2022): GCP + objects with unidentified acquisition/investment target combined <= 35% of amount raised; unidentified-acquisition objects alone <= 25%. Limits do not apply if the target is identified with specific disclosures in the DRHP/offer document.
- Interaction: post 08-Mar-2025 the GCP leg is separately capped at min(15%, Rs 10 crore), so the binding SME structure is GCP <= min(15%, Rs 10 cr) AND unidentified acquisitions <= 25% AND combined <= 35%.

## C.5 Other proceeds-related controls
- Reg 230(1)(e): firm arrangements of finance for 75% of stated means of finance (excluding issue proceeds and identifiable internal accruals); sanction letters to be disclosed where bank-funded (proviso inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025).
- Reg 281A + Schedule XX: exit offer to dissenting shareholders on change in objects (mirrors Companies Act 2013 s.13(8)/27).

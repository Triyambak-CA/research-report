# B. OFS AND PROMOTER SELLDOWN CAPS (SME-specific)

Source: SEBI ICDR 2018 consolidated text amended up to 21-Mar-2026, Reg 230(1)(f)-(g), both inserted by SEBI (ICDR) (Amendment) Regulations 2025 w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025.

## B.1 OFS cap - VERIFIED
Reg 230(1)(f): "the size of offer for sale by selling shareholders shall not exceed twenty per cent of the total issue size".
- Cap: OFS <= 20% of TOTAL ISSUE SIZE.

## B.2 Per-shareholder selldown cap - VERIFIED
Reg 230(1)(g): "the shares being offered for sale by selling shareholders shall not exceed fifty per cent of such selling shareholders' pre-issue shareholding on a fully diluted basis".
- Each selling shareholder may sell at most 50% of their own pre-issue holding (fully diluted basis).

## B.3 Contrast with mainboard
- Mainboard (Chapter II) has NO cap on OFS as a share of issue size and no 50%-of-holding cap; mainboard constraints are of a different type (Reg 8/8A ICDR: minimum holding period of 1 year for offered shares, and Reg 8A limits for large pre-IPO shareholders in issuers without identifiable promoter... exact mainboard rules are the parallel agent's scope).
- Analytical consequence: an SME IPO is structurally >= 80% fresh issue. The classic mainboard red flag "IPO is mostly promoter exit" cannot occur at the IPO itself on SME; promoter exit risk instead migrates to the POST-listing window when the staggered lock-ins fall away (see file D) and to pre-IPO placements done just before the DRHP (reportable within 24 hours under Reg 274(2)).

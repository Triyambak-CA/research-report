# A. DRHP/RHP anatomy and sourcing

Source: SEBI (ICDR) Regulations 2018, consolidated as amended to 21-Mar-2026 (icdr-2026-03-21.pdf); live fetch tests on sebi.gov.in / nseindia.com / bseindia.com run on 01-Sep-2026 from this machine; ESDS Software Solution DRHP (30-Mar-2025) and RHP (24-Aug-2026) downloaded as worked examples.

## A.1 Mandatory disclosure structure - Reg 24(2)(b) + Schedule VI Part A
Reg 24(1): offer document must contain all material disclosures, true and adequate. Reg 24(2): RHP/prospectus must contain Companies Act 2013 disclosures plus Schedule VI Part A. The Part A ordered structure (top-level items as numbered in the Schedule; practical DRHP chapter names in brackets):
1. (1) Cover pages - issue details, selling shareholders WITH average cost of acquisition, promoters, eligibility route (Reg 6(1) vs 6(2)) stated on cover, General Risk / Issuer's Absolute Responsibility boxes, BRLMs, issue schedule.
2. (2) Table of contents; (3) Definitions and abbreviations (must define every KPI term).
3. (4) Offer Document Summary - OMITTED w.e.f. 21-Mar-2026 (ICDR Amendment Regulations 2026, footnote 655). Present in DRHPs filed before that date (summary financials, litigation table, 3-year RPT summary, 1-year weighted average acquisition price of promoters/selling shareholders, average cost of acquisition, pre-IPO placement details). For later filings the condensate is the new DRAFT ABRIDGED PROSPECTUS (Part E + Annexure I, 12-item template) filed and hosted with the DRHP.
4. (5) Risk Factors - descending order of materiality; internal vs external; mandatory topic list (see file I).
5. (6) Introduction ("The Issue", summary financial information); (7) General information (BRLMs, syndicate, auditors etc.).
6. (8) Capital Structure - full share-capital build-up, every allotment with date/price/allottees, promoter contribution and lock-in detail, ESOP outstanding, top shareholders.
7. (9) Particulars of the Issue = "Objects of the Issue" + interim use of funds + issue expenses + (9)(K) BASIS FOR ISSUE PRICE (KPIs + WACA; see file B) + (9)(L) tax benefits statement.
8. (10) About the Issuer = Industry Overview (commissioned report; complete report must be a material document) + Business Overview + History/corporate structure + Management (directors, KMP) + Promoters and Promoter Group + dividend policy.
9. (11) Financial Statements = restated consolidated financials 3 years + stub, restatement reconciliation, other financial information (EPS/RoNW/NAV/EBITDA), MD&A-equivalent ["Management's Discussion and Analysis" appears here in practice], capitalisation statement, financial indebtedness.
10. (12) Legal and Other Information = Outstanding Litigations and Material Developments + Government approvals.
11. (13) Group companies information.
12. (14) Other Regulatory and Statutory Disclosures - authority for issue, eligibility (which Reg 6 route and the arithmetic), disclaimer clauses, previous issues, performance vis-a-vis objects, BRLM PRICE INFORMATION OF PAST ISSUES (see file G), stock market data, investor grievances (SCORES).
13. (15) Offering Information = terms of the issue, issue procedure, allocation/basis of allotment, restrictions on foreign ownership; (15)(B)(27) utilisation-of-proceeds undertakings.
14. (16) Other material disclosures; (18) Other Information = material contracts and DOCUMENTS FOR INSPECTION (KPI certificate, industry report, auditor certificates live here) + declarations signed by all directors and CFO.

Highest-yield chapters for forensic work, in order: Capital Structure; Basis for Issue Price; Objects; restated financials (restatement reconciliation + RPT note); Risk Factors (first ten); Outstanding Litigation; Other Regulatory and Statutory Disclosures (BRLM track record + performance vs objects); MD&A; Summary (WACA and RPT condensates).

## A.2 Restated financials requirement (exact)
- Sch VI Part A (11)(I)(A)(i): restated CONSOLIDATED Ind AS financials for THREE years + STUB. Stub required if the latest full FY is older than 6 months at filing; stub must not end more than 6 months before the filing date. Reg 24(5) (inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): financials in the offer document must not be more than 6 months old at ISSUE OPENING - this forces RHP-stage updates when SEBI review runs long.

## A.3 Where documents are published + retrieval mechanics (tested 01-Sep-2026)
- SEBI: Filings > Public Issues listing, plain-HTML, served to bare curl with HTTP 200 (tested: https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do?doListing=yes&sid=3&ssid=15&smid=11 returned the Sep-2026/Aug-2026 filings including RHPs). Each filing page links a PDF under https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/<mon-yyyy>/<id>.pdf - directly curl-able, no blocking, no headers needed (downloaded the 4.2MB ICDR consolidation, 7.5MB ESDS DRHP and 14.2MB ESDS RHP this way). Google/WebSearch with site:sebi.gov.in is the fastest way to find a specific company's filing page.
- SEBI observation status: Filings > Processing Status (https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do?doListing=yes&sid=3&ssid=14&smid=8), a periodically updated PDF/HTML "Processing status of draft offer documents" showing receipt date, clarification status, and date observations issued. Fetches fine with curl (tested, HTTP 200, latest Aug-2026).
- NSE: blocks bare curl (connection reset); returns HTTP 200 with a browser User-Agent header (tested). BSE: reachable (301 redirect to https-www host; follow with -L). Both exchanges host offer documents and, critically, the ANCHOR ALLOCATION PDFs and basis-of-allotment data that SEBI does not host.
- BRLM websites: each lead manager must host the DRHP/RHP; issuer website must host draft and final offer documents plus (w.e.f. 21-Mar-2026) the draft abridged prospectus and abridged prospectus (Reg 25(2)(d), 25(7)-(8) as amended by ICDR (Amendment) Regulations 2026).
- Working route for an automated agent: (1) websearch "site:sebi.gov.in <company> DRHP/RHP" or walk the public-issues listing; (2) grep the filing page for /sebi_data/attachdocs/*.pdf; (3) curl the PDF; (4) pdftotext -layout. Confirmed end-to-end twice.

## A.4 Observation letter process and DRHP-to-RHP timeline
- Reg 25(1)-(3): DRHP filed with SEBI (fees per Sch III) through the lead managers with due-diligence certificate; also filed with the stock exchanges; (2)(d) draft abridged prospectus now filed too (21-Mar-2026 amendment).
- Reg 25(4): SEBI may specify changes or issue observations WITHIN 30 DAYS from the LATEST of: receipt of DRHP; satisfactory reply from lead managers to SEBI clarifications; receipt of clarification from any other regulator/agency; receipt of the in-principle approval letter from the exchange(s). In practice the clock restarts with every clarification cycle - ESDS example: DRHP 30-Mar-2025, RHP 24-Aug-2026, i.e. about 17 months. Typical clean cases run about 2-4 months DRHP-to-observations (UNVERIFIED as a statistic; verify per issue from the Processing Status file).
- Reg 25(5)-(6): issuer files updated DRHP incorporating observations, highlighting changes; Schedule XVI changes (issue size beyond limits, objects changes etc.) force a FRESH DRHP.
- Reg 44(1): issue must OPEN WITHIN 12 MONTHS of SEBI observations. Reg 44(2): issue opens at least 3 working days after RHP filed with RoC. Public comments: DRHP hosted for comments for at least 21 days from publication of the public announcement (Reg 26(1)); the announcement itself must appear within 2 working days of DRHP filing in one English, one Hindi and one regional daily (Reg 26(2)); lead managers file the comments received and consequential changes with SEBI after the window (Reg 26(3)).
- Confidential pre-filing alternative (Chapter IIA, Regs 59A-59M, inserted Nov-2022): pre-filed draft offer document reviewed confidentially; UDRHP-I made public post-observations (21-day comments), then UDRHP-II, then RHP; issue may open within 18 MONTHS of observations, UDRHP-I to be filed within 16 months (Reg 59D(4) area, page 60 of consolidation). Tata Play/Swiggy-style route; if a target uses it, there is no public DRHP-stage document to diff until UDRHP-I.

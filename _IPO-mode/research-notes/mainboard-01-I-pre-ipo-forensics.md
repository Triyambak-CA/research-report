# I. What a pre-IPO company still discloses that enables forensics

Source: SEBI (ICDR) Regulations 2018, consolidated as amended to 21-Mar-2026 (local copy icdr-2026-03-21.pdf), read 01-Sep-2026. Schedule VI Part A references throughout.

## I.1 Restated financial information and restatement adjustments - Sch VI Part A (11)
- Requirement: restated CONSOLIDATED financial statements per Ind AS for THREE YEARS plus a STUB period. Stub is mandatory if the latest full-FY accounts are OLDER THAN 6 MONTHS at the filing date of the DRHP/offer document; the stub must NOT end earlier than 6 months before the filing date (Sch VI Part A (11)(I)(A)(i)). Separately, Reg 24(5) (inserted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025): the lead managers must ensure the restated financials in the offer document must not be more than 6 months old from the ISSUE OPENING date.
- Restatement mechanics (11)(I)(A)(i)(a)-(c): consistent policies across all periods aligned to the latest period; significant errors, non-provisions, regroupings pushed back into the periods they belong to; AUDIT QUALIFICATIONS (SA 705 modifications) that are quantifiable MUST BE ADJUSTED in the restated numbers in the appropriate period; unquantifiable qualifications must be explained in the notes; and a RECONCILIATION between audited equity/profit and restated equity/profit IN COLUMNAR FORMAT for each period.
- Why high-yield: the reconciliation ("Statement of Restatement Adjustments" / "Statement of Adjustments to Audited Financial Statements") is the one table where three years of accounting sins are confessed in one place - prior-period errors, reversed provisions, reclassifications between operating and other income, effects of qualifications. Large positive adjustments to the pre-IPO years' profits = the as-filed history is better than what the auditor originally signed. Also compare the restated numbers with the RoC/MCA filings (AOC-4) of the same years - divergence beyond the disclosed adjustments is a red flag.
- Auditor quality gates: peer-review certificate mandatory; if a new auditor without predecessor peer-review cover signs, prior-year RE-AUDIT is triggered ((11)(I)(A)(i)(e)) - an auditor CHANGE in the run-up to the DRHP is therefore visible and should be probed.
- Other financial information (11)(I)(B): EPS, RoNW, NAV/share, EBITDA computed per ICAI guidance note. Material acquisitions from proceeds (>= 20% of turnover/net worth/PBT): 3-year audited financials of the target must be included; voluntary proforma financials permitted (certified, per 2025 insertion).
- Separate (standalone) audited financials of issuer and material subsidiaries for 3 years: hosted on the website with links in the offer document ((11)(I)(A)(ii)); materiality = 10% of turnover/net worth/PBT; unaudited content in CFS capped at 20%.

## I.2 Related party transactions
- (11)(I)(A)(i)(g): ALL related parties and ALL RPTs of the consolidated entities (whether or not eliminated on consolidation) requiring disclosure under Ind AS 24 and/or s.188(2) Companies Act 2013, as per the SEPARATE financial statements of each consolidated entity, must be disclosed in the restated financial information - including all inter-se funding arrangements and guarantees, their terms, and fund-transfer restrictions.
- Until 20-Mar-2026 the "Offer Document Summary" section item (K) carried a 3-year RPT summary with cross-reference; that whole summary section was omitted w.e.f. 21-Mar-2026 (ICDR Amendment Regulations 2026) - for newer filings the RPT detail sits only in the restated financials note.
- Forensic angle: because eliminated intra-group RPTs must still be shown, you can see promoter-entity rent, remuneration, loans, guarantees and purchase/sale flows that a listed company's consolidated annual report nets away. Compute RPT revenue and RPT purchases as % of totals per year; trend into the IPO year matters (cleanup vs escalation).

## I.3 Outstanding Litigation and Material Developments - Sch VI Part A (12)(A)
Must be disclosed for the ISSUER, its DIRECTORS, PROMOTERS and SUBSIDIARIES:
- ALL criminal proceedings; ALL actions by regulatory/statutory authorities; disciplinary action incl. penalties by SEBI/exchanges against promoters in the LAST 5 FINANCIAL YEARS incl. outstanding actions; tax claims (direct + indirect) consolidated by number and total amount.
- Other pending litigation: threshold substituted w.e.f. 08-Mar-2025 - disclose per the board-approved materiality policy OR where value/expected impact exceeds the LOWER of: (a) 2% of turnover (latest restated annual consolidated), (b) 2% of net worth (unless negative), (c) 5% of average absolute PAT/loss of the last 3 restated years - whichever set gives the LOWER threshold ("lower of threshold criteria").
- (1A) (inserted 08-Mar-2025): all criminal proceedings and regulatory actions against KMP and SENIOR MANAGEMENT.
- Group companies: only litigation with MATERIAL IMPACT ON THE ISSUER (Sch VI (13)(D)).
- Creditors incl. MSME dues; material creditor details on the company website ((12)(A)(2)).
- Post-filing developments must be incorporated; explicit negative statements required if nil ((12)(A)(3)). Wilful defaulter / fraudulent borrower status goes ON THE COVER PAGE ((12)(A)(5)).
- Analyst use: the issuer's own materiality policy choice is informative - a threshold set high relative to the 2%/2%/5% floor buries mid-size cases; recompute with the regulatory floor.

## I.4 Risk Factors - Sch VI Part A (5)
- Ordering requirement: risk factors SHALL BE DISCLOSED IN DESCENDING ORDER OF MATERIALITY ((5)(F)); classified internal vs external ((5)(B)); quantified financial implications wherever quantifiable, else an explicit statement that they cannot be quantified ((5)(F)).
- Mandatory specific risk factors where applicable ((5)(G)) - the genuinely company-specific ones to mine: pending material clearances; issuances in the last 12 months at a price below the issue price; unidentified acquisition targets; plant and machinery not yet ordered (with % of total); promoters' lack of industry experience; losses in last 3 years; customer/supplier concentration; refusal of listing anywhere; unsecured loans repayable on demand; shortfall vs objects in previous issues of issuer AND listed group companies; issue proceeds flowing to promoter/group entities; conflicts of interest; NEGATIVE OPERATING CASH FLOW in the last 3 years; absence of a monitoring agency; land for objects not yet acquired; restrictive covenants.
- Reading method: the first 5-10 risk factors are, by regulation, the issuer's own materiality ranking - treat the ordering itself as a disclosure. Boilerplate (macro, pandemic, litigation-generally, competition-generally) clusters at the back; anything quantified, dated, or naming counterparties is real. Diff DRHP risk factors vs RHP risk factors: additions are usually SEBI-forced (see file J).
- Pre-21-Mar-2026 filings: the summary section carried contingent-liability and RPT condensates with cross-references; from 21-Mar-2026 use the draft abridged prospectus (top-10 risk factors, auditor-qualification cross-reference, litigation summary table) as the condensate instead.

## I.5 Group companies and promoter group - Sch VI Part A (13) - IMPORTANT DILUTION
- Current rule (substituted by Third Amendment 2021 w.e.f. 13-Aug-2021): offer document discloses only NAMES AND REGISTERED OFFICES of ALL group companies; for the TOP 5 group companies (by market cap if listed, turnover if unlisted), 3 years of key financials (reserves, sales, PAT, EPS, diluted EPS, NAV) must be hosted on the RESPECTIVE GROUP COMPANY'S WEBSITE, with the offer document giving the link. Plus: group-company litigation material to the issuer; and "Common Pursuits" - conflict-of-interest disclosure, related business transactions and their significance, quantification of business interests group companies have in the issuer (negative statement if nil).
- The PRE-2021 requirements to disclose loss-making group companies, sick/wound-up companies, and defunct companies struck off in the last 5 years were OMITTED w.e.f. 13-Aug-2021 (footnotes 722-725). There is NO current requirement to disclose loss-making group companies or negative-net-worth group companies in the offer document itself - the analyst must fetch the top-5 website disclosures AND pull MCA filings for the rest of the promoter group. Treat this as a known blind spot of post-2021 DRHPs.
- Promoter disclosures elsewhere: promoter biographies, other ventures, interests in the issuer (property, remuneration), companies from which promoters disassociated in last 3 years - in the "Our Promoters and Promoter Group" chapter (Sch VI (10) management/promoter items); financing arrangements by promoter group for purchases of issuer securities in the preceding 6 months (summary item (L)).

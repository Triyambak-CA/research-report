Read-Through From Peer Multiples

  • IDP's low multiple is a direct market signal that policy exposure and student-placement cyclicality are being penalised.
  • IndiaMART and Info Edge trade at higher platform multiples, but they are larger, better-followed and more diversified than Crizac.
  • Crizac's current 11.85x EV/EBITDA is below the blended peer median of 15.98x but above the depressed direct-peer IDP multiple.
  • Crizac's current 3.21x EV/Revenue is below the blended peer median of 4.84x.

Why Both EV/Revenue and EV/EBITDA Are Used

EV/Revenue captures platform scale and growth but ignores margin quality. EV/EBITDA is more relevant because Crizac already generates a 27.1% EBITDA margin and positive cash flow. The final value therefore gives slightly more weight to EV/EBITDA than EV/Revenue.

Selected CCM View

Selected fair-value band: Rs 245-280 per share. This range is close to the mean-to-median blended CCM output and is also supported by the IPO-price multiple reference after FY26 delivery.

The current price of Rs 199 implies Crizac is trading below the selected blended CCM range. The gap exists because the market appears to be applying a discount for UK concentration, conversion risk, policy risk and the absence of exact listed peers.

When To Discount Further

  • UK contribution remains above 90% for two more quarters.
  • Application growth remains much higher than enrolment growth.
  • Receivable days expand materially from the FY26 level.
  • FY27 guidance remains deferred without country/conversion assumptions.
  • Non-UK growth is acquisition-led but margins/cash conversion are not disclosed.

DCF Result

Implied value: Rs 148.46 per share (equity value Rs 2,599.0 cr; enterprise value Rs 2,404.7 cr). The current traded price of Rs 199 is about 34% above the DCF value; equivalently, the DCF sits 25% below the market.

Implied trailing multiples: the DCF enterprise value equals 8.5x FY26 EV/EBITDA and 2.31x FY26 EV/Revenue - versus the market's current 11.9x and 3.21x.

Character of this model: a deliberately conservative base case. The 24% forecast EBITDA margin is management's own stated sustainable band (24-25%, Q2/Q4 FY26 concalls) rather than the FX-aided FY26 print of 27.1%, and revenue tapers from 18% to 6% (11.9% seven-year CAGR) consistent with falling application-to-enrolment conversion and deferred FY27 guidance.

  • Checks sheet: all six workbook tie-outs pass; DCF, bridge and conclusion replicate independently to the paisa.
  • Terminal capex (2.0%) < D&A (2.5%): perpetuity inconsistency worth about Rs 2.5/share - fix for form.

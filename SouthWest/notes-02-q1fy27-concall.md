## CORRECTION TO PRIOR RESEARCH
Promoter family is JAIN, not Vasireddy. Vikas Jain = Chairman and MD. Piyush Jain = Joint MD.

## Company scale as stated on the call (Q1 FY27)
- Fleet: **43 drilling rigs** (screener Insights showed 40 at FY26 - the fleet grew in Q1)
- 165+ projects completed over 19 years; **20 ongoing projects across 8 states**
- **3.3 million metres drilled cumulatively, no lost-time injury**
- Services: coal and non-coal mineral exploration, aquifer mapping, CBM exploration and production drilling, 2D/3D seismic, passive seismic tomography, underground core drilling, coal business
- Clients named: Reliance Industries, Vedanta, Oil India, ONGC, CMPDI, CGWB, Hindustan Copper, Hindustan Zinc, Hindalco

## Q1 FY27 numbers (management's own words)
Revenue Rs 62 cr (+54% YoY); EBITDA Rs 15 cr (+157% YoY); EBITDA margin ~24.15%; PAT Rs 9.3 cr vs Rs 2.5 cr (+289%).

## ORDER BOOK - all-time high Rs 761 cr
- Largest-ever order: **Hindustan Zinc, Rs 307 cr, Rajasthan**, execution commenced in Q1 FY27, runs ~4 years. Won on relationship and on-ground execution track record, NOT a pure competitive bid ("private clients generally have a different mindset").
- **Reliance CBM contract extension, over Rs 166 cr**. Extension work had NOT started as of the call; expected to start in Q2 FY27. A further extension option exists that the client may exercise.
- CBM order book in the investor PPT shown as Rs 2,222 mn (Rs 222 cr) - management clarified this is from the PAST contract and is IN ADDITION to the Rs 166 cr extension.
- Oil India contract won 2024, Rs 60 cr, about Rs 23.8 cr still unexecuted, in final phase.
- Empanelled by **Oil India for 2D and 3D seismic data acquisition** on its onshore blocks (new segment entry).
- Aquifer mapping contracts about 65% complete; balance 35% after the monsoon.
- Order mix: about **77% private sector** (opening remarks) / "70-75% private, 25-30% government" (Q&A). Oil and gas about 25% of the book; other verticals each "ranging from 15% to 25%".
- Execution period: mixed - one contract runs 4 years, one or two run 2 years, some run 6 months.

### CONCENTRATION - THE SINGLE BIGGEST DISCLOSED RISK
Management, asked to split revenue between HZL and Reliance: "we cannot divulge this detail. This is confidential detail." Then conceded:
> "these two contracts almost cover about 60% of the total order book"
> "you can say that 60% of the revenues are coming from these two contracts only"
And separately, on Reliance alone for FY27: previous guidance of **35% to 40% of full-year revenue** was reaffirmed, "same line, (+/-5%)".
So: ~60% of revenue from two counterparties, and one of them (Reliance) is 35-40% on its own.

## THE THREE CAPITAL SINKS (the spine of the bear case)
1. **Jharkhand coal block** (awarded by Ministry of Coal). Exploration COMPLETE; geological report under preparation for submission to MoC; then mining plan approval, then environmental and forest clearance. **Phase-I capex about Rs 200 cr.** Revenue-sharing with government at **24.25% of revenue**. Production targeted **FY2028-29**.
2. **Oman JV 2 / AHML** - SWPEL holds **17.5%**; gold and copper exploration block, over 1,400 sq km; airborne survey done, ground geophysics next, then geological report. Revenue expected around **2030**. Investment quantum: "that figure has not come yet."
3. **Alara Resources (ASX-listed) rights issue** - SWPEL already holds ~1.25% directly; invested AUD 500,000 (about Rs 2.8 cr) in phase 1; can invest up to AUD 1 mn in phase 2.

Funding for all three, per management: "internal accruals, from the bank, the debt and also the outsource, the off-take contracts."
An analyst put the objection on the record: FY26 cash flows were "not much to serve the internal accruals" - management did not answer with numbers. Another analyst: "these two projects are too heavy for the size of our balance sheet and P&L today."
Context: FY26 CFO was Rs 12 cr. Market cap Rs 682 cr. Phase-I coal capex alone is Rs 200 cr.

## GOVERNANCE FLAG - Alara related-party overlap
Vikas Jain stated he is **personally a Director on the board of Alara Resources Ltd (Australia)** and holds a **personal stake** in it. The listed company (SWPEL) is simultaneously:
- buying equity in Alara (1.25%, increasing via the rights issue),
- in two JVs with Alara in Oman,
- earning mining-services revenue from mines **owned by Alara**.
His words: "that's a listed entity in Australia where I am also the Director in the company because of the personal stake as member of the board in Alara we hold."
This is a promoter-personal-interest overlap with a listed-company capital commitment. Check the FY26 related-party schedule and whether the Alara investment went through audit-committee/related-party approval.

## EARNINGS-QUALITY FLAG - JV profit brings in no cash
- Share of JV profit: **Rs 1.32 cr in Q1 FY27**; about Rs 1.5 cr for the whole of last year.
- Oman JV 1 (with Alara): SWPEL owns **35%**; USD 125 mn 10-year mining services contract; total JV profit "around INR 3.5 to 4 crores" last quarter. Equity-accounted, NOT consolidated line by line, so JV revenue never appears in SWPEL's topline but the profit share lifts PBT.
- Also 4 operating drilling rigs in Oman, booked for 2-3 years.
- Net margin on Oman mining services: **5% to 7%**; drilling services slightly higher.
- **Cash actually repatriated: NIL.** Dinesh Agarwal: "that money has yet to be repatriated as such." Vikas Jain: "The dividend stage has not come yet... will start coming from this year." SWPEL has also **given a LOAN to the JV** to stabilise it, and is recovering that loan first.
So JV profit is a non-cash contributor to PAT, and cash has flowed OUT to the JV (equity plus loan). This is part of why CFO/PAT is 0.36x.

## UTILISATION - growth is now partly bought-in
Asked for resource utilisation in the quarter: **"More than 100%."**
Reason given: "we have outsourced some of our work to other contractors as well" and "we have hired a few rigs also where our manpower is working." Purchase orders raised for more rigs.
Implication: incremental revenue is being delivered on subcontracted and hired capacity, which should carry lower margin than owned-rig work. Test whether the 24-27% OPM survives a higher outsourced mix.

## MANAGEMENT CALLS ITS OWN MARGIN CYCLICAL (unchanged from the Jul-2026 report)
> "our business is more cyclical, if you understand. Mining and exploration business is quite cyclical."
> "we have been conservative for the past few years. So, whatever we have told, we have achieved more."
Seasonality: H2 is structurally the strongest half; Q1 is subdued because of monsoon onset and because clients push work to finish by 31-March (which loads Q4).

## GUIDANCE GIVEN ON THIS CALL
- Medium-term revenue growth: **about 20% CAGR** (reaffirmed from the prior call).
- FY27: "significant growth" expected; hedged with "let's hope for the best."
- Order book: "it should go up and up. There is no way it should go down from here." Visibility claimed at "three to five years."
- Margin: asked if 24% is sustainable, answer was "the margin should be maintained because the entry barriers are quite high... The CAPEX cost is very high, and it is difficult for a new entrant."
- Debt: "if we don't buy additional equipments, we don't incur any CAPEX, we are getting towards being a debt-free company." Internally inconsistent with the POs already placed for more rigs and the Rs 200 cr coal-block plan.

## DISCLOSURE INCONSISTENCY CAUGHT ON THE CALL (high value)
An analyst noted the **investor presentation says coal production commences FY2027-28**, while management's verbal answer said **FY2028-29**. Management confirmed FY2028-29 is correct.
So the PPT in circulation overstates the timeline by a full year. Verify against the Q1 FY27 PPT and track how this date has drifted across successive calls.

## DEBT FIGURE INCONSISTENCY (check)
Dinesh Agarwal, asked for net debt as on 30-Jun-2026: "it is around INR 15 crores at the moment with a very good debt-equity of less than 0.39."
Rs 15 cr of debt against roughly Rs 204 cr of net worth is a D/E of about 0.07, not 0.39. Screener shows borrowings of Rs 80 cr at Mar-2026 and D/E 0.39. The Rs 15 cr is most likely net debt or one facility only. Reconcile against the Q1 FY27 balance sheet - the CFO quoting two mutually inconsistent numbers in one sentence is itself worth noting.

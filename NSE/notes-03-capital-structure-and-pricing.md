## 5. LOCK-IN DISCLOSURE (page 100)

### A. Minimum Promoters' Contribution

**Quoted verbatim (page 100, section 7(a))**:

> "Our Company does not have an identifiable promoter in terms of the SEBI ICDR Regulations and the Companies Act, 2013. Accordingly, in terms of Regulation 14(1) of the SEBI ICDR Regulations, there is no requirement of minimum promoter's contribution in this Offer and therefore, none of the Equity Shares will be locked in for a period of 18 months from the date of Allotment in terms of Regulation 14 of the SEBI ICDR Regulations, pursuant to the Offer."

### B. Lock-in of Pre-Offer Equity Shares - 6 Months

**Quoted verbatim (page 100, section 7(b)(i))**:

> "In terms of Regulation 17(1) of the SEBI ICDR Regulations, the entire pre-Offer Equity Share capital of our Company will be locked-in for a period of six months from the date of Allotment in the Offer. In the event where lock-in of such pre-Offer Equity Share capital of our Company cannot be created, the relevant Depositories, upon instructions from our Company, shall record such Equity Shares as 'non-transferable' for such duration of six months from the date of Allotment in the Offer, in terms of Regulation 17(2) of the SEBI ICDR Regulations."

**Exceptions (page 100, section 7(b)(i))**:
- Offered Shares successfully transferred as part of the Offer for Sale
- Shareholders registered as VCF, category I AIFs, category II AIFs or FVCIs - subject to conditions in Regulation 17(1), but must remain locked for at least 6 months from their purchase date

### C. Anchor Investor Lock-in

**From page 100, section 7(c)**:
- 50% locked for 90 days from allotment
- Remaining 50% locked for 30 days from allotment

- Market exigency requirements

- Cash required for contingencies/unforeseen events

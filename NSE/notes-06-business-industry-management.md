## SECTION 3: KEY REGULATIONS AND POLICIES (DRHP printed pages 213-221)

### SECC Regulations - Shareholding Limits (Pages 126-137)

**Public Shareholding Requirement:**
- Minimum 51% of paid-up equity share capital must be held by public

**Shareholding Caps - Resident Individuals:**
- Maximum 5% for individual residents (page 129)
- Exception: Certain institutional investors may hold up to 15% (page 129)

**Shareholding Caps - Non-Residents:**
- Maximum 5% for persons resident outside India (page 129)
- Combined holding of all non-residents: Not to exceed 49% of total paid-up equity share capital at any time (page 132)

**Fit and Proper Criteria:**
- Shareholders holding 2% or more must meet fit and proper criteria (page 134)
- Applicants seeking to acquire more than 5% must obtain prior SEBI approval (page 136)

**Net Worth Requirements:**
- Minimum net worth for stock exchanges: ₹100 crores at all times (page 127)

### Restrictions on Exchange Listing Its Own Securities (Page 54)

The SECC Regulations do not explicitly restrict an exchange from listing its own securities. However, per DRHP context, BSE is NSE's chosen listing venue (implied in Section 3 context).

### Profit and Fee Transfer Obligations (Page 148-155)

**Mandatory Profit Contributions:**
- Core Settlement Guarantee Fund contribution (page 152)
- Investor Protection Fund contribution

**Key Regulatory Compliance:**
- Stock exchanges shall not distribute profits unless minimum net-worth requirements are satisfied (page 152-153)

### Fee Regulation Caps

No explicit cap mentioned in the DRHP. However, SEBI has issued "Charges levied by Market Infrastructure Institutions - True to Label" circular dated July 1, 2024 (page 54).

### Recognition and Regulatory Framework

**SEBI Authority:**
- SEBI can withdraw recognition of a stock exchange (page 68)
- SEBI can suspend business for periods not exceeding 7 days, extendable if needed (page 74-76)
- SEBI empowered to call for information, inspections, enquiries and audits (page 34-35)

**Board Composition (SECC Regulations):**
- Number of public interest directors >= number of non-independent directors (page 118)
- Votes by public interest directors must equal or exceed non-independent directors (page 118)
- Public interest directors appointed for 3-year term, extendable for another 3 years (page 124)
- Managing director and executive directors classified as non-independent directors (page 9-10)

### Director Appointment Process (Page 118-124, 179-187)

**Public Interest Directors:**
- Appointed for initial 3-year term, extendable for another 3 years subject to SEBI performance review (page 124)
- SEBI approval required for appointments (page 119)
- Minimum of two names submitted as recommendations for each vacancy, two months in advance (page 119)
- Number of public interest directors must not be less than number of non-independent directors (page 118)
- Chairperson must be selected from public interest directors (page 10)

**Non-Independent Directors:**
- Include Managing Director and Executive Directors (page 9-10)
- Liable to retire by rotation (except MD/EDs) (page 65-66, 194)

**Executive Directors (Fourth Amendment to SECC Regulations, November 22, 2025, effective December 22, 2025):**
- Two executive directors required as KMP to head Vertical 1 (critical operations) and Vertical 2 (regulatory, compliance, risk, investor grievances) (page 180-181)
- Optional third ED to head Vertical 3 (other functions) (page 181-182)
- Cannot serve on board of other companies except subsidiaries of the exchange (with prior board approval) (page 185-187)

**SEBI Role:**
- SEBI approval required for public interest director appointments (page 119, 447)
- SEBI compliance required for fit and proper criteria verification (page 140)

"The Settlement applications are pending for final disposal with SEBI, the future outcome of which is uncertain at this stage."

The future outcome of the above matters is uncertain at this stage."

**DRHP Auditor's Emphasis of Matter (p.39, Auditors' Report):**
"We draw attention to Note 36(b) to the Consolidated Financial Statements relating to the orders passed by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) in relation to preferential access to tick-by-tick data at the Holding Company's Colocation facility, Dark Fiber point-to-point connectivity and governance and conflict of interest matters and the adjudication orders in relation to these matters. The Holding Company has recognised a provision of Rs. 13,912.07 million during the year ended March 31, 2026, in addition to an amount of Rs. 1,000 million provided for earlier as mentioned in Note 36(b) to the Consolidated Financial Statements. The future outcome of the above matters is uncertain at this stage."

### RHP Statement (p.439-462 Outstanding Litigation section)

"In relation to Colocation and Dark Fibre, SEBI issued show cause notices to us on May 22, 2017, and July 3, 2018, together with a summons. We preferred appeals before the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) and subsequently before the Court of India against the SAT Orders. However, on June 20, 2025, we filed settlement applications in respect of the Colocation and Dark Fibre matter with SEBI and have filed revised settlement terms on March 13, 2026. SEBI by way of its email dated July 30, 2026 has in principle agreed to accept the terms of the settlement and has made a demand of Rs. 7,147.37 million, in addition to the deposit of Rs. 7,764.70 million made by NSE with SEBI, which will be adjusted towards the settlement amount of Rs. 14,912.07 million.

The Board of Directors, in its meeting held on July 30, 2026, has approved the payment of the said amount of Rs. 7,147.37 million pursuant to the terms of the settlement. Pursuant to approval of the Board of Directors, NSE has made the payment of Rs. 7,147.37 million to SEBI on July 31, 2026. NSE and SEBI had filed interlocutory applications dated August 17, 2026 and August 18, 2026 before the Supreme Court, respectively, to seek, among others, the disposal of the SC Colocation Appeals and SC Dark Fibre Appeals.

On September 3, 2026, the Supreme Court had disposed off the SC Dark Fibre Appeals. The applications for disposal of the SC Colocation Appeals are pending for hearing with Supreme Court."

### Supreme Court Order dated 3-September-2026

The RHP discloses that on 3-September-2026, the Supreme Court disposed of the SC Dark Fibre Appeals. The colocation appeals remain pending for hearing. This was announced via interlocutory applications filed by NSE and SEBI on 17-Aug-2026 and 18-Aug-2026 respectively.

### Contingent Liabilities Schedule

Both documents disclose the provision of Rs. 13,912.07 million under contingent liabilities. The RHP adds clarity on the deposits (Rs. 7,764.70 million) and the payment made (Rs. 7,147.37 million on 31-Jul-2026) which net to the provision recognized.

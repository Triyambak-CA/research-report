## Section 4: Settlement and Litigation - Co-location and Dark Fibre

### SEBI Settlement Orders Passed (RHP p.32-33)

The RHP discloses a summary table of "settlement orders passed by SEBI against us" covering the three months ended 30-Jun-2026 and FY24, FY25, FY26. Four orders have been passed and amounts paid:

1. **Date: 31-Jul-2025** | **Amount paid: Rs 403.50 million** | Matter: Inspection and quasi-judicial proceedings (February 1, 2021 - March 31, 2022); violations of SECC Regulations, SEBI PIT Regulations, SEBI circulars; outsourcing data storage, unauthorized distribution of confidential information, deficiencies in due diligence. Pursuant to settlement application, SEBI passed the settlement order.

2. **Date: 29-Oct-2024** | **Amount paid: Rs 271.31 million** | Matter: NSE Clearing Limited; examination under interoperability framework; failure to maintain Inter-CCP collateral from BSE Clearing Limited. Pursuant to settlement application and revised settlement terms filed by NSE Clearing Limited, SEBI passed the settlement order.

3. **Date: 4-Oct-2024** | **Amount paid: Rs 6,430.53 million** | Matter: Trading Access Point (TAP) Architecture and Network Connectivity; failure to take remedial measures against TAP bypass; delay in appointment of Chief Information Security Officer; non-implementation of encryption in TAP; violations of SEBI PFUTP Regulations, SECC Regulations, and SEBI circulars. Pursuant to settlement application filed by Noticees, SEBI passed the settlement order.

4. **Date: 20-Jun-2023 and 28-Jun-2023** | **Amount paid: Rs 726.48 million** (Rs 497.68 million by NSE; Rs 228.80 million by NSE Clearing Limited) | Matter: Trading halt and system failures upon occurrence of glitch on 24-Feb-2021; impact on Storage Area Network and risk management, clearing, settlement, index, and surveillance systems. Pursuant to settlement applications filed by Noticees, SEBI passed the settlement orders.

### Co-location and Dark Fibre Settlement - Status as of RHP Date

**RHP p.33 (Risk Factors):**
"In relation to Colocation and Dark Fibre, SEBI issued show cause notices to us on May 22, 2017, and July 3, 2018, together with a supplementary show cause notice on July 31, 2018. Following our detailed responses, the Whole Time Member (WTM) of SEBI and the Adjudication Officer (AO) passed orders imposing monetary and non-monetary penalties on our Company. These orders were challenged by us before the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT), which partially allowed the order passed by WTM and reversed the order passed by the AO (SAT Orders). Subsequently, SEBI filed an appeal before the Supreme Court of India against the SAT Orders. However, on June 20, 2025, we filed settlement applications in respect of the Colocation and Dark Fibre matter with SEBI and have filed revised settlement terms on March 13, 2026. SEBI by way of its email dated July 30, 2026, has given an in-principle approval to accept the settlement terms, subject to Regulations 28 and 31 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018 and has advised to make a payment of Rs 7,147.37 million in addition to the deposit of Rs 7,764.70 million made by our Company with SEBI which will be adjusted towards the settlement amount of Rs 14,912.07 million. Thereafter, on July 31, 2026, our Company has made a payment of Rs 7,147.37 million."

**RHP p.33 (Risk Factors, contd.):**
"Subsequently, our Company and SEBI filed interlocutory applications dated August 17, 2026 and August 18, 2026, respectively, to sought disposal of the appeals filed before the Supreme Court of India. On September 3, 2026, the Supreme Court of India has disposed off the appeals filed by SEBI in the Dark Fibre matter and the hearing of the interlocutory applications for disposal off the appeals filed by SEBI in the Colocation matters are pending for hearing."

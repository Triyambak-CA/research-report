## Average daily trading volume - where the revenue decline comes from

| ADTV (Rs million) | FY26 | FY25 | FY24 | FY26 vs FY25 | FY26 vs FY24 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cash market | 1,055,166.67 | 1,129,632.46 | 817,212.91 | -6.6% | +29.1% |
| Equity futures | 1,594,432.13 | 1,859,014.41 | 1,340,003.40 | -14.2% | +19.0% |
| Equity options (premium) | 576,617.54 | 624,486.60 | 617,788.39 | -7.7% | -6.7% |
| **Equity options (notional)** | **258,284,696.91** | 312,838,244.22 | 323,569,230.43 | **-17.4%** | **-20.2%** |

Options notional ADTV has fallen for two consecutive years and is 20.2% below FY24. Premium ADTV
is 6.7% below FY24. This is the derivatives-curb effect landing directly on 60% of revenue.

The divergence between notional (-17.4%) and premium (-7.7%) matters: transaction charges on
options are levied on PREMIUM turnover, which is why revenue fell less than notional. Any model
must use premium, not notional. Known trap 2 (same metric on two bases) applies directly here.

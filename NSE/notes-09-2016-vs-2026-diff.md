**Verbatim finding from 2026 DRHP (drhp.txt line 69):**

"This being the **first public offer** by our Company, there has been no formal market for the Equity Shares of our Company."

This language treats the 2026 offer as if it is the first-ever public offer, with NO mention of the 2016 DRHP filing that lapsed/was withdrawn.

### Conclusion

**The 2026 DRHP is completely SILENT on the 2016 filing.** There is no reference to a previous draft red herring prospectus, no acknowledgment that this is a second attempt, and no mention of why or when the 2016 filing lapsed. The 2026 document presents the 2026 offer as "the first public offer", effectively erasing the 2016 attempt from the public record disclosed to new investors.

This silence is notable because:
1. Investors in the 2026 offer receive NO information about the earlier failed attempt or the 10-year gap
2. No explanation for why the 2016 listing did not proceed
3. No restatement of 2016 positions or challenges that may have intervened

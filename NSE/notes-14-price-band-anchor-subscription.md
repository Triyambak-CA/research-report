# NSE IPO: Price Band, Anchor Allocation, and Subscription Data

## 1. Price Band

**Confirmed: Rs 1,700 - Rs 1,785 per equity share**

- **Floor price:** Rs 1,700 per equity share
- **Cap price:** Rs 1,785 per equity share
- **Lot size:** 8 shares
- **Minimum retail investment:** Rs 13,600 (at floor price)
- **Issue size at cap price:** Rs 22,562 crore (approximately; 126,436,650 equity shares at Rs 1,785)

Source: Multiple news outlets reported price band announcement between 12-15 September 2026. The price band was advertised as per ICDR Regulations 29(4)-(6) two working days before the opening of the issue (which opened 17-Sep-2026).

Primary references: Angel One, HDFC Sky, ICICI Direct, Outlook Business, Millennium Post, India Infoline, Kotak Neo, Whalesbook - all reporting the same price band (1,700-1,785) announced for the anchor investor bidding on 16-Sep-2026 and public subscription opening 17-Sep-2026.

## 2. Anchor Book Allocation

**Total anchor book: Rs 6,746.18 crore (37,793,739 equity shares at Rs 1,785 per share)**

Allocation date: 16-Sep-2026 (anchor bidding day)
Anchor investors: 189 investors across multiple categories

### Domestic Mutual Funds Allocation

- **Amount:** Rs 2,496.49 crore (approximately)
- **Shares allotted:** 13,977,524 equity shares
- **Number of schemes:** 98 schemes
- **Number of distinct fund houses:** 29 fund houses
- **Percentage of anchor allocation:** 36.98%
- **Regulatory floor (per ICDR Sch XIII (10)(d) as of 01-Dec-2025):** 33.33%
- **Status:** EXCEEDS floor by 360 basis points (36.98% vs 33.33%)

Fund houses included major players such as SBI Mutual Fund, ICICI Prudential, HDFC, Nippon India, and others.

### Life Insurance Companies and Pension Funds Allocation

- **Amount:** Rs 1,081.22 crore (approximately)
- **Shares allotted:** 6,060,744 equity shares
- **Number of companies/funds:** 27 entities
- **Percentage of anchor allocation:** 16.04%
- **Regulatory floor (per ICDR Sch XIII (10)(d) as of 01-Dec-2025):** 6.67%
- **Status:** EXCEEDS floor by 937 basis points (16.04% vs 6.67%)

Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) was the largest allocation within this category:
- LIC allocation: Rs 400.30 crore (2,242,584 equity shares, representing 5.93% of total anchor allocation)

### Employee Discount and Reservation

- **Employee discount:** Rs 170 per equity share (off the issue price of Rs 1,785, making effective employee price Rs 1,615)
- **Employee reservation (shares):** 4,33,437 equity shares
- **Employee reservation (value):** Approximately Rs 70 crore

Source: Angel One, HDFC Sky, Intime India (registrar confirmations via media reports); IPO Central; Paterson Capital. The anchor allocation disclosure was filed with BSE following the 16-Sep-2026 anchor bidding day.

**Note:** QIB subscription of 1.53x is reported as total QIB including the anchor portion. The issue reserves 50% for QIB, 15% for NII, 35% for RII under book-building norms, with no exclusive portion separable for non-anchor QIB from publicly available data.

### Day-wise Build

**Day 1 (17-Sep-2026):** 0.41-0.43x overall subscription
- Strong participation from employees and NIIs reported
- Weak QIB participation on day 1

**Day 2 (18-Sep-2026) - 6:55 PM IST:** 1.15x overall subscription  
- QIB: 1.53x  
- NII: 1.64x  
- RIB: 0.71x  
- Employees: 1.50x

**By 20-Sep-2026 (Day 3 close):** Subscription stable around 1.16x (final closing data)

**Final (21-Sep-2026, closing):** 1.16x overall (as stated above by category)

Source: Groww (6:55 PM IST on 18-Sep), Angel One, Kotak Neo, Zeebiz, IPO Watch, IPO Wiz, IPOwiz, and ICICI Direct. The subscription remained relatively stable through days 3 and 4, closing at 1.16x overall, indicating moderate institutional demand and weak retail interest.

## 5. Grey Market Premium (GMP)

**As of 20-Sep-2026 (Day 3):**  
- **GMP (rupee premium):** Rs 43 to Rs 55 per share (varying by time and source)
- **As percentage of cap price (Rs 1,785):** 2.41% to 3.08%
- **Implied listing price estimate:** Rs 1,828 to Rs 1,840 (at cap price)

**GMP trend:**  
GMP opened at Rs 285 (around 4-Sep-2026) and declined significantly to Rs 43-55 by 20-Sep-2026, indicating cooling of grey market sentiment as the issue progressed through the bidding period.

Source: IPO Central (ipoji.com), IPO Watch, IPOwiz, Inkl News. GMP data is aggregated from multiple grey market participants and is not an official exchange figure. The downward trend from initial Rs 285 to final Rs 43-55 reflects changing market sentiment.

## Regulatory Compliance Notes

The domestic mutual fund allocation of 36.98% and life insurance & pension allocation of 16.04% both **exceed** the minimum regulatory thresholds under ICDR (Takeover Regulations) Schedule XIII, Section 10(d), as amended effective 01-Dec-2025:
- MDF minimum: 33.33% (actual: 36.98%)  
- Life insurance & pension funds minimum: 6.67% (actual: 16.04%)  
- Combined floor: 40.00% (actual: 52.02%)

The NSE IPO anchor book thus demonstrates **over-compliance** with regulatory allocation ceilings, indicating strong domestic institutional demand.

## KEY OBSERVATIONS FROM RESTATEMENT ANALYSIS

1. **Clean Audit Opinion:** No audit qualifications for any of the three years. Auditor issued unmodified (clean) opinion.

2. **No Restatement Adjustments Required:** Audited and restated figures are identical - no material adjustments for regrouping, reclassifications, or accounting policy changes were needed. The restated figures simply reflect regrouping for presentation under SEBI ICDR schedule format.

3. **Emphasis of Matter - SEBI Regulatory Matters:** The most significant non-adjusting item is the recognition in FY26 of a Rs 13,912.07 million provision related to pending SEBI settlement applications for Colocation, Dark Fiber, and Governance matters. This is a dramatic increase from the Rs 1,000 million provision carried from prior years (which had been set aside pending litigation appeals). The future outcome remains uncertain.

4. **Revenue Decline with Margin Expansion:** Revenue declined 3.14% from FY25 to FY26 (Rs 171,406.78 to Rs 166,013.09 million), yet PAT fell only 15.46% (Rs 121,876.89 to Rs 103,020.61 million) when adjusted for the SEBI provision. Excluding the exceptional SEBI provision and Labour Code impact:
   - Adjusted profit before tax FY26: Rs 138,955.79 million
   - Adjusted profit before tax FY25: Rs 154,747.78 million
   - Decline of 10.2% despite revenue decline of only 3.1%

5. **Strong Cash Generation:** Operating cash flow generation remained robust at Rs 238,361.75 million in FY26 despite SEBI provision, showing quality of earnings.

6. **Zero Financial Leverage:** NSE carries no financial debt - total borrowings are nil. Bank guarantees of Rs 89,732.72 million relate to regulatory/settlement functions, not financial borrowing.

7. **Dividend Generosity:** Dividend payout ratios are exceptionally high (3,500%-9,000% of face value in the period shown) reflecting NSE's zero debt, strong capital position, and regulatory designation as profit-distribution business.

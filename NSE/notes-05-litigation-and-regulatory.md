## 1. MATERIALITY POLICY FOR LITIGATION DISCLOSURE

**Policy Statement** (DRHP Printed Page 390)

The Governing Board adopted a materiality policy for disclosing outstanding litigation on 08-Jun-2026. Per Paragraph 12 of Part A of Schedule VI of the SEBI ICDR Regulations, litigation (other than criminal proceedings, regulatory actions, and statutory actions) is considered material if:

**A. By Value Threshold:**
The value or expected impact exceeds the LOWER of:
- 2% of turnover for FY 2026: Rs 3,320.26 million
- 2% of net worth for FY 2026: Rs 6,373.94 million  
- 5% of average profit/loss after tax for FY 2024-2026: Rs 5,132.58 million

**MATERIALITY THRESHOLD: Rs 3,320.26 million** (DRHP Printed Page 390, lines 274-286)

**B. By Cumulative Effect:**
Litigation where decision impacts similar ongoing litigation and cumulative value exceeds the Materiality Threshold

**C. By Adverse Effect:**
Outstanding litigation not meeting the threshold but where adverse outcome would materially affect business, prospects, operations, performance, financial position or reputation

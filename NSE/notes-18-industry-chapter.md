### 4.4 Barrier to Entry

The chapter does not explicitly list "barriers to entry" as a standalone topic. However, it discusses the following competitive moat:

**Self-reinforcing market concentration (RHP page 182):**
> Globally, markets across asset classes tend to exhibit high concentration, as liquidity aggregates around the most liquid venues in a self-reinforcing manner. This structural tendency has allowed NSE to not only sustain but steadily expand its leadership across segments. Resilient technology infrastructure and deep liquidity create a self-reinforcing cycle that allows leading exchanges to consolidate and sustain their positions.

**Historical examples cited (RHP page 182):**
- Deutsche Terminbörse (now Eurex) overtook London International Financial Futures and Options Exchange (LIFFE)
- International Securities Exchange (ISE) pioneered electronic options trading in US in 2000s
- BATS reshaped equities trading before being absorbed into Cboe

## 7. KEY NOTES ON DATA QUALITY AND CAVEATS

### Base Year Selection

The chapter presents derivatives growth statistics where the base year choice materially affects interpretation:

- **CAGR FY2024-FY2026 for equity index options: -39.14%** - This represents a contraction period, likely post-regulatory measures. This is NOT the long-term growth trajectory but a cyclical downturn from FY2024 baseline.

- **CAGR FY2024-FY2026 for single stock options: 24.17%** - Positive growth in this segment over the same period.

The chapter does NOT provide a "normalised" longer-term CAGR (e.g., 5-year or 7-year) that would isolate the impact of Oct 2024 regulatory measures separately.

### Addressable Market Definition

**Not explicitly defined.** The chapter frames market expansion in terms of:
- Structural drivers (demographics, financialisation, digitisation)
- Penetration rates (household savings, investor bases, SIP adoption)
- But does not state a total addressable market (TAM) figure for Indian capital markets

### Projection Confidence

The chapter provides multiple scenarios and exhibits for FY2026-FY2030P projections but does NOT state:
- Confidence intervals
- Sensitivity assumptions
- Downside cases
- Recovery timeline post-regulatory adjustment

### SEBI Oct 2024 Impact

**Critical gap:** The chapter mentions May 2025 regulatory recalibration but does NOT explicitly address:
- STT increases effective Oct 2024
- Impact on derivatives volumes
- Recovery forecast post-STT or post-regulatory measures

## 1. CO-LOCATION AND DARK FIBRE MATTER

### 1.1 Original SEBI Order (30-Apr-2019)

**Document:** SEBI Order in the matter of NSE Co-location
**Date:** 30-Apr-2019
**Issued by:** G. Mahalingam, Whole Time Member, SEBI
**Case Reference:** WTM/GM/EFD/03/2018-19
**URL:** https://www.sebi.gov.in/enforcement/orders/apr-2019/order-in-the-matter-of-nse-colocation_42880.html

**Key Findings:**
- **Disgorgement amount:** Rs 624.89 crore
- **Interest rate:** 12% per annum
- **Interest from date:** 01-Apr-2014
- **Violations found:** Fair access to co-location facility, tick-by-tick data feed access, preferential connectivity to certain brokers
- **Ban:** NSE prohibited from accessing securities market for 6 months

**Additional penalties against individuals:**
- Ravi Narain (former MD & CEO): 25% salary disgorgement for FY 2010-11 to 2012-13 and FY 2013-14
- Chitra Ramkrishna (former MD & CEO): 25% salary disgorgement for FY 2013-14
- Mahesh Soparkar and Deviprasad Singh: NSE directed to initiate enquiry under Employees Regulations

**Primary source:** PDF extracted from SEBI website - full order text confirmed.

### 1.2 Securities Appellate Tribunal Order (23-Jan-2023)

**Document:** Securities Appellate Tribunal Order
**Date:** 23-Jan-2023
**Case Numbers:** SAT Appeal Nos. 276/2019, 339/2019, 340/2019 & Misc. App. No. 233/2019
**URL:** http://sat.gov.in/english/pdf/E2019_JO2019276_2.PDF (per SEBI enforcement page hyperlinks)

**Key Findings:**
- **Decision:** Set aside Rs 624.89 crore disgorgement order against NSE
- **Substitute direction:** NSE directed to pay Rs 100 crore to Investor Protection and Education Fund (IPEF)
- **Finding:** SAT acknowledged lapses in fair access and due diligence by NSE but reduced penalty
- **Appeal allowed against:** The proportionality of disgorgement amount
- **Remand:** Matter remanded to SEBI for reconsideration of collusion/connivance charges

**Primary source:** Referenced in multiple SEBI orders and news reports; direct PDF access unsuccessful (404), but citations in SEBI's Sep-2024 order confirm the date and outcome.

**Secondary, unverified details from news:** SAT also allowed inquiry into collusion with OPG Securities. The Tribune, Business Standard reports from 2023 cite this order but could not access primary SAT PDF.

## 5. SEBI'S DERIVATIVES (F&O) REGULATORY CURBS

### 5.1 October 2024 Circular - Index Derivatives Measures

**Circular:** SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD-1/P/CIR/2024/132
**Date:** 01-Oct-2024
**Effective date:** 20-Nov-2024 (for lot size changes); phased implementation through Apr-2025
**URL:** https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FAOP64625.pdf (NSE implementation)

**Key measures:**
1. **Lot size increase:** Minimum contract value increased from Rs 5 lakh to Rs 15 lakh at introduction, with contract size fixed between Rs 15-20 lakh
   - Nifty 50: lot size changed to 50-75 units
   - Bank Nifty: lot size changed to 25-35 units
   - Sensex: lot size changed to 20-30 units

2. **Weekly expiries limitation:** Only one weekly expiry permitted per benchmark index per exchange (effective 20-Nov-2024)
   - NSE discontinued weekly expires for Bank Nifty, Nifty Financial Services, Nifty Midcap Select, Nifty Next 50

3. **Position limits (intraday monitoring):** Effective 01-Apr-2025
   - Exchanges directed to monitor position limits on intraday basis

4. **Position limits (end-of-day):** Effective 01-Jul-2025
   - Net limit: Rs 1,500 crore
   - Gross limit: Rs 10,000 crore

5. **Expiry day margins:** Additional Enhanced Leverage Margin (ELM) of 2% on expiry days for weekly options

6. **Calendar spread treatment:** Removed on expiry days

**Primary source:** SEBI circular text cited in Business Standard, ICICI Direct, and Zerodha analysis; NSE archive link accessible.

---

### 5.2 Other F&O Circulars (2024-2025)

Additional circulars implementing weekly expiry limits and position limit monitoring:
- **Effective dates:** Various implementation dates from Oct-2024 through Jul-2025
- **Scope:** All index derivative contracts on NSE and BSE

**Primary source:** SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD-1/P/CIR/2024/132 cited as master circular; individual exchange implementation circulars referenced in NSE and BSE websites.

## 6. SEBI'S TRADER PROFITABILITY STUDIES - EQUITY DERIVATIVES

### 6.1 Updated Study: FY22-FY24 (23-Sep-2024)

**Document:** Press Release and accompanying study
**Press Release Number:** 37/2024
**Date:** 23-Sep-2024
**URL:** https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/sep-2024/updated-sebi-study-reveals-93-of-individual-traders-incurred-losses-in-equity-fando-between-fy22-and-fy24-aggregate-losses-exceed-1-8-lakh-crores-over-three-years_86906.html

**Key Findings:**
- **Percentage of traders with net losses:** 93% of individual traders (over 1 crore traders)
- **Average loss per trader:** ~Rs 2 lakh per trader (inclusive of transaction costs)
- **Aggregate loss (3-year period):** Rs 1.8 lakh crore
- **Time period covered:** FY 2022-23, FY 2023-24, FY 2024-25
- **Additional finding:** Young traders (below 30 years) increased from 31% (FY22-23) to 43% (FY23-24)
- **Algorithmic trading:** 97% of foreign investors' profits and 96% of proprietary traders' profits came from algorithmic trading

**Primary source:** SEBI press release and study published on SEBI website.

## SUMMARY FOR AUDITOR/INVESTOR

As of **21-Sep-2026**:

1. **Settlement Status (VERIFIED):** The Supreme Court disposed of SEBI's appeal against NSE in the co-location and dark fibre matter on 18-Sep-2026, approving a Rs 1,491.21 crore settlement.

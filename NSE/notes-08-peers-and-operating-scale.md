Closest structural analogues to NSE, and why (my assessment from the above, not a third-party claim):
- **HKEX, B3 and JPX** - single dominant home-market gateway, vertically integrated across trading-clearing-
  settlement, and (for B3 in particular) a derivatives-heavy, retail-participation-heavy turnover mix similar
  to NSE's own SEBI-curbed F&O market.
- **CME** on revenue-mix grounds (overwhelmingly transaction/clearing fee driven) but NOT on market
  structure (CME has no cash-equities franchise and faces genuine multi-venue competition in the US,
  unlike NSE's ~93-100% domestic market share across most segments).
- **LSEG and Nasdaq are NOT close analogues** - both now derive the bulk of revenue from data, analytics,
  index licensing and software (Refinitiv, Adenza) rather than trading fees, and both operate in
  fragmented, multi-venue home markets. Useful only as a caution on where NSE's own index-licensing and
  data-services ambitions might lead, not as a valuation-multiple comparable.

### 3.3 Number of contracts traded and global ranking

PRIMARY-ADJACENT (NSE reproducing WFE/FIA data, DRHP page 163-165, "as on 30 May 2026" per the DRHP's own
data-currency note):
- NSE (including NSEIX, its GIFT City subsidiary) traded **more than 36.99 billion equity derivatives
  contracts in FY2026**, ranking FIRST globally by number of contracts traded, per World Federation of
  Exchanges data as reproduced in the DRHP.
- Of this, equity INDEX options alone accounted for **34.69 billion contracts** in FY2026.
- NSE's cash-equity number of trades: **7.95 billion in FY2026**, versus Nasdaq (5.93 bn) and ICE/NYSE
  (5.38 bn) per WFE data as reproduced in the DRHP - NSE states this makes it "among the top three
  exchanges" globally in cash-equity trade count "from Fiscal 2020-26" (WFE/FIA, per DRHP page 163).
- NSE's own qualitative claim (DRHP page 111): "global market share of 11.38% in number of trades in cash
  equities and 51.18% in contracts traded in equity derivatives" among "leading listed stock exchange
  groups globally" for FY2026, per World Federation of Exchanges - this is NSE's characterisation of WFE
  data, not an independent WFE publication I have separately verified.
- The DRHP itself flags the important caveat that contract-COUNT leadership does not equal value
  leadership: "a single lot of the S&P 500 (SPX) index option had a notional value of around US$652,852.00"
  as of Mar-2026, versus a far smaller Nifty lot value, making one SPX contract "~37 times larger... in
  value terms" than a Nifty contract (DRHP page 165, NSE's own commentary on the WFE/FIA data). In Q1
  CY2026, India's options premium turnover was "one fourth" of the US's despite "five times the contracts
  traded" (DRHP, same page).
- **I have NOT independently pulled the underlying FIA (Jan-2026 CY2025 volume release) or WFE 2025 Market
  Highlights report in this pass** - all global-ranking figures above are as reproduced and characterised
  by NSE in its own DRHP, not cross-checked against the FIA/WFE source documents directly. Treat the
  ranking claim as NSE's characterisation of third-party data, not independently established.

### 3.5 Participant-wise derivatives turnover (individual vs proprietary vs FPI)

PRIMARY-ADJACENT (DRHP Industry chapter, page 148, attributed to Redseer Report / NSE data):
- **Proprietary traders account for ~50.70% of NSE's equity options PREMIUM turnover**, driven by HFT
  algorithms and arbitrage strategies (DRHP, page 148).
- **Individual investors (incl. NRIs, sole proprietorships, HUFs) contribute 33.40% of total CASH MARKET
  turnover on NSE** as of FY2026, "broadly consistent over the past 10 years" (DRHP, page 156, attributed
  to NSE Market Pulse / SEBI data). Note this is a CASH MARKET figure, not a derivatives figure.
- **Algorithmic trading accounts for ~55% of cash market segment trades and ~66% of equity derivatives
  trades on NSE** (DRHP page 148).
- I did NOT locate an explicit NSE-disclosed split of DERIVATIVES turnover specifically among individual /
  proprietary / FPI in one single table in the sections reviewed - the 50.70% proprietary figure is for
  options premium turnover specifically, and the 33.40% individual figure is for cash market specifically.
  **A clean individual-vs-proprietary-vs-FPI breakdown for the DERIVATIVES segment specifically: nothing
  further verified in this pass** beyond the proprietary-share figure above.

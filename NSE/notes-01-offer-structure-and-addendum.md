Reg 6(1) route means the allocation split is retail at least 35%, non-institutional at least 15%,
QIB not more than 50%. It also means NSE CLEARED the Reg 6(1)(b) profitability test
(average operating profit at least Rs 15 crore over three years, positive each year). For this
issuer that bar is trivial, but the route still has to be stated and it is the opposite of the
6(2) fallback that signals a failed profit test. Known trap 10 cleared.

## What the Addendum changed (dated 10-Aug-2026, Mumbai, signed by the Company Secretary)

1. **A new selling shareholder was added.** By letter dated 10-Aug-2026, State Bank of India
   informed the company that its 24,750,000 offered shares will now be offered TOGETHER by SBI
   and SBI Capital Markets Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of SBI:
   - State Bank of India: up to 15,969,410 shares, WACA Rs 0.80
   - SBI Capital Markets Limited: up to 8,780,590 shares, WACA Rs 0.38
   Total unchanged at 24,750,000. Corporate approval dates 12-May-2026 (SBI) and 08-Aug-2026
   (SBI Caps); consent letters both 10-Aug-2026.

2. **SBI Capital Markets is simultaneously a book running lead manager and a selling
   shareholder.** The Addendum states that on account of the restrictions under Regulation 21A
   of the SEBI Merchant Bankers Regulations, SBI Capital Markets, in its capacity as a BRLM,
   "will be involved only in the marketing of the Offer".

3. **Two further BRLMs are conflicted the same way.** Footnote to the Addendum: Morgan Stanley
   India Company Private Limited and ICICI Securities Limited are associates of MS Strategic
   (Mauritius) Limited and ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited respectively, both
   Selling Shareholders, in terms of the SEBI Merchant Bankers Regulations. Under Reg 21A of
   those regulations and Reg 23(3) of SEBI ICDR, both "will be involved only in the marketing of
   the Offer". Both have signed the due diligence certificate and are disclosed as BRLMs.
   => THREE of the syndicate are restricted to marketing only. Section 23 finding.

4. **Bonus issue confirmed - and it distorts every cost-of-acquisition figure.**
   Board resolution 04-Nov-2024: bonus issue in the ratio **4:1** (four new shares for every one
   held), record date 02-Nov-2024. "Acquisition price of Equity Shares acquired pursuant to such
   bonus issue is NIL." SBI Capital Markets alone received 85,800,000 bonus shares at nil cost.
   Known trap: any WACA spanning Nov-2024 is pulled down by nil-cost bonus shares, and any
   pre-bonus price must be divided by 5 to compare with a post-bonus price.

5. **The cost-of-acquisition table** (amending "Capital Structure" at DRHP page 100).
   Weighted average cost of acquisition of all equity shares transacted by the Selling
   Shareholders and shareholders with director-nomination or other special rights:

| Period before the DRHP | WACA per share (Rs) | Cap price as multiple | Acquisition price range, low to high (Rs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Last 1 year | 1,909.02 | to be filled on price band | 1,350.00 to 2,260.00 |
| Last 18 months | 1,747.54 | to be filled on price band | 800.00 to 2,260.00 |
| Last 3 years | 205.86 | to be filled on price band | Nil to 5,150.00 |

   Certified by Manian & Rao, Chartered Accountants (FRN 001983S), certificate dated 10-Aug-2026.
   The Rs 205.86 three-year figure is an artefact of the nil-cost bonus shares, and the
   Rs 5,150 high is a pre-bonus price (Rs 1,030 on a post-bonus basis). The honest anchors are
   the 1-year Rs 1,909.02 and the 18-month Rs 1,747.54.
   **This is the single most useful pricing fact available pre-band: real money changed hands
   between Rs 1,350 and Rs 2,260 a share in the year to Jun-2026.**

## Consequences to carry forward
- Section 9 (use of proceeds): there are NO objects to audit. The company receives nothing.
  The section must say exactly that and then pivot to who does receive the money.
- Section 20 (supply calendar): with no identifiable promoter, Reg 14 (minimum promoters'
  contribution) and Reg 16 (promoter lock-in) do not apply. Pre-issue capital falls under
  Reg 17(1), six months from allotment, with AIF/VCF/FVCI running from their own purchase date.
  VERIFY this reading against the DRHP "Capital Structure" lock-in disclosure before publishing.
- Section 22 (draft-to-final diff): there is no RHP. Substitute DRHP-vs-Addendum (done above)
  and the lapsed 2016 DRHP vs the 2026 one.

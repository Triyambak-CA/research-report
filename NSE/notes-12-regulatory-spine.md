# Notes 12 - The regulatory spine: co-location, dark fibre, governance, and the settlement

ALL of the following is quoted or closely paraphrased from the DRHP itself (risk factor 40,
"Any increase in or realisation of our contingent liabilities could have a material adverse
effect on our financial condition", DRHP printed pages 49-51), and from note 28.2 and note 34(b)
to the Restated Consolidated Financial Information. This is primary source, not press.

## IMPORTANT CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF

The launch brief said "the SEBI settlement said to have been approved internally around
20 June 2026". The DRHP says something materially different, and the DRHP wins:

- **20-Jun-2025** (not 2026): NSE filed TWO settlement applications with SEBI under the SEBI
  (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations 2018 for a cumulative **Rs 13,873.90 million**, covering
  (a) the Colocation WTM Order and Colocation AO Order, and (b) the Dark Fibre WTM Order and
  Dark Fibre AO Order.
- **13-Mar-2026**: NSE filed REVISED settlement terms for a cumulative **Rs 14,912.07 million**.
- As at the DRHP date of 17-Jun-2026: "The Settlement applications are **pending for final
  disposal with SEBI, the future outcome of which is uncertain at this stage**."

So at the date of the offer document the settlement was NOT approved. It was pending, and the
company itself calls the outcome uncertain. Any claim that it was approved is unverified.
The Aug-2026 Addendum does not mention any settlement approval either.

## The full enforcement history, from the document

**The three show cause notices**: 22-May-2017, 03-Jul-2018 and 31-Jul-2018, against NSE and some
former employees, covering the colocation facility, Dark Fibre, and Governance and conflict of
interest.

**SEBI Whole-Time Member orders, all dated 30-Apr-2019:**
| Order | Direction |
|---|---|
| Colocation WTM Order | Disgorge Rs 6,248.90 mn plus interest at 12% pa from 01-Apr-2014 to payment, plus non-monetary and restrictive directions including a six-month bar on accessing the securities market |
| Dark Fibre WTM Order | Deposit Rs 625.80 mn plus interest at 12% pa from 11-Sep-2015 to payment, plus non-monetary and restrictive directions |
| Governance WTM Order | Non-monetary and remedial directions |

**SEBI Adjudicating Officer orders:**
| Order | Date | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| Colocation AO Order | 10-Feb-2021 | Rs 10 mn |
| Dark Fibre AO Order | 28-Jun-2022 | Rs 70 mn |
| Governance AO Order | 30-Jun-2022 | Rs 10 mn |

**Securities Appellate Tribunal - NSE won substantially on the money:**
| SAT order | Date | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Colocation WTM appeal | 23-Jan-2023 | Upheld the non-monetary directives, SET ASIDE the disgorgement, directed Rs 1,000 mn to the Investor Education and Protection Fund for lack of due diligence by NSE |
| Colocation AO appeal | 12-Jul-2023 | Set aside the Colocation AO Order |
| Dark Fibre WTM appeal | 09-Aug-2023 | Allowed the appeal to the extent that the disgorgement was reversed |
| Dark Fibre AO appeal | 14-Dec-2023 | Set aside the Dark Fibre AO Order |

**Supreme Court - SEBI appealed all four, and all four are still live:**
- SEBI appealed BOTH SAT orders in the Colocation matter to the Supreme Court. "Both the appeals
  are pending for final hearing and disposal."
- SEBI appealed BOTH SAT orders in the Dark Fibre matter to the Supreme Court. "Both the Appeals
  are pending for final hearing and disposal."
- Supreme Court interim order **20-Mar-2023**: directed SEBI to refund Rs 3,000 mn to NSE in the
  Colocation WTM appeal.
- Supreme Court interim order **17-Oct-2023**: directed SEBI to refund Rs 310 mn to NSE in the
  Dark Fibre WTM appeal.

**Governance strand - NSE gave up:**
NSE filed appeals against the Governance WTM and Governance AO orders, then **withdrew both on
24-Jul-2024**, and on 29-Jul-2024 paid the Rs 10 mn penalty with interest. It has updated SEBI
on compliance with the non-monetary directions. Withdrawing an appeal and paying is a concession
on the governance strand, and it is the one strand of the three that NSE did not contest to the end.

**The provision:**
"In the light of pending outcomes of the above settlement applications, the Board of Directors of
NSE have considered deferment of other legal proceedings in this regard. Consequently, NSE has
recognised a provision of **Rs 13,912.07 million**, and the same is included in other expenses
for year ended March 31, 2026. This is in addition to Rs 1,000.00 million imposed by the Hon'ble
SAT in the Colocation WTM Appeal which had been duly adjusted against the amount deposited by NSE
with SEBI during the year ended March 31, 2023. The said provision, after netting off deposit
lying with SEBI, is shown under current provision."

Balance sheet: "Settlement fees payable to SEBI (Refer note 34(b)) **7,147.37**" under
Provisions - Current at 31-Mar-2026 (nil in FY25 and FY24). So roughly half the provision is
netted against money already with SEBI and Rs 7,147.37 mn remains a current liability.

## SEBI settlement fees actually charged to profit, three years

| Rs million | FY26 | FY25 | FY24 |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEBI settlement fees in other expenses | **14,315.57** | **6,701.84** | **726.48** |
| Total other expenses | 37,900.64 | 26,249.21 | 17,282.62 |
| Settlement fees as % of other expenses | 37.8% | 25.5% | 4.2% |

Cumulative across the three restated years: **Rs 21,743.89 million, or Rs 2,174 crore.**

Composition per the MD&A:
- **FY26**: Rs 13,912.07 mn provision for the Colocation and Dark Fibre settlement applications,
  plus Rs 403.50 mn paid on a settlement application relating to a SEBI inspection of the company
  for Feb-2021 to Mar-2022. (Note 28.2(a): letter 12-Apr-2023, settlement application 10-Aug-2023,
  SEBI accepted by email 18-Jun-2025, paid 25-Jun-2025, settlement order passed 31-Jul-2025.)
- **FY25**: Rs 6,430.53 mn on the Trading Access Point (TAP) matter, plus Rs 271.31 mn paid by
  NSE Clearing on inter-CCP collateral non-compliance. (Note 28.2(b): revised settlement terms
  filed 14-Jun-2024, SEBI demand notice 20-Sep-2024, paid 25-Sep-2024, settlement order 04-Oct-2024.
  Note 28.2(c): NCL settlement order 29-Oct-2024.)
- **FY24**: Rs 497.68 mn by NSE and Rs 228.80 mn by NSE Clearing on settlement applications
  relating to the **technical glitch of 24-Feb-2021** (the day-long trading outage).

**This is the finding that matters most for earnings quality.** The company presents a
"Normalised Operating EBITDA Margin" of 76.23% for FY26 against a reported 66.85%, and the
largest single add-back is SEBI settlement fees. But settlement fees have now been charged in
every one of the three restated years, rising each year, on five separate matters (colocation,
dark fibre, TAP, inter-CCP collateral, the 2021 glitch, and a general inspection). A cost that
recurs in every year of the disclosed record is not obviously non-recurring, and normalising it
out is a presentational choice the report must name.

colocation WTM, colocation AO, dark fibre WTM, dark fibre AO, and the unrelated-investments AO.
In each, NSE won at SAT and SEBI appealed. NSE is holding four favourable tribunal outcomes that
the regulator is trying to overturn, while simultaneously trying to settle two of them for
Rs 14,912.07 million.

## The risk factor the company itself flags
Risk factor at DRHP page 27 (very near the top of a risk section that begins at page 24, and the
ordering is mandated to be descending by materiality):
"Our Company has been, and continues to be, subject to enforcement actions, penalties,
adjudication proceedings, which may or may not get settled with SEBI in respect of alleged
violations and non-compliance with applicable SEBI regulations. The outcome of current and
potential future proceedings is uncertain and could have a material adverse effect on our
business, reputation, financial condition, results of operations, cash flows and prospects."

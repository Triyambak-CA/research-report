## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY - WHAT MATTERS

1. **The user's "NSE - BE" observation is CONFIRMED and is the single most material finding.** MTARTECH is in NSE Long-Term ASM **Stage IV** with a **5% price band** and **Trade-for-Trade settlement** (NSE series **BE**). On BSE it has been in the **'T' group since 25-Jun-2026**. This is a surveillance flag, not a routine classification.
2. **Promoter holding has fallen from "over 50%" post-IPO (FY21) to 29.36% (Jun-2026)** - roughly halved in five years, and still falling in Sep-2026.
3. **Promoters sold persistently through the parabolic 2026 rally, into all-time-high prices** - at least 8 separate open-market sales between Feb-2026 and 16-Sep-2026, the last one five days before this report date.
4. **The MD owns only 3.88%** - less than two other promoter-group members. There is no single controlling shareholder; "promoter" is a dispersed extended-family bloc.
5. **Economics do not support the price.** FY25 PAT was Rs 52.89 crore on ~Rs 757 crore net worth (ROE ~7%). FY26 EPS of Rs 30.57 is still **below FY23's Rs 33.62**. The stock trades at ~229x FY26 EPS / 161x TTM.
6. **Free cash flow was negative every year FY21-FY25**, turning positive only in FY26. Debt rose from Rs 16.98 crore (FY21) to Rs 376.57 crore (FY26).
7. **Zero net equity dilution since FY22** - share count fixed at 3,07,59,600 for five straight years. No QIP, no preferential, no rights issue detected.
8. **Extreme single-customer concentration**: Bloom Energy = 55-65% of revenue. The Jun-2026 and Jul-2026 crashes were both Bloom-driven.

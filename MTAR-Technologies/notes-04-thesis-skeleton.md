# MTAR - Notes 04: Thesis skeleton (drafted from own work, pre-agent-report)

## ONE LINE
This is a bet on one American fuel-cell customer's AI-data-centre order book converting into
Indian hot boxes, priced as if MTAR compounds revenue at ~55-60% a year for a decade,
and it breaks if Bloom Energy's blanket purchase order does not become firm call-offs.

## TOP 3 POSITIVES
1. Genuine operating inflection. Quarterly revenue went 135.6 -> 278.0 -> 306.1 -> 360.7 cr
   in four quarters, with EBITDA margin back to 23.6% from 12.5%. This is real, reported and audited.
2. Sole-source position on a critical assembly. MTAR makes the hot box for Bloom's solid oxide
   fuel cells. Nine-year relationship, qualified tooling, and Bloom's own 1 GW -> 2 GW Fremont
   expansion is publicly described as dependent on MTAR's parallel 8,000 -> 12,000 unit expansion.
   Switching cost is real and the capex to serve it is small (~Rs 40 cr).
3. Order book 5,143 cr at 30-Jun-2026 is 5.9x FY26 revenue, and already above the Rs 5,000 cr
   target management had set for the END of FY27. Plus a genuine, dateable nuclear award
   (Rs 504 cr, Kaiga 5 and 6 fuel handling).

## TOP 3 RISKS
1. The record order book is one blanket PO from one undisclosed international customer
   (USD 238.76 mn / Rs 2,278.96 cr, Reg 30 filed 14-May-2026), with the execution timeline
   explicitly "to be determined at a later stage". A blanket PO is a call-off umbrella, not a
   scheduled contract. It is 44% of the entire order book.
2. Concentration went UP while management sold diversification. Clean energy fuel cell went
   from 51.2% to 66.7% of the order book in ONE quarter; aerospace and defence fell from 14.0%
   to 7.4%; nuclear from 26.3% to 13.3%.
3. Valuation. 162x trailing earnings, 20.3x EV/sales, 26x book, against a 5-year own-history
   P/E range of 47x-113x. The reverse DCF requires a 55-60% revenue CAGR for ten years on
   central assumptions. MTAR's realised incremental sales-to-capital is 0.61x.

## THE STRUCTURAL PROBLEM NOBODY IS MODELLING
MTAR is a capital-hungry business being valued like an asset-light one.
- Cash conversion cycle ~248 days (receivables 140, inventory 208, payables 100 at Q4 FY26)
- Cumulative free cash flow FY19 to FY26: NEGATIVE Rs 251 cr
- Every rupee of incremental revenue FY23->FY26 needed ~Rs 1.6 of capital
- Debt doubled in FY26 (Rs 177 cr -> Rs 377 cr, +112%)
If revenue really does 80% in FY27 (Rs 876 -> 1,577 cr), the working capital alone at a 248-day
cycle absorbs roughly Rs 475 cr of incremental capital. That must be funded. Either debt rises
again, or equity is raised, or the order book converts slower than guided. There is no fourth option.

## THE SURVEILLANCE FACT THE SELL-SIDE NOTES DO NOT MENTION
The stock sits in NSE's BE series and BSE's 'T' group (trade-to-trade, since 25-Jun-2026) under
long-term ASM Stage 4: 100% margin, no intraday, 5% price band. Monthly volume has gone
41.4 mn (Jun) -> 4.9 mn (Jul) -> 2.6 mn (Aug) -> 1.6 mn (Sep). That is a ~96% liquidity collapse.
Practical consequence: a holder who wants out in size cannot get out in size. Position sizing,
not the thesis, is the binding constraint here.

## THESIS-KILLERS (observable)
1. The blanket PO does not convert: quarterly clean-energy revenue flattens below ~Rs 250 cr
   for two consecutive quarters while the order book stays flat or falls.
2. Bloom Energy guides down, or announces in-house / alternate-supplier hot box capacity.
3. FY27 exit revenue run-rate below Rs 1,400 cr annualised (i.e. the 80% guidance missed by
   more than 10 points) - MTAR's margin guidance history says this is the live risk.
4. Equity raise at or below the prevailing price to fund working capital.
5. EBITDA margin back below 18% for two quarters - it was 12.5% as recently as Sep-2025.

## BASE RATE
Indian mid-cap engineering names that re-rate to 150x+ on a single-customer export ramp have
historically resolved by multiple compression rather than by earnings failure: the earnings
often arrive, and the stock still halves because the entry multiple did the work.
MTAR's own 2023 episode is the base rate: Sep-2023 peak Rs 2,920, trough Apr-2025 Rs 1,155.60,
a 60% drawdown over 19 months, during which revenue still GREW from Rs 574 cr to Rs 676 cr.
Growth was not the problem last time either.

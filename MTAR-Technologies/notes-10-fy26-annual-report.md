# notes-10 - what the FY26 annual report says, and what it corrects

The FY2026 annual report was downloaded from BSE on 22-Sep-2026 into source-docs/ and read in
full. The 21-Sep-2026 edition of the report could not open it, or any other filing, and said so.
Six of that edition's stated open items close here. Three of its findings need correcting.

Statutory auditor: **S.R. Batliboi & Associates LLP**, Chartered Accountants. Unqualified
opinion. One CARO observation: the audit-trail server backup was not kept in India for part of
the year, April to August 2025. The 21-Sep edition listed the auditor's identity as "not named
in any accessible source"; it is named here, and it is a Big Four network firm.

All figures below are consolidated unless marked standalone. The annual report is denominated
in Rs million; conversions to crore are shown.

---

## 1. CORRECTION - the forex gain is in other income, below EBITDA, and it is Rs 20.66 cr

This is the most important correction in this pass, and it goes against the report.

The 21-Sep edition, Section 6, states that Rs 25 cr of FY26 forex gain is 60.8% of the
year-on-year profit increase, and then reasons: "it cannot be sitting in other income, which
runs at Rs 0.5-3.7 cr a quarter and annualises to well under Rs 10 cr. It is therefore either a
translation benefit inside revenue or netted inside other expenses - and either way it is
**inside the reported EBITDA margin**. FY26's margin recovery from 17.85% to 19.52% is
substantially a currency effect."

**Note 21, Other Income, consolidated** (Rs million):

| | FY2026 | FY2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Interest on bank deposits and others | 10.02 | 10.28 |
| Interest income on income tax refund | 1.81 | 1.80 |
| **Foreign exchange gain (net)** | **206.57** | **25.71** |
| Profit on sale of assets | 5.63 | 12.91 |
| Profit from sale of mutual funds | - | 0.42 |
| Fair value gain on financial instruments at FVTPL (net) | 3.39 | - |
| Miscellaneous income | 3.48 | 0.38 |
| **Total** | **230.90** | **51.50** |

So:
- Total other income FY26 is **Rs 23.09 cr**, not "well under Rs 10 cr". The quarterly run rate
  of Rs 0.5-3.7 cr held for the first three quarters; the gain landed in Q4.
- The forex gain is **Rs 20.66 cr**, not Rs 25 cr, and it **is in other income**, which sits
  **below** EBITDA.
- It is **89.5% of the entire other income line**.

**What must be withdrawn:** the claim that the gain is inside EBITDA, and with it the claim that
"FY26's margin recovery from 17.85% to 19.52% is substantially a currency effect". The operating
margin improvement is not a currency effect. On this point the report was wrong and the
correction favours the company.

**What survives, restated:** the profit-quality point, on the correct arithmetic.
- Forex gain rose from Rs 2.57 cr to Rs 20.66 cr, an increase of **Rs 18.09 cr** pre-tax.
- Profit before tax rose from Rs 71.57 cr to Rs 126.15 cr, an increase of **Rs 54.58 cr**.
- The forex increase is therefore **33.1% of the growth in pre-tax profit**, not 60.8%.
- Of the Rs 20.66 cr, **Rs 8.63 cr is unrealised** (cash flow statement: "net unrealised exchange
  gain 86.30"). Non-cash, and reversible on the next currency move.

A third of the profit growth from a currency line, 42% of which never turned into cash, is still
a real earnings-quality observation. It is a smaller and more precise one than the report made.

## 2. CORRECTION - the natural hedge is better than the report allows

The 21-Sep edition, Section 6: "A natural hedge only works when costs are also in foreign
currency, and MTAR's cost base is overwhelmingly Indian."

Directors' report, foreign exchange earnings and outgo, FY2026:
- Earnings: **Rs 7,267.66 Mn = Rs 726.77 cr**
- Outgo: **Rs 4,264.55 Mn = Rs 426.46 cr**

Foreign currency outgo is **58.7% of foreign currency earnings**. The cost base is not
overwhelmingly Indian on the traded side: MTAR imports heavily. Net long foreign currency
exposure is about **Rs 300 cr**, roughly 34% of revenue, not the near-100% the report's sentence
implies. The natural hedge management describes is real, if partial.

The company also states it hedges through forward contracts, so the unhedged position is smaller
again. ICRA's phrasing is the careful one: margins are exposed "to the extent of the unhedged
position".

## 3. CLOSED - related party transactions, the report's own "single most important unresolved item"

The 21-Sep edition's source list calls this "the single most important unresolved item in this
report". The Ind AS 24 note is small and clean. Standalone, Rs million:

| Item | FY2026 | FY2025 |
|---|---|---|
| KMP remuneration | 80.27 | 95.24 |
| Director sitting fees and commission | 14.02 | 11.16 |
| Job work purchases from subsidiary | 24.44 | 32.04 |
| Interest income from subsidiary | 6.12 | 6.12 |
| Long-term loan to subsidiary (balance) | 61.17 | 61.17 |
| Trade advances receivable from subsidiary (balance) | 58.59 | 4.66 |

In crore: KMP remuneration **Rs 8.03 cr**, down from Rs 9.52 cr, in a year when profit after tax
rose 78%. Total related-party flows are under **1% of revenue**. There is no evidence of value
leaking out of the listed entity to promoter-controlled parties.

**The finding is that there is no finding.** The report should say so as plainly as it stated
the gap, because the gap was presented as its biggest.

One item to note rather than flag: trade advances receivable from the subsidiary rose from
Rs 0.47 cr to Rs 5.86 cr. Twelvefold, but trivial in absolute terms.

## 4. CLOSED - guarantees to related parties

> "Corporate guarantee of Nil (March 31, 2025: 195) has been extended during the previous year to
> wholly owned subsidiary (Gee Pee Aerospace & Defense Private Limited) for availing loan from
> the bank to meet the working capital requirements."

Maximum outstanding during FY25 was Rs 195.00 Mn (**Rs 19.50 cr**); the balance at 31-Mar-2025
was Rs 19.50 Mn (**Rs 1.95 cr**); at 31-Mar-2026 it is **nil**. The Section 186(4) disclosure
shows the same, an unsecured capex loan to Gee Pee Aerospace and Defence Private Limited.

**Gee Pee Aerospace & Defense Private Limited** is a wholly owned subsidiary the 21-Sep edition
never names; it discusses Magnatar. ICRA names it too, and records NADCAP certification at units
5 and 7 under it. The group structure section needs it.

## 5. CLOSED - contingent liabilities, and the secondary figure was right

> "Claims against the Company not acknowledged as debts is amounting to Rs. 22.67 for March 31,
> 2026 (March 31, 2025: Rs. 22.67)."

**Rs 2.27 cr**, unchanged year on year. Against net worth of about Rs 823 cr that is **0.28%**.
The 21-Sep edition used a secondary figure of Rs 2.27 cr for FY25 "only with that label". The
secondary figure was correct, and it is the same number for FY26. No disputed tax demands are
disclosed as contingent.

## 6. CLOSED - the investments question, and it closes in the company's favour

The 21-Sep edition's open items ask whether Rs 238.69 cr of investments is restricted margin
money, noting that if so, true net debt is Rs 376.6 cr rather than Rs 137.9 cr.

Note 4(b), investments: **Rs 2,153.39 Mn = Rs 215.34 cr**, being **4,63,02,855 units of SBI
Savings Fund, direct plan, growth** (Nil at 31-Mar-2025). Quoted, carried at fair value through
profit or loss. Plus Rs 0.10 Mn of unquoted equity, and standalone Rs 66.94 Mn of subsidiary
equity, giving the standalone total of Rs 222.04 cr.

This is an ordinary liquid debt fund, freely redeemable. It is **not** restricted. ICRA reaches
the same place independently, calling it "free cash and liquid investments of Rs. 218.5 crore as
on March 31, 2026". **The adverse case does not hold.**

**Margin money is a separate and much smaller line.** Note 12, bank balances other than cash:
**Rs 202.11 Mn = Rs 20.21 cr** (FY25 Rs 14.73 cr), and the footnote reads:

> "Margin money deposits represent security held by bank for the bank guarantees and letter of
> credits of Rs. 1,499.48 (March 31, 2025: Rs. 726.65) issued by the bankers on behalf of the
> Company."

So the restricted deposit is Rs 20.21 cr, but it secures **Rs 149.95 cr of bank guarantees and
letters of credit outstanding**, up from Rs 72.67 cr - **more than doubled in the year**. That
is the off-balance-sheet exposure stream-A listed as "NOT AVAILABLE". It is the right size for
an importer running Rs 426 cr of foreign currency outgo.

## 7. NEW - capital commitments jumped thirteenfold and nobody has reported it

> "Estimated amount of contracts (net of advances) remaining to be executed on capital account
> and not provided for: Rs. 1,600.17 (March 31, 2025: Rs. 121.20)."

**Rs 160.02 cr at 31-Mar-2026 against Rs 12.12 cr a year earlier. A 13.2x increase.**

This is contracted capex, not a plan. It sits against FY26 free cash flow of Rs 63.3 cr and
ICRA's "capex plans of around Rs 250-300 crore in the medium term". Rs 160 cr of that Rs 250-300
cr is already committed. Neither the report nor, as far as the research streams show, any
published commentary carries this number.

## 8. NEW - the cash arrived at the very end of the year, and the interest line proves it

Interest on bank deposits and others **fell** from Rs 10.28 Mn to Rs 10.02 Mn, while the
investments balance went from **nil to Rs 215.34 cr** and total cash and liquid investments
reached Rs 218.5 cr at the year end.

A full year of interest income of about Rs 1.0 cr against a closing balance of Rs 215 cr is a
yield of under 0.5%. The only reading consistent with both numbers is that **the balance did not
exist for most of the year**. It arrived in the fourth quarter, with the customer advances. The
report's cash-flow finding and this one are the same event.

## 9. CONFIRMED - advances from customers are the reconciler for everything

Contract liabilities (advances from customers), consolidated:

| | 31-Mar-2026 | 31-Mar-2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Contract liabilities (Rs million) | 2,526.53 | 433.41 |
| In crore | **Rs 252.65 cr** | **Rs 43.34 cr** |

**Up Rs 209.31 cr, or 482%, in one year.** Revenue recognised in FY26 out of the opening
contract liability was Rs 181.62 Mn (Rs 18.16 cr), so almost the whole increase is new money in.

This single line reconciles four separate things the report treats as separate:
- the Q4 FY26 operating cash flow at 354% of that quarter's EBITDA, and Q1 FY27 at 291%
- the "working capital days halved" headline, since net working capital nets advances off
- the cash conversion cycle **worsening** to 419 days, since it does not net them off
- the record order book, which is what the advances were paid against

**The working capital improvement, the cash flow surge and the order book are one event.**

Operating cash flow, consolidated: **FY26 Rs 196.90 cr** (Rs 1,969.03 Mn), FY25 Rs 101.25 cr.
This ties exactly to the company's stated Rs 196.9 cr and to screener's Rs 197 cr. The Rs 169.01
cr figure the 21-Sep edition used as the "aggregator" number is TradingView's alone and is the
outlier. Capex, consolidated Rs 133.60 cr (standalone Rs 128.30 cr), which resolves that
discrepancy: both figures were right, on different bases.

**FY26 free cash flow is therefore Rs 63.30 cr**, not the Rs 35.4 cr in Section 19, which
inherited the wrong operating cash flow.

## 10. Receivables and inventory, from the notes

Trade receivables, consolidated: **Rs 336.82 cr** (FY25 Rs 209.78 cr), up **60.6%** against
revenue up 29.6%. Allowance for expected credit loss is **Rs 0.12 cr, unchanged**, on Rs 337 cr
of receivables. Ageing: 92% not yet due, 7% within six months.

Inventories, consolidated: **Rs 500.52 cr** (FY25 Rs 346.05 cr). Raw materials Rs 271.42 cr
(54%), work in progress Rs 213.27 cr (43%), stores Rs 15.83 cr (3%). Raw materials rose 63% in
the year, which matches ICRA's explanation that "the sharp increase in inventory in recent times
can be attributed to the raw materials, which were in transit".

## 11. Borrowings

Standalone secured borrowings **Rs 369.22 cr** (FY25 Rs 177.24 cr). Long term including current
maturities Rs 191.99 cr; short-term cash credit Rs 177.23 cr. Interest rates: term loans **7.30%
to 9.85%**, cash credit **6.67% to 9.69%**. Debt more than doubled in the year.

## 12. UNRESOLVED - the promoter pledge

The annual report discloses **no promoter pledge**. The only pledge reference anywhere in it is
a CARO line: "The Company has not raised loans during the year on the pledge of securities held
in its subsidiaries", which concerns the company pledging subsidiary securities, not promoters
pledging their shares. Promoter encumbrance is disclosed in the quarterly shareholding pattern,
not in the annual report, so the annual report's silence settles nothing.

- Annual report shareholding: promoter group **91,14,707 shares, 29.63%**, no pledge disclosed.
- The premium aggregator extract reports **3.52% pledged** as at 22-Sep-2026.
- The public aggregator page carries no pledge row at all.
- Section 7 of the report describes visible encumbrance peaking near 1.60%, released to about
  0.52%, then rising on 17-Sep-2026 with a 32,000-share pledge, about 0.14%.

None of these reconcile to 3.52%, and none of them is a primary source for the current position.
**State the conflict; do not print either figure as fact.** The Jun-2026 and Sep-2026
shareholding pattern filings would settle it. The 21-Sep edition's Section 19 line "Promoter
pledge: None reported" must go, because it asserts as fact something no primary source in hand
supports.

## 13. Segment revenue - the labels, settled from the company's own chart

The Ind AS 115 disaggregation note in the annual report gives **only a geographic split**, not a
product split:

| | FY2026 | FY2025 |
|---|---|---|
| India (Rs million) | 1,474.30 | 1,328.80 |
| Outside India (Rs million) | 7,178.89 | 5,317.66 |
| **Export share of contract revenue** | **82.96%** | **80.00%** |

The product split comes from the company's Q1 FY27 investor presentation, which carries one
chart per sector with the bars labelled FY23 to Q1 FY27. Read off those charts:

| Company's own label | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 | Q1 FY27 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clean Energy - Civil Nuclear Power | 43.8 | 61.9 | 18.4 | 23.6 | 3.2 |
| Clean Energy - Fuel Cells, Hydel & Others | 441.7 | 351.2 | 416.9 | 615.4 | 220.8 |
| Aerospace & Defence | 49.4 | 39.0 | 93.2 | 103.8 | ~36.3 |
| Products & Others | 23.7 | 108.3 | 147.5 | 134.1 | 100.4 |

Also from the deck, verbatim: **"Diversified Order Book of Rs. 5,143.3 Cr as on 30th Jun 2026"**
and **"robust closing order book of Rs. 3431 Cr in Clean Energy by end of Q1 FY 27"**. Clean
energy is therefore **66.7% of the June book**, confirming the report's figure from the
company's own document.

### The aggregator label error

The premium Insights series as received carried **the right values against the wrong labels**.
Three of the four product labels were rotated:

| Label as received | Actual series |
|---|---|
| "Aerospace & Defence" | Clean Energy - Civil Nuclear Power |
| "Clean Energy Civil Nuclear" | Clean Energy - Fuel Cells, Hydel & Others |
| "Clean Energy Fuel Cells / Hydel / Other" | Aerospace & Defence |
| "Products & Others" | correct |

Proof, on four independent tests:
1. Each row's last four values match the company's own sector chart exactly, FY23 to FY26.
2. The four rows sum to 876.90 against FY26 revenue of 876.21, and to 676.00 against FY25's
   676.0. Both tie.
3. ICRA states clean energy including civil nuclear and fuel cells is "nearly 73% of total
   revenue in FY2026". Fuel cells 615.4 plus civil nuclear 23.6 is 639.0, which is **72.9%**.
4. ICRA states more than 70% of FY26 revenue came from Bloom Energy. Fuel cells alone are
   615.4, which is **70.2%**.

**Whether the rotation is the aggregator's or the transcription's cannot be determined from
here**, because the premium panel is not reachable from this session and the public page does not
carry the series. Either way the lesson for Section 19 is the same and it is the one the skill
already states: never use a machine-extracted operating series without mapping every label back
to the company's own disclosure first. Taken at face value, these labels would have put civil
nuclear at 70% of revenue in a company whose civil nuclear line billed **Rs 3.2 cr** in the most
recent quarter.

### Series alignment

The rows are **right-anchored at FY2026**. The eight-value rows run **FY2019 to FY2026**. The
four-value inflow row runs FY2023 to FY2026. Confirmed by the two revenue ties above and by four
consecutive years matching the company's charts.

The **seven-value export share row could not be aligned with confidence and is not used**. Under
right-anchoring it gives FY25 as 64%, against the annual report's 80.0%. The filing's geographic
split is used instead. Given that three of four labels in the same extract were rotated, an
unreconciled row is not worth printing.

## THREE DATA-INTEGRITY FLAGS - internal contradictions in the company's own disclosure
1. **FY27 margin guidance stated THREE ways on the SAME DAY (30-Jul-2026):**
   MD prepared remarks: "EBITDA margin of 22% +/-100 basis points"
   CFO prepared remarks minutes later: "as against yearly guidance of 24% we have given"
   The investor presentation filed the same day: "EBITDA margin of 24% +/- 100 bps"
   A 200 bps gap on the single most important forward metric. **No analyst on the call caught it.**
2. **The CFO quoted IDENTICAL ROCE figures for two different periods:**
   Q4 FY26 call: "Our ROC is at 17.2% versus 11.4% in the last year."
   Q1 FY27 call: "ROCE is at 17.2% versus 11.4%, and we expected to reach 23% next year."
   Same pair of numbers, one quarter apart, for what should be different periods.
3. **Exports: "majority of our revenues" vs 18-19% in the deck.**
   MD 13-May-2026: "Today, exports contribute to majority of our revenues."
   CFO same call: "more than 70% our revenue is from the exports only."
   The company's own presentations report exports at 19% of 9M FY26 and 18% of FY26.
   Almost certainly a billing-entity artefact (Bloom hot boxes ship to the US but appear to be invoiced
   domestically), but the gap matters because the CFO leaned on rupee depreciation as a FY27 tailwind
   on the same call.
Minor: Q4 FY25 the MD said FY25 clean energy was "around INR 417 crores" and later on the SAME CALL that fuel
cells alone were "around INR 450 crores plus" - the sub-segment larger than its parent.
Q1 FY27: within a single CFO answer, asset turnover on the Rs 500 cr capex was "minimum four to five times"
and then, two sentences later, "at least 6x".
**Working capital days are reported on TWO INCOMPATIBLE BASES**: the CFO quoted 59 days for Q1 FY27 and 172 days
for FY26 on the calls; the decks report 146 days and 266 days for what appear to be the same periods.
Both series show improvement but cannot be compared to each other or to guidance without reconciling the definition.

=> **NET DEBT AT 30-JUN-2026 IS ROUGHLY Rs 45 cr, NOT Rs 377 cr.** Use this for enterprise value.
How it was done: renegotiated commercial and credit terms, faster delivery to customer premises, daily and
weekly monitoring of every working capital element, and a GST refund programme targeting ~Rs 70 cr a year.
"Everything is done organically."
=> A meaningful part of the 59-day figure is likely customer ADVANCES on the new blanket orders, which is
   both a real cash benefit and a reason the figure may not be sustainable at that level. Target is <100 days.

## THE MARGIN MECHANISM - and why it reverses fast
Q1 FY27 gross margin **45.61% vs 47.65%** a year earlier = **-204 bps**
Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin **23.54% vs 18.13%** = **+541 bps**
=> The ENTIRE margin expansion is operating leverage on a fixed cost base of about 28% of revenue.
   Gross margin is FALLING. If revenue growth decelerates from 130%, the margin reverses fast.
   CFO: "we have spent almost 28% on the fixed cost... With increased revenue, we will have at least 5%
   operating leverage." No analyst on any call has stress-tested this.

## FY27 ARITHMETIC
80% growth = Rs 1,577 cr. Q1 done Rs 360.7 cr. Remaining three quarters need **Rs 405 cr each**
= a 12% sequential step-up sustained for three quarters.
75% (the bottom of the band) = Rs 1,533 cr -> Rs 391 cr a quarter.
Simple annualisation of Q1 = Rs 1,443 cr = +65%.
MTAR's revenue is H2-weighted (FY26 H1 292.2 vs H2 584.0 = exactly 2.00x, a guidance MTAR HIT precisely),
so the guidance is reachable. But nuclear (Rs 3.2 cr in Q1 against a guided Rs 150 cr+ year) and A&D
(Rs 36.4 cr a quarter, needs ~Rs 70 cr) are both running well behind the pace the guidance implies.
**If clean energy alone has to carry the year, the 80% depends entirely on Bloom's delivery schedule -
which the company will no longer discuss.**

One quarter accounts for more promoter decline than all the disclosed 2026 selling combined

Promoter holding fell 5.00 percentage points in the December 2024 quarter alone - roughly 15.4 lakh shares, about ₹255 cr at the time. No block deal of that size appears in the accessible record, and the stock traded a narrow range through the quarter with no volume spike. The most likely explanation is a reclassification of promoter-group members to public rather than a sale, and a suggestive but non-causal coincidence is that a non-executive director from a promoter family branch resigned inside that exact quarter. Promoter dealing, netted

Eight open-market sales by seven different promoters over 24 months, totalling 2,88,741 shares or about 0.94% of equity, indicatively ₹190-210 cr. Against exactly one promoter buy, in February 2025, unquantified, at a price roughly one fifth of where the selling later happened. Six of the eight sales, 83% of the shares, went out between May and September 2026 - at and after the all-time high, and while the stock was under Stage IV surveillance. The most recent was five days before this report. Section 7 sets out the full netting, the inter-se gifts and the pledge trail.

Delivery percentage and index events

Any delivery percentage published for MTAR after 25-Jun-2026 is trivially 100% and carries no information, because trade-to-trade settlement makes delivery compulsory. For the period before that, low delivery is one of the recognised triggers for the surveillance framework MTAR was placed in - which is circumstantial evidence that delivery was poor during the parabolic run, though it is not confirmed.

The Midcap 150 entry is an unusual, dated, mechanical event

MTAR enters the Nifty Midcap 150 with effect from 30-Sep-2026, announced around 16-Sep-2026 as one of seven entrants. Mechanically, funds tracking the midcap index must buy while smallcap trackers must sell. The unusual part is the setting: MTAR will join a midcap index while in trade-to-trade settlement with a 5% price band and ninety-six per cent less liquidity than it had three months ago. Passive funds will be executing rebalance trades into a 100%-delivery, circuit-limited book. That is a recipe for a dislocated rebalance in either direction, and it is nine days away.

MTAR is currently in the smallcap 250 and 500, the microcap 250, the total market index and a defence index. No inclusion in or candidacy for the major global index families was found.

Analyst positioning

Seven analysts cover the stock and all seven rate it Buy, giving a consensus of Strong Buy - a distribution with no dissent at 162x earnings. Motilal Oswal published a ₹7,550 target on 31-Jul-2026. JM Financial initiated with a Buy and a ₹9,375 target around 08-Sep-2026, modelling a 66% revenue compound rate and 92% profit compound rate through FY29. A separate consensus service shows an average twelve-month target of ₹7,358 across four analysts, with a high of ₹10,007 and a low of ₹3,650.

Institutional initiation is genuinely recent: IIFL from August 2025, ICICI Securities from January 2026, Avendus Spark from May 2026, with Motilal Oswal escalating to a more senior analyst. That is the discovery phase, not abandonment. Notably, the analyst who asked the most rigorous questions across three calls did not appear on the most recent one.

Read the target dispersion, not the average

The low target is 48% below the current price and the high is 42% above it. When the covering analysts' own range spans a threefold difference in value, the honest reading is that nobody on the street has a confident view either.

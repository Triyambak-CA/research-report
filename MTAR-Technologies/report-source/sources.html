Sources, and what could not be verified

Two editions, two access positions - read this before relying on any figure

The 21-Sep-2026 edition was researched from an environment whose network policy blocked every primary-filing repository: the exchange websites and their archives, the company's own investor relations pages, the rating agency's site, and the major Indian financial data aggregators. No annual report, exchange filing, audited statement or investor presentation could be opened as a document. Two channels worked: a market-data terminal, and through it a library of full verbatim earnings-call transcripts for seven of the last eight quarters. Every figure was tagged as read directly, surfaced through an intermediary, or derived with the arithmetic shown, and nothing was estimated silently.

This edition, 22-Sep-2026, adds the documents. The following were downloaded and read in full: the FY2025-26 and FY2024-25 annual reports filed with the exchange, the Q1 FY27 investor presentation and earnings call transcript, and the ICRA rating rationales of 07-Jul-2026 and 13-Oct-2025. A paid data aggregator's premium panel was pulled from a logged-in session and its public company page fetched directly.

Where a filing and an aggregator disagree, the filing wins and the disagreement is reported as a finding. No aggregator output is presented anywhere as a primary source; every such figure is labelled and dated. Price and market data remain as at 21-Sep-2026 so that the valuation work in Section 14 is not silently re-anchored to a different day's close.

Where this pass changed the earlier conclusions, it says so in the section concerned rather than quietly restating. Three findings were corrected against the filings, six stated open items were closed, and one closed in the company's favour. They are listed below.

Internal contradictions found in the company's own documents

Recorded here in one place because they bear on how much weight any single company figure can carry: FY25 aerospace and defence revenue published three ways; return on capital published at values from 9.65% to 18.2% for the same years, with a deck and a call published the same day pointing in opposite directions; FY27 margin guidance given as 22% by the chief executive, 24% by the finance chief and 24% in the presentation, all on 30-Jul-2026; export share at 18-36% in decks against 71-83% in annual reports; net working capital for March 2026 at 146, 172 and 266 days; Q1 FY26 gross margin at 54.2% and 47.65%; FY25 order inflow at ₹817 cr in February and ₹720 cr in May; a silent restatement of FY24 return on capital from 10.60% to 10.36%; and a subsidiary's name rendered three different ways across the company's own filings. To that list this edition adds a sixth measure of the same year's working capital, and an aggregator product series whose values were right while three of its four labels were attached to the wrong rows.

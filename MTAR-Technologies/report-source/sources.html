Sources, and what could not be verified

Access constraint - read this before relying on any figure

The network policy in the environment this report was researched from blocked direct access to every primary-filing repository: the exchange websites and their archives, the company's own investor relations pages, the rating agency's site, and the major Indian financial data aggregators. No annual report, no exchange filing, no audited financial statement and no investor presentation could be opened as a document.

Two channels worked. A market-data terminal supplied prices, market capitalisation, multiples and a validated fundamental series, and through it a document library supplied full verbatim earnings-call transcripts for seven of the last eight quarters plus machine-generated structured summaries of the company's own filings. A search engine supplied everything else, returning summaries of documents it had read server-side.

Every figure in this report is therefore one of three things, and the distinction is maintained throughout: read directly from a market-data feed or a verbatim transcript; surfaced from a primary document through an intermediary that read it; or derived by arithmetic from those, with the arithmetic shown. No figure has been estimated silently, and where something could not be obtained it is stated as missing rather than assumed.

Internal contradictions found in the company's own documents

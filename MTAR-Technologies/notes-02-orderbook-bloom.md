# MTAR - Notes 02: Order book, Bloom Energy, key findings

## THE CENTRAL FINDING - the record order book is ONE blanket PO from ONE undisclosed customer
Reg 30 filing 14-May-2026: "international blanket purchase orders" USD 238.76 mn = Rs 2,278.96 cr.
- Customer identity NOT disclosed - "confidentiality obligations"
- Described as "in continuation of regular business from the existing customer"
- "in the nature of BLANKET purchase orders"
- "execution timeline will be determined at a later stage"
- Company confirmed promoter group has no interest in the awarding entity; not an RPT
=> A blanket PO is an umbrella agreement with call-offs, NOT a firm scheduled order.
=> Booking it into the disclosed order book and calling it a record 3x jump is an aggressive disclosure choice.
=> Book-to-bill and execution period CANNOT be reliably computed for the largest item in the book.
=> "Existing customer" + international + that scale = almost certainly Bloom Energy (MTAR's sole large intl customer).

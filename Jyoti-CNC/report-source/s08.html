The strongest counter-argument, tested. Many misses share one root cause: one capacity delay drives five lines, Huron's new building three, EMS customers' unbuilt plants three. Some were caused by customers or regulators, not by over-reach. Collapsing to root causes still leaves a record in which nearly every dated operational promise slipped, and the capex cost, debt path, inventory targets, the half-year cash-flow statement and the 30-35% growth assent are not explained by customers or regulators.

Q1 FY27: what management said about a 20% profit fall

Revenue +24.0% to ₹508.5 cr; reported EBITDA margin 21.4% (24.4% a year earlier); finance cost doubled to ₹24.3 cr; other income fell from ₹20.5 cr to ₹4.0 cr; PAT down 20.0% to ₹57.1 cr. On the call the Chairman gave a year-on-year growth figure for standalone profit (+21%) but none for consolidated profit, said the company had "started FY27 on a strong footing with healthy revenue growth, improving profitability", attributed a ₹20-22 cr margin shortfall to Huron, and answered a direct question on what drove interest up with a statement about the stock of debt. The interest rise was signalled one quarter earlier (11-Feb-2026: "Lower growth in PAT is largely attributed to increased finance cost"); the Huron shortfall was not, and two specific assurances given ten weeks earlier did not hold. By arithmetic, the swing in other income alone (₹16.5 cr) more than explains the ₹14.3 cr fall in profit.

Section 18

Order book, execution track record and growth strategy

A ₹4,848 cr order book looks like 2.3 years of revenue in hand. Read quarter by quarter, it is two different things: a core machine business that turns its orders over in months and keeps refilling, and two long-dated blocks, aerospace-and-defence and electronics, that have barely moved in two years. The company has never disclosed the book's age, its GST basis, its currency or its cancellations.

Intake fell while revenue grew. FY26 intake was 9.5% below FY25 against management's "similar momentum" guide; the book grew 8.9% on the year and 5.5% in the first nine months. Management's explanation shifted over the year from "we are not aggressively taking the orders" to "no customers are accepting ... more than 2 years" to "we have been considerate in the order booking". The Q1 FY27 intake of ₹601 cr was the highest first quarter yet.

Ageing: the two blocks that do not move

! About ₹630 cr of EMS orders are 21-33 months old

EMS intake after Sep-2024 was nil on every deck through Q3 FY26 and token since. The EMS book went from about ₹729 cr (Sep-2024) to about ₹630 cr (Jun-2026) while EMS revenue ran at ₹25-45 cr in the quarters it appeared at all. On first-in, first-out, the whole ₹630 cr was booked by Sep-2024, and part of it dates from a ₹304.9 cr order from "an entity in the electronics manufacturing services (EMS) industry" in the Sep-2023 book (prospectus p.170). Delivery dates given and missed: Q2-Q3 FY25, "one year" (Nov-2024), "before December" (Feb-2025), Aug-Sep 2025, Q3-Q4 FY26, "September onwards" 2026. The reason given is that customers' plants are unfinished; in May-2026 the Chairman said "Nothing has been invested much on the last component manufacturing in the last 1.5 years", and that EMS customers are waiting for ECMS clearances. The EMS book also fell by about ₹135 cr in Q4 FY24 with almost no EMS revenue or intake to explain it; asked on 21-May-2024, the Chairman said "I will ... come back to you" and "no orders has been cancelled". It was never reconciled.

The aerospace and defence book has sat at ₹1,700-1,900 cr for eleven quarters against about ₹816 cr of A&D revenue a year: about 2.2 years of cover, and no net growth since Dec-2023. A&D intake guidance of ₹1,500-2,000 cr for FY25 produced about ₹701 cr. Most of the A&D book is executed through Huron according to the Chairman's earlier statements ("80%" Huron-driven, Nov-2024), which puts it squarely inside the export-licence problem in Section 9. Whether the reversed ₹67 cr of Huron revenue went back into the order book is not stated.

Execution record: core versus new

Core machine business Reliable

Machines: 3,063 (FY24), 4,072 (FY25), 5,550 (FY26), 1,406 in Q1 FY27. Utilisation of the 6,000 nameplate above 90% in FY26 and over 100% in Q4 FY26. The auto and general-engineering books turn in months. The FY25 intake range was met. Management's demand and margin numbers have held about half the time.

New verticals and capacity Unreliable

EMS: a book that has not been delivered in two years. Huron: revenue guidance of EUR 50-55 mn for FY25 against EUR 27-28 mn delivered, an assembly line five dates late, and now an investigation. The 10,000-machine plant: five missed dates and a cost that went from ₹200-250 cr to ₹425-450 cr. Semiconductor tools: "large in 26 and 27" became "in the next 2 years". A US technology centre promised "within one year" in Nov-2024 is unconfirmed.

The split matters for any forecast: the core business belongs in a model; EMS, Huron's growth and the semiconductor line belong in an option bucket until they deliver.

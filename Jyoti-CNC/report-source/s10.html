! Media reports, citing prosecutors and a judicial source: "fraudulent exports to Russia, via India"

franceinfo (France Télévisions, Pierre de Cossette, 11-Apr-2026 06:00 CEST), attributing it to the Parquet national antiterroriste (PNAT), and an AFP wire the same day, attributing it to "une source judiciaire", reported that executives of Huron Graffenstaden are suspected of having set up, "à compter de 2022, un système d'exportations frauduleuses vers la Russie, via l'Inde, de machines-outils, classées comme biens à double usage" (from 2022, a system of fraudulent exports to Russia, via India, of machine tools classified as dual-use goods), in breach of export-control law and the EU embargoes tightened after Russia's invasion of Ukraine. StrasInfo (13-Apr-2026) rewrote the AFP account. Verified against the articles on 29-Sep-2026. This is an allegation by prosecutors as reported in the press. No court document was seen, nothing has been proven, and Huron and Jyoti deny wrongdoing.

The reported chronology: a customs control by the customs intelligence directorate in Apr-2024 (three months after listing); a preliminary investigation opened on 09-Oct-2025 (AFP); four current or former Huron staff taken into custody on 31-Mar-2026, the same day bank accounts were seized (AFP), the director general reportedly arrested at his stand at a trade fair at Villepinte (one anonymous source); a judicial investigation opened on 03-Apr-2026, and the director general placed under formal investigation ("mis en examen") in early April and under judicial supervision. Offences listed in the reports include organised smuggling of dual-use goods, delivery to a foreign power of material liable to harm the fundamental interests of the nation, breach of international economic restrictions and, per AFP, criminal association and customs money laundering. The director general is not named in any article read.

Jyoti's Reg 30 letter of Sunday 12-Apr-2026 (primary) described interviews "over this week", the restriction on the director general, seizures of about EUR 4.0 mn and two Jyoti SAS properties, and a formal judicial investigation, and said Huron "refutes the allegations". It disclosed the customs directorate by name, the restriction on the director general, the interim seizures and a "formal judicial investigation". It did not mention Russia or a route via India, gave no offence list, described the custody of four people as employees having "been interviewed", and did not mention that the director general had been placed under formal investigation. Its phrase "over this week" sits badly with arrests on 31-Mar. The shares fell 14.7% on the next trading day, 13-Apr-2026.

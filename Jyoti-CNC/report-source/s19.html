Restatements found by laying the series side by side

No silent restatement of revenue or profit was found: every quarterly figure used in later decks as a comparative matches the figure first reported, and four quarters sum to the audited year in FY25 and FY26. The drift is in operating metrics: the basis of "utilisation" changed three times without notice; realisation per machine moved from a revenue basis to an executed-order basis around Feb-2026, which lowers it by 5-6%; the unit wording moved between "sold", "delivered", "produced", "manufactured", "executed" and "built"; and the order book's stated life moved between 18 months and 2.5 years. None of these is in screener's panel, because none is labelled as a change in the source documents.

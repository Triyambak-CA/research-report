Triggers, themes and government scheme plays

The next six months carry more dated events than the last twelve: a new plant that must open, a half-year balance sheet that will show whether unbilled revenue is turning into cash, a French investigation with no timetable, and a pledge register that should show the September halving. The market is currently paying for three themes at once (defence, electronics manufacturing, capital goods), which is why the stock rose 90% from its June low.

Thematic classification

The market is paying for the theme now: the stock rose from ₹585.50 (01-Jun-2026) to ₹1,113.85 (21-Sep-2026) on the ECMS approval, a strong standalone quarter and defence and electronics enthusiasm, with low delivery percentages on the biggest up-days. Defence manufacturing is a crowded theme in Indian equities in 2026; electronics-manufacturing capital goods is earlier; machine tools as a theme is small because there are few listed names, which is part of why the scarcity premium in Section 3 exists. Theme status: crowded on defence, early on electronics, and priced for both.

The register has three stories. The promoter group has not sold a share since listing, but one member of it borrowed against almost all of his stake before halving that borrowing in ten weeks. The two largest outside holders, who bought at ₹31 a share five months before the IPO, have sold more than half their stakes. And in the quarter the French investigation broke, institutions sold and retail bought.

! A retail-for-institutional swap in the bad-news quarter

A sharp rise in retail holders during a falling, bad-news quarter is the classic froth-in-reverse signal: professional holders leaving, retail buying the dip. The stock then fell a further 16% to its ₹585.50 closing low on 01-Jun-2026 and has since rallied 90% to ₹1,113.85 (21-Sep-2026). Who held through that is the Sep-2026 filing's story.

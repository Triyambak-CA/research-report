## 16. Material Accounting Policies

### Key Policies Summary (from Notes 1-2, STANDALONE)

| Policy | Basis |
|---|---|
| Revenue Recognition | Ind AS 115 - over time or at point of time depending on performance obligations |
| Inventory Valuation | Lower of Cost and NRV (Net Realisable Value) |
| PPE Depreciation | Straight-line over useful life per Schedule II, Companies Act, 2013 |
| Borrowing Costs | Capitalized if directly attributable to acquisition/construction of qualifying assets; otherwise expensed |
| Employee Benefits | Defined contribution (PF) at cost; Defined benefit (Gratuity) actuarially valued |
| Financial Instruments | At amortised cost (most); Fair value through P&L for investments & derivatives |
| Provisions | Recognized when present obligation exists and outflow probable per Ind AS 37 |
| Leases | Right-of-use asset and liability recognized; short-term & low-value assets expensed |
| Consolidation | Full consolidation of subsidiaries; intercompany transactions eliminated |
| Tax | Current tax at applicable rate; deferred tax on temporary differences per Ind AS 12 |

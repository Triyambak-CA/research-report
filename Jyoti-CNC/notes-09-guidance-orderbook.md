## Part 0 - Ranked findings (read this first; detail and citations in Parts A-H)

1. GUIDANCE CREDIBILITY IS LOW, AND UNEVEN. Of 52 scoreable call claims (Feb-2024 to May-2026), 9 were
   met or beaten, 6 partly met, 37 missed: 17% strict, 23% with half-credit (Part B). Demand and margin
   claims score ~50%; dated capacity, capex-cost, working-capital, debt and Huron claims score close to
   nil. ESTABLISHED claims and outcomes; the ratio is my scoring (INFERRED). Counter: many misses share
   one root cause (one plant delay, one Huron building, EMS customers' plants); even collapsed, nearly
   every dated operational promise slipped.
2. Q1 FY27 (07-Aug-2026): revenue +24%, reported EBITDA margin 21.4%, finance cost x2.00 (12.18 -> 24.34
   cr), PAT -20.0% (71.42 -> 57.14 cr). Management gave no year-on-year figure for consolidated PAT on the
   call, called the quarter "improving profitability", did not answer the interest-driver question, and
   adjusted out a 10 cr forex loss while last year's 16 cr forex gain was never adjusted out. The interest
   drag was signalled in Feb-2026; the Huron shortfall was not, and two 29-May-2026 assurances ("It's all
   are done"; 67 cr to be recognised) failed within ten weeks. ESTABLISHED (Part C).
3. HURON INVESTIGATION: not foreshadowed on any earlier call; on both later calls it was framed as an
   export-licence delay ("new normal", "end user certificates"), while the judicial character, the EUR
   4.0m / 3.02m seizure, the two Jyoti SAS properties, the DG restriction and legal cost were never
   volunteered. ~100 cr of revenue on 7-8 machines for Chinese and Turkish customers is unrecognised;
   subsidiaries lost 30.07 cr in Q1 FY27. PCJ's "Jyoti only has dispatched at '22, '23 there [Russia]"
   is ESTABLISHED as a quote; what it means is UNVERIFIED and must not be published as an admission
   (Part H).
4. MACHINE COUNTS DO NOT RECONCILE ACROSS SOURCES. FY24: 3,063 sold (AR; = prospectus H1 1,275 + 797 +
   991) vs 3,495 (call, carried by screener, not rebuildable) vs 3,600 (Infomerics). FY25: 4,072 (company)
   vs 3,517 (Infomerics): the CRA shows volume -2%, the company +33%, gap unexplained. FY26: 5,550 (calls,
   ties to executed value) vs 5,369 manufactured (AR). Screener's series mixes four sources (Part F).
5. ORDER BOOK QUALITY IS UNDISCLOSED. Eleven bridges close to the rupee with no FX, cancellation or
   adjustment line; no GST, ageing or geography basis ever stated. The A&D book has been flat at
   1,700-1,900 cr since Dec-2023 (~2.2 years of A&D revenue). ~630 cr of EMS orders is 21-33 months old
   with no new EMS intake since Sep-2024; a ~135 cr EMS reduction in Q4 FY24 was never explained. The
   1,282.6 cr quarter is Q2 FY25 (Sep-2024), not Dec-2024; its spike is ~526 cr of EMS, customer never
   named. Customer advances are about 1.5-1.8% of the book. Book-to-bill FY25 1.44, FY26 1.13; FY24 not
   determinable (Part E).
6. CASH: PCJ said H1 FY26 operating cash flow "crossed Rs. 50 crores"; the filed half-year figure is 13.15
   cr. FY26 CFO 54.39 cr (10% of EBITDA; 87.30 before the seized cash). Unbilled revenue 162 -> 529 -> 613
   cr after a promise it would "reduce drastically". Gross debt 304 -> 850 cr against "completely debt
   free" in 2-3 years. ESTABLISHED (Parts B, D).
7. CAPACITY: the 6,000 "already completed" in Sep-2024 was re-dated by management to Q4 FY25; the extra
   10,000 has missed five dates (Mar-2025 to Jun-2026) and is due "end of September" 2026 (COD Oct-2026 per
   Infomerics); its cost rose from 200-250 to 425-450 cr and its funding from internal accruals to a 300 cr
   term loan. The "6,000" base was claimed to include Huron on 29-May-2026, contrary to every other
   document (Part G).
8. DISCLOSURE HABITS: the CFO has not answered one question in eleven calls; the promoter pledge was never
   raised or mentioned; the Nov-2025 deck and call contradict each other on Tumakuru; decks print "24-30
   months" of visibility while the CMD says 18-20 months; the FY26 revenue KAM names over-time measurement as a
   judgement but lists only point-in-time procedures, while 465 cr of standalone unbilled POCM revenue sits
   on the balance sheet (Part D).

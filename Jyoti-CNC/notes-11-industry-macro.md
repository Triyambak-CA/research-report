### D. Incidental findings (adjacent, not asked)
- Order-book EMS share is 13% but FY26 revenue EMS share is 1%: revenue mix will shift sharply toward EMS in FY27 if the order book converts (tr-2026-08, 07-Aug-2026). Management said EMS is "fully busy" and customers wait for ECMS clearance.

- Economic Survey 2025-26 places "defence electronics" and TBMs in a long-horizon capability tier with no near-term localisation mandate.

## 5. Debt Picture: Pre-IPO vs Post-IPO

### Financial Indebtedness at Key Dates:

| Particulars | 30-Sep-2023 (pre-IPO) | 31-Mar-2024 (FY24 end) | 31-Mar-2025 (FY25 end) | 31-Mar-2026 (FY26 end) |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|

| **Current borrowings (Rs crore)** | - | 219.27 | 394.32 | 439.48 |
| **Total borrowings (Rs crore)** | 507.48 | 303.78 | 496.88 | 849.71 |

### Key Observations:

1. **Pre-IPO debt (as at 30-Sep-2023):** Rs 507.48 crore of sanctioned facilities, with Rs 5,074.83 crore (Rs 507.48 crore) outstanding. Repayment of Rs 475 crore from IPO proceeds was intended to reduce this significantly.

2. **Post-repayment (31-Mar-2024):** Total debt decreased to Rs 303.78 crore - a reduction of Rs 203.70 crore from the pre-IPO level, reflecting the Rs 475 crore debt repayment partially offset by other borrowing activities.

3. **Re-leveraging trajectory:**
   - FY24 (31-Mar-2024): Rs 303.78 crore (64% reduction from pre-IPO)
   - FY25 (31-Mar-2025): Rs 496.88 crore (increase of Rs 193.10 crore)
   - FY26 (31-Mar-2026): Rs 849.71 crore (increase of Rs 352.83 crore)

4. **Nature of re-borrowing:** The FY25 and FY26 annual reports indicate fresh borrowings were taken for working capital and business expansion, including:
   - Working capital facilities from Union Bank, HDFC Bank, SBI Bank
   - Term loans from Union Bank of India (Rs 266.80 crore by Mar-31-2026)
   - Foreign currency loans (Euribor-linked) from Axis Bank (Rs 101.89 crore Euro loan by Mar-31-2026) and SBI (Rs 156.43 crore Euro loan by Mar-31-2026)
   - Equipment financing loans (Axis Finance Rs 95.51 crore by Mar-31-2026)

The company's debt-raising pattern shows an initial IPO-driven deleveraging followed by aggressive re-leveraging for growth and working capital, with a particular emphasis on external currency borrowings for international operations (Jyoti SAS and Huron acquisitions).

## Summary

This document traces Rs 949.45 crore of IPO proceeds from Jyoti CNC's Jan-2024 listing through Sep-2024. All three objects were substantially executed: Rs 475 crore debt repayment was fully utilised by Mar-31-2024; working capital funding reached Rs 349.47 crore (97% of Rs 360 crore plan); general corporate purposes reached Rs 104.71 crore (92% of Rs 114.45 crore plan). By Sep-30-2024, Rs 929.18 crore (98%) had been deployed, with Rs 20.27 crore parked in fixed deposits. CARE Ratings reported no deviations across three consecutive quarterly monitoring reports. The company subsequently re-leveraged significantly post-IPO, growing total debt from Rs 303.78 crore (31-Mar-2024) to Rs 849.71 crore (31-Mar-2026) to fund working capital and aggressive international expansion via Huron acquisitions.

## 4. Disclosure timeliness

SAST Reg 31(3) requires the Reg 31(1)/31(2) disclosure within seven working days of the
creation, release or invocation. On BSE, the 22-Jun and 29-Jun creations and the 02, 03 and
06-Jul releases were all received on 17-Jul-2026, outside that window. The 12-Aug creation was
received on 26-Aug-2026, also outside it (15-Aug-2026 is a Saturday; seven working days end
21-Aug). The later filings were on time. NSE's Reg 31 feed could not be located, so whether the
NSE copies were earlier is NOT VERIFIED. Treat as a promoter-group compliance lapse on the BSE
record, not as a finding against the company.

## 5. What could not be retrieved (named gap)

The individual Reg 31(1) creation filings for the Sep-2024 quarter, the Mar-2025 quarter and the
Mar-2026 quarter step-ups (in all 2,19,52,000 shares) were not retrieved. BSE's announcement page
returned only its default three-month window (30-Jun to 29-Sep-2026) in the automated session and
its API is Akamai-blocked; NSE's corporate-announcements feed does not carry Reg 31 filings. So
the counterparty for most of the pledge built before Jun-2026 is known only by inference (HDFC
Bank for at least 1,71,00,000 shares, Aditya Birla Capital for 25,52,000).
Virani's Reg 31(4) letter dated 06-Apr-2026 declares he "had disclosed all encumbrances ... made
by me during the financial year ended on March 31, 2026". That declaration could not be tested
against the FY26 filings for the reason above.

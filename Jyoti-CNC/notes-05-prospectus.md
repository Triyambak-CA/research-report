- **Global Market Share:** Approximately 0.4% (twelfth largest globally) in calendar year 2022

**Product Portfolio**
- Over 200 variants across 44 series
- Product types: CNC Turning Centers, CNC Turn Mill Centers, CNC Vertical Machining Centers (VMCs), CNC Horizontal Machining Centers (HMCs)

- **H1 FY24 + Last 3 Fiscals:** Over 8,400 machines supplied
- **Since April 1, 2004:** Over 30,000 CNC machines supplied globally
- **Customer Base:** More than 3,500 customers in India and across Asia (excluding India), Europe, North America and rest of world

- Total Order Book: ₹33,153.26 million

- **Facilities in India:** Fully integrated operations including production lines, foundry, sheet metal shop, paint shop, sub-assembly and assembly lines, repair facility in Rajkot, Gujarat

## 6. Top 20 Risk Factors (Risk Factor Headlines)

**Source:** SECTION II: RISK FACTORS (pages 47-89 of printed prospectus; line 2549+ in text file)

### Internal Risk Factors:

1. **Past Losses and Negative Equity:** "We have incurred losses and consequently, had a negative return on equity in the past. Losses in future could have an adverse impact on our growth prospectus and would also preclude us from undertaking actions such as declaring dividends." (Line 2593; Page 47)

2. **Customer Concentration and Lack of Long-Term Contracts:** "Our success is dependent on our relationship with our customers, and we do not, generally enter into long term purchase contracts. This exposes us to risk emanating from the inability to retain our established customers as our clients." (Line 2638; Page 48) - Top 10 customers represented 39.92% of revenue in H1 FY24

3. **High Indebtedness and Low Debt Service Coverage:** "We have incurred significant indebtedness and carry substantial debt servicing obligations. Further we have high debt equity ratio and a low debt service coverage ratio. If we do not generate sufficient amount of cash flows from operations, our liquidity and our ability to service our indebtedness could be adversely affected." (Line 2752; Page 50)

4. **No Long-Term Supply Agreements:** "Our Company does not have long-term agreements with suppliers for our input materials and a significant increase in the cost of, or a shortfall in the availability, or deterioration in the quality, of such input materials could have an adverse effect on our business and results of operations." (Line 2847; Page 51)

5. **Complete Reliance on Third-Party Logistics:** "We are completely reliant on third-party logistics service providers for transport of input materials and finished products." (Line 2915; Page 52)

6. **Subsidiary Going Concern Issue:** "Our Statutory Auditors have made certain comments in respect of our Company's standalone audited financial statements for 6 months period ended September 30, 2023 and the Fiscals 2023 and 2022. Our erstwhile statutory auditors have made certain comments in respect of our Company's standalone audited financial statements for Fiscals 2021. The comments indicate that our Material Subsidiaries had accumulated losses and their net worth were eroded which consequently indicates material uncertainty that may impact the Material Subsidiaries to continue as going concerns." (Line 2944; Page 53)

7. **Delayed Submission of Audited Consolidated Financial Statements:** "Our Company has delayed in submission of our audited consolidated financial statement in the past. We cannot guarantee that such delays will not occur again in the future." (Line 3080; Page 54)

8. **Industry-Dependent Business:** "Our business is dependent on the performance of the Application Industries with a large portion of revenue derived from aerospace and defence, auto and auto components and general engineering sectors." (Line 3099; Page 54)

(Line 3193; Page 56)

(Line 3345; Page 58)

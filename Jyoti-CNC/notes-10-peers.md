Sanity test of Jyoti's 10-12% (DERIVED, my arithmetic): Jyoti standalone FY26 revenue Rs 1,949 cr (screener standalone) is 11.2% of FY26 Indian production of Rs 17,387 cr. It is 5.4% of FY26 consumption of Rs 36,419 cr. Consolidated Rs 2,093 cr / Rs 17,387 cr = 12.0%, but that includes Huron in France so it is not an Indian share. The 10-12% claim therefore fits a share of Indian PRODUCTION (all machine tool types, including presses), not of consumption. Jyoti's exports are inside its revenue, so this overstates domestic share slightly. If Ace's 22-25% is also a share of production, Ace's FY24 revenue of Rs 2,214 cr would need a production base of Rs 8,900-10,000 cr; IMTMA all-machine production is about Rs 14,600 cr for FY25 (implied), so Ace's 22-25% is not a share of that total either. So the claims use different, unstated denominators (likely CNC only). Nothing verified on a common basis.

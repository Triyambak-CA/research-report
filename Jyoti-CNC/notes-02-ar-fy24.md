## SUMMARY - 10 MATERIAL FIGURES

1. **Revenue Growth**: Standalone revenue from operations increased 43.5% YoY to Rs. 11,897.24 crore, driven by machinery sales up 44.0% to Rs. 11,050.81 crore (PDF p. 158-159).

2. **Debt Reduction**: Total borrowings fell sharply to Rs. 921.92 crore from Rs. 6,249.23 crore year-prior, with non-current down 97.7% to Rs. 25.74 crore (PDF p. 154-155), reflecting IPO capital deployment to repay bank facilities.

3. **Cash Generation**: Operating cash declined to negative Rs. 649.18 crore (FY23: +206.89) due to Rs. 768.21 crore inventory build and Rs. 503.08 crore tax outflow, but investing outflows of Rs. 2,311.94 crore (PP&E Rs. 1,059.83 crore + deposits Rs. 710.82 crore) offset by Rs. 11.26 crore IPO net proceeds (PDF p. 128-129, 190).

4. **Equity Infusion**: Equity capital increased 38.0% to Rs. 454.85 crore (22.74 crore shares post 5:1 split Jan 2024) from IPO of Rs. 3.02 crore shares at Rs. 331 per share (Rs. 10.0 crore net proceeds) plus prior-year share conversions (PDF p. 150-151).

5. **Profitability Surge**: Profit after tax (standalone) jumped 256% to Rs. 1,399.94 crore from Rs. 393.25 crore; effective tax rate stable at ~25%; consolidated PAT Rs. 1,508.61 crore benefited from Jyoti SAS turnaround (FY23 subsidiary loss of Rs. 54.64 crore consolidated vs FY24 gain) (PDF p. 128, 189).

6. **Inventory Buildup**: Raw materials rose 61.1% to Rs. 3,476.45 crore, finished goods 348% to Rs. 98.44 crore; total inventory Rs. 7,922.58 crore (FY23: 7,154.38) representing ~7.5 months of sales, indicating production ramp-up for order book (PDF p. 146-147).

7. **Trade Receivables Expansion**: Receivables increased 37.9% to Rs. 2,335.10 crore from customer base expansion; ageing shows 60.8% < 6 months, 13.0% in 6-12 month bucket; expected credit loss Rs. 48.27 crore (2.0% of gross) (PDF p. 147).

8. **Interest Expense Decline**: Finance costs decreased 7.6% to Rs. 657.76 crore due to 82.4% repayment of rupee loans; interest on borrowings fell to Rs. 525.31 crore from Rs. 600.44 crore; weighted average cost of borrowings remains ~12.5% on residual working capital facilities (PDF p. 160-161).

9. **Capital Investment Program**: CWIP jumped 820% to Rs. 331.67 crore (FY23: 35.93) signaling major expansion; capex in FY24 Rs. 1,059.83 crore (vs. Rs. 240.11 crore prior year), funded by cash generation and IPO proceeds (PDF p. 128-129, 146).

10. **Tax Position**: Current tax liability Rs. 108.93 crore (down from 130.08); deferred tax liability net Rs. 123.20 crore (down from 202.12); no pending income tax disputes disclosed; applied tax rate 25.17% (standard Indian rate) with reconciliation showing depreciation benefit & R&D tax credits (PDF p. 160-161).

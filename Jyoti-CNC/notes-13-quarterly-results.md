## 3. The Q4 FY26 revenue reversal (May-2026 deck, slide 10, primary)

"Revenue for Q4FY26 stood at Rs. 599.2 crores, a growth of 4% primarily due to the reversal of
revenue amounting to ~Rs. 67 crores in the Huron subsidiary, arising from the ongoing
investigation and accounting treatment of reversal of revenue under the Percentage of Completion
Method. However, this does not represent a loss of revenue, but rather a deferment to future
periods. Excluding this reversal, revenue growth would have been ~16% for Q4FY26 and ~19% for FY26."
And: "EBITDA was impacted as the costs associated with the revenue reversal were not
proportionately reversed and continued to be recognized in the current period."

Read with notes-04: Huron recognises revenue on machines over time (percentage of completion).
Machines placed under French customs control could not be completed or delivered, so revenue
already booked on them was reversed. The "deferment, not a loss" claim depends entirely on the
investigation ending with the machines released and the customers still wanting them. That is
a management assertion, NOT VERIFIED.

## 4. What the table says

- Consolidated EBITDA margin peaked at 30.9% in Q4 FY25 and has not returned there; 24.4-26.8%
  through FY26, 21.4% reported in Q1 FY27.
- Finance cost roughly doubled from Rs 11-12 cr a quarter (FY25) to Rs 20-24 cr (Q3 FY26 onward),
  tracking borrowings from Rs 497 cr to Rs 850 cr.
- Other income swung between -0.4 and 24.8 a quarter; FY26 total Rs 60.5 cr of which forex gain
  Rs 43.4 cr (notes-04). Q1 FY27 had an Rs 10 cr unrealised forex LOSS inside EBITDA instead.
- The standalone Indian business is growing 20%+ with stable 27-32% EBITDA margins. The group
  story in FY26-FY27 is the subsidiaries.

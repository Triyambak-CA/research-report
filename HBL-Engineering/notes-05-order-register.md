### 3. Order Book Reconciliation vs CARE and Segment Revenue
**CARE book snapshots:** 30-Jun-2022 Rs 594 cr → 31-Aug-2024 Rs 1,178.75 cr → 28-Feb-2025 Rs 3,174.35 cr → 31-Jul-2025 Rs 4,479 cr → 31-Dec-2025 Rs 2,999 cr. **Announced intake:** Only Reg 30-threshold orders (typically Rs 200+ cr) are captured; register total Rs 6,521 cr ex-GST spread unevenly (FY25-FY26 concentrated). **Segment revenue (screener, secondary):** FY25 electronics Rs 541 cr, FY26 Rs 1,626 cr. **Roll-forward test fails:** Jun-2022 Rs 594 + FY23-24 announced intake Rs 404 ex-GST - FY23-24 electronics billings (est. Rs 1,100 cr) ≠ 31-Aug-2024 Rs 1,178.75 cr book. **Gap Rs 600-700 cr:** Pre-Feb-2022 backlog + non-announced orders (below Reg 30 threshold, ~Rs 80-150 cr/order pre-FY26). Sep-2025 electronics sales spike (Rs 794 cr) confirms execution rate exceeded announced intake that quarter, drawing down backlog from Jul Rs 4,479 to Dec Rs 2,999 (Rs 1,480 cr billed Sep-Nov, order book only Rs 1,000 cr intake announced). **Conclusion:** Announced register is floor; actual intake 30-40% higher (pre-threshold + unanounced).

### 4. Book-to-Bill
**FY25 (Announced Reg 30 orders only):** Rs 2,447.04 cr ex-GST / Rs 541 cr electronics sales = **4.53x** (unsustainable; inflated by screener's understated FY25 electronics figure; actual denominator likely Rs 800+ cr if Kavach ramp-up included). **FY26 (Announced):** Rs 3,231.61 cr ex-GST / Rs 1,626 cr electronics sales = **1.99x** (credible; Sep-2025 Rs 794 cr quarter confirms Kavach execution acceleration).

### 5. Orders Dated After CARE Book-as-at Dates (Trap: Post-Balance-Date Stuffing)

Expected increase Rs 1,305 cr vs intake Rs 1,720 cr → Rs 415 cr gap suggest non-announced orders or order dates mismatched. **31-Dec-2025 CARE (Rs 2,999 cr) vs Jan-Aug 2026:** Orders announced Jan-Feb-Apr-May-Aug 2026 = Rs 2,982 cr ex-GST; book fell Jul-Dec from Rs 4,479 to Rs 2,999 (Rs 1,480 cr execution) → **Apr-Jun 2026 orders unannounced (estimated Rs 300+ cr)**. **Trap confirmed:** May-2026 CLW order (Rs 1,714 cr ex-GST, announced 28-May-2026) dated after Dec-2025 CARE snapshot but before Feb-2026 rationale update; LoA date not disclosed in filing (only announcement date). Treat as new order post-Dec-2025 book.

## Gaps and Limitations

1. **Reg 30 threshold:** Register captures only orders above materiality (typically Rs 200+ cr for listed company with Rs 3,300 cr annual revenue). Orders Rs 50-150 cr (below threshold or waived) not announced. Actual intake ~30-40% higher than register total.

2. **Bid vs awarded:** Sep-2022 updates include bid-stage orders (WCR Rs 353.84 cr, Western Railway Rs 81.67 cr, "lowest bidder, contracts expected shortly"). Later filings do not confirm award or LoA; likely expired bids or awaiting ministry approval. Not included in Jan-2022 to Sep-2026 order register (treat as contingent).

3. **Consortium accounting:** HBL-Shivakriti, HBL-Siemens orders state "lead member" but do not disclose HBL's precise share. Assumed 100% of announced value attributed to HBL (conservative for consortia; actual HBL share may be 60-80%).

4. **LoA vs announcement date:** Register uses announcement date (NSE filing date) for fiscal quarter assignment. Actual LoA dates (when disclosed separately) differ by days to weeks; not disclosed in 50% of filings (ICF, BLW, PLW, both CLW orders). Fiscal quarter may shift if LoA date tested.

5. **GST rate variance:** Aug-2025 WCR order (Rs 54.12 cr) states "inclusive of GST" without rate → assumed 18%, marked UNVERIFIED.

6. **Segment revenue definition:** Screener's "electronics segment" includes Kavach, TCAS, TMS, signalling, defence (mixed). FY25 stated as Rs 541 cr is likely understated (Kavach was ramping; invoice patterns suggest Rs 600-800 cr credible for Kavach-heavy Q4). Annual report segmental note required to isolate Kavach.

7. **No Jan-2022 data:** NSE filing index starts 03-Feb-2022 (row 174, trading window notice). No Jan-2022 orders in data. Search for BSE filings recommended if completeness to Jan-2022 required.

1. **HBL guides in filed letters, not calls, and the letters are precise.** Ten dated letters, decks and board notes since Feb-2023, plus the FY24-FY26 annual reports, carry numbers to the crore (Step 0). The chairman then says "we are careful not to say it is management guidance" (AGM 25-Sep-2025 PDF p.2). Section 8 should score the letters as guidance. Established.
2. **Headline guidance is right in direction only when Kavach orders arrive, and then it is too low to be useful.** FY24 EBITDA came in 66% above the Feb-2023 number and FY26 EBITDA 122% above it; FY25, when tenders slipped, missed all four numbers issued for it, including the 27-May-2024 floor that excluded new tenders (2,093 vs 1,946). Established.

Established as a contradiction; the explanation is inferred.
5. **The drivetrain channel reversed without comment:** AR-FY24 rejected selling kits to truck makers as "tedious, low margin" and chose to be a truck OEM; AR-FY26 plans to sell kits to "E-truck makers". The e-truck marketing subsidiary (Torquedrive) is being struck off. Established.
6. **Kavach durability language moves the decline year out as it approaches** (FY28 at the Sep-2025 AGM, FY29 in AR-FY26), and the Nov-2025 warning that FY26 "should not be a basis for future expectations" became "FY 27 ... significantly better than FY 26" by May-2026, while Q1 FY27 PBT fell 24% y-o-y. Established.
7. **The 15-Jan-2026 FY26 Kavach estimate (Rs 1,880 cr) exceeds the whole FY26 Electronics segment (Rs 1,626 cr).** Either it was GST-inclusive, in which case the FY27 "at least Rs 1,900 cr" is flat, or FY26 Kavach missed by about Rs 254 cr within ten weeks. Inferred; one question to the company settles it.
8. **Drivetrain money is not disclosed.** Direct floor: Rs 3.00 cr Torquedrive impairment and Rs 5.40 cr JV commitment. The Rs 22.65 cr of development projects "discarded in view of their non viability" in FY24 is the only disclosed candidate for abandoned EDT work; attribution is inferred, not established.

## Step 2 - Scorecard

Verdict rules: **hit** = outcome within +/-10% of the claim or the dated event happened by the date; **beat** = outcome more than 10% better than claimed (counted as a hit, but flagged, because a claim missed on the upside by 20-120% was not informative); **partial** = part of a compound claim met, or met only against a later revision; **miss** = outcome more than 10% worse, or the dated event did not happen by the date; **too early** = deadline not yet reached. Claims are split into three buckets because a single blended ratio hides the pattern.

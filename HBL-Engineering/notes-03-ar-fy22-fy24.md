## 2. REVENUE RECOGNITION POLICY & IND AS 115

### Policy (Consolidated, Notes 31/32 across FY22-FY24)

- Point-in-time: Goods upon delivery/dispatch (domestic) or proof of export (exports)
- Over-time: Services/projects where output method (units produced) or input method (effort/cost) depict performance
- Measurement: Cumulative catch-up for contract price changes

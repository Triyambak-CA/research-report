What the scuttlebutt adds

Three things the financial statements do not show. First, the hiring is entirely rail deployment, so whatever the annual report says about drivetrains and sonar, the company's spending on people is going where the revenue is. Second, the Tonbo IPO is a possible value-marking event that HBL has not mentioned to its own shareholders, consistent with its sparse disclosure elsewhere (Section 7). Third, there is no evidence of product failure in the field despite rapid deployment, which supports the execution reputation that the cancellation and the lost tender otherwise dent.

Section 06

Earnings quality and forensic checks

The FY26 profit is real in the sense that matters most: it was invoiced, largely collected, and taxed at the full rate. What is weak is its repeatability and the care with which it is reported. Line items of an audited year changed after the audit, one year's segment assets exist in two versions, the exchange twice rejected the format of the results, and a fifth of FY26's operating cash flow is provisions not yet paid.

Reporting-quality findings

! The FY26 audited P&L was changed after audit, without explanation

The Jun-2026 quarter results (08-Aug-2026) reprint the "31-Mar-26 Audited" column with current tax Rs 279.60 cr (the audited results of 23-May-2026: Rs 283.65 cr), deferred tax -6.63 (-6.34), share of associates Rs 12.01 cr (Rs 16.34 cr) and profit Rs 814.44 cr (Rs 814.89 cr). The Mar-2026 quarter column moved the same way (associates Rs 11.71 cr to Rs 8.55 cr). The net change in profit is small, Rs 0.45 cr; the point is that an audited column was altered with only "regrouped, wherever necessary" as a note, and the annual report published in between carries the original numbers.

  • Two versions of FY25 segment assets, both labelled audited. AR FY25: defence Rs 297.54 cr, unallocated Rs 583.74 cr. Quarterly filings' FY25 column: defence Rs 356.12 cr, unallocated Rs 525.16 cr. This matters for the screener lead on defence capital employed: the rise from Rs 95 cr to Rs 432 cr is partly real (the defence cell plant and fuze factory, Section 17) and partly an allocation choice.
  • Q3 FY26 costs reshuffled. Employee cost Rs 81.31 cr and other expenses Rs 126.30 cr as first filed became Rs 66.50 cr and Rs 141.11 cr three months later.
  • Exchange queries. NSE sought clarification on the Q4 FY25 results (XBRL discrepancies) and on the Q2 FY26 results (XBRL discrepancies, and "not as per format prescribed under Schedule III of the Companies Act, 2013 or as per Indian Accounting Standard"): the best quarter in the company's history was filed in a non-compliant format.
  • Small internal contradictions. The FY26 segment note says only batteries and electronics are reportable, then reports three segments; the consolidated cash flow statement labels interest paid of Rs 12.68 cr as "Interest income received" under financing; the CWIP note's stale date. None is material alone; together they describe a finance function that has not scaled with the company.

Screener's "other liabilities" jump, explained

Screener shows other liabilities rising from Rs 422 cr to Rs 661 cr in FY26. It is not customer advances, which fell from Rs 55.98 cr to Rs 39.00 cr. The Rs 239 cr increase is mainly: unearned and deferred revenue (contract liabilities) up Rs 97 cr to Rs 115.85 cr; warranty provision up Rs 80 cr; the unpaid CMD commission provision up Rs 37 cr to Rs 56.02 cr; the new labour-code gratuity provision Rs 25.49 cr; statutory dues up Rs 23 cr (AR FY26 notes 24, 29, 30). In other words, the liability side grew with obligations HBL has already expensed, which is conservative, and with deferred revenue it has billed but not yet earned, which is FY27 revenue already invoiced.

Earnings quality score

6 / 10. For: profit converts to cash (93% before tax), receivables are in line, tax is paid in full, other income is small, and the warranty has been provided rather than hoped away. Against: about 35% of FY26 revenue came from one order that was a quarter cancelled; the segment profit that drove the year is struck from expense allocations the company can and does move between filings; an audited year was altered after audit; and the finance function's filings have drawn two exchange queries. The number is the report's judgement on the evidence above.

Section 06

Earnings quality and forensic checks

The FY26 profit is real in the sense that matters most: it was invoiced, largely collected, and taxed at the full rate. What is weak is its repeatability and the care with which it is reported. The FY26 annual report shows associates' profit one way on the face of the P&L and another way in its own note, one year's segment assets exist in two versions, the exchange twice queried the format of the results, and a fifth of FY26's operating cash flow is provisions not yet paid.

Reporting-quality findings

! Associates' profit shown gross on the face, net in the note

The audited FY26 consolidated P&L (and the 23-May-2026 results) shows share of associates' profit of Rs 16.34 cr above tax, with the associates' own tax (Rs 4.33 cr) buried in group tax expense (Rs 283.65 cr current tax). The same annual report's associate note 9A gives the correct after-tax share, Rs 12.01 cr (NSTL Rs 4.76 cr plus Tonbo Rs 7.25 cr). The Jun-2026 results (08-Aug-2026) then reprinted the FY26 column on the after-tax basis (associates Rs 12.01 cr, current tax Rs 279.60 cr) with only a "regrouped, wherever necessary" note. Profit does not change (Rs 814.44 cr before non-controlling interest in both), so this is a presentation error, now corrected silently, not a restatement of earnings. The same gross presentation appears in FY24 and FY25 (notes-06). It overstates consolidated PBT by the associates' tax and it went through audit three years running.

  • Two versions of FY25 segment assets, both labelled audited. AR FY25: defence Rs 297.54 cr, unallocated Rs 583.74 cr. Quarterly filings' FY25 column: defence Rs 356.12 cr, unallocated Rs 525.16 cr. This matters for the screener lead on defence capital employed: the rise from Rs 95 cr to Rs 432 cr is partly real (the defence cell plant and fuze factory, Section 17) and partly an allocation choice.
  • Q3 FY26 costs reclassified. Employee cost Rs 81.31 cr and other expenses Rs 126.30 cr as first filed became Rs 66.50 cr and Rs 141.11 cr three months later: the Rs 14.81 cr New Labour Codes charge moved from employee cost to other expenses, as the FY26 results notes disclose. Disclosed and harmless, but the same charge sat in two different lines in two filings.
  • Exchange queries. NSE sought clarification on the Q4 FY25 results (XBRL discrepancies) and on the Q2 FY26 results (XBRL discrepancies, and "not as per format prescribed under Schedule III of the Companies Act, 2013 or as per Indian Accounting Standard"): the best quarter in the company's history was filed in a non-compliant format.
  • Small internal contradictions. The FY26 segment note says only batteries and electronics are reportable, then reports three segments; the consolidated cash flow statement labels interest paid of Rs 12.68 cr as "Interest income received" under financing; the CWIP note's stale date. None is material alone; together they describe a finance function that has not scaled with the company.

Screener's "other liabilities" jump, explained

Screener shows other liabilities rising from Rs 422 cr to Rs 661 cr in FY26. It is not customer advances, which fell from Rs 55.98 cr to Rs 39.00 cr. The Rs 239 cr increase is mainly: unearned and deferred revenue (contract liabilities) up Rs 97 cr to Rs 115.85 cr; warranty provision up Rs 80 cr; the unpaid CMD commission provision up Rs 37 cr to Rs 56.02 cr; the new labour-code gratuity provision Rs 25.49 cr; statutory dues up Rs 23 cr (AR FY26 notes 24, 29, 30). In other words, the liability side grew with obligations HBL has already expensed, which is conservative, and with deferred revenue it has billed but not yet earned, which is FY27 revenue already invoiced.

Earnings quality score

6 / 10. For: profit converts to cash (93% before tax), receivables are in line, tax is paid in full, other income is small, and the warranty has been provided rather than hoped away. Against: about 35% of FY26 revenue came from one order that was a quarter cancelled; the segment profit that drove the year is struck from expense allocations the company can and does move between filings; the audited P&L presents associates' profit on a basis its own note contradicts; and the finance function's filings have drawn two exchange queries. The number is the report's judgement on the evidence above.

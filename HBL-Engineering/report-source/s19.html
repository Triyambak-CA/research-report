Restatements a one-document read would miss

  • FY25 segment assets exist in two versions, both labelled audited. AR FY25 (p.269): defence Rs 297.54 cr, unallocated Rs 583.74 cr. The quarterly filings' "31-Mar-2025 audited" column (e.g. 08-Nov-2025): defence Rs 356.12 cr, unallocated Rs 525.16 cr. Rs 58.6 cr moved between segments. Screener's quarterly capital-employed series follows the quarterly version, so its defence ROCE series is on a different base from the annual report.
  • The FY26 audited P&L lines changed after audit. The Jun-2026 results (08-Aug-2026) reprint the FY26 audited column with current tax Rs 279.60 cr (23-May-2026 filing: Rs 283.65 cr), share of associates Rs 12.01 cr (Rs 16.34 cr) and profit Rs 814.44 cr (Rs 814.89 cr). Q4 FY26 associates moved from Rs 11.71 cr to Rs 8.55 cr. The only note is "regrouped, wherever necessary".
  • Q3 FY26 cost lines were reshuffled. Employee cost Rs 81.31 cr and other expenses Rs 126.30 cr as first filed (07-Feb-2026) became Rs 66.50 cr and Rs 141.11 cr in the Mar-2026 filing: Rs 14.81 cr moved.
  • Segment scheme changed from two reportable segments (batteries, electronics) to three (industrial batteries, defence and aviation, electronics); the FY26 annual report's segment narrative still says only batteries and electronics are reportable while its table shows three.

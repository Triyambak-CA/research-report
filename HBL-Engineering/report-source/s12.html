Section 12

Triggers, themes and government scheme plays

The next four quarters are dominated by delivery deadlines rather than by new launches. About Rs 2,980 cr of on-board Kavach orders placed in 2026 fall due between Jan-2027 and May-2027 under the same forfeiture terms that cost HBL 541 sets last year. Whether HBL delivers them in full decides FY27, and FY27 decides whether CARE's upgrade test and the market's Kavach assumption hold.

Is the market paying for these themes?

Railway safety / Kavach: fully priced and maturing. Over five years HBL's trailing P/E has ranged from 21.8 to 82.0 (screener), and every listed Kavach name has run; the story is now crowded, with four vendors and published unit prices. Defence electronics: the market pays 46-123 times earnings for it (Section 3), and HBL's small defence segment is arguably under-attributed in that sense, but it has not yet produced growth. Electric trucks: early and unpaid; no listed Indian company earns money from heavy e-truck drivetrains. Batteries: out of favour (lead-acid peers at 18-37 times), which is where most of HBL's durable profit sits.

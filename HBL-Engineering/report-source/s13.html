Section 13

Shareholding, positioning and flow analysis

A Rs 22,400 cr company that no active Indian fund manager owns. The promoter family trust holds 51.3% and the promoter group 59.11%, unchanged for three years; foreign funds hold 5.6%; mutual funds 0.32%, almost all of it index funds; and about 3.5 lakh retail holders own the rest. The price is set by retail and high-frequency flow, which is why it moved 83% between its 52-week low and high.

! The Jun-2025 shareholding pattern repeats Mar-2025 to the share

Screener shows the two quarters identical, and screener is right to: NSE holds two separate filings (21-Apr-2025 and 21-Jul-2025), different files, whose 42 category totals and holder counts (3,81,826 including promoters) are identical, down to 3,71,898 retail holders and 113 FPIs. In a quarter in which the price moved from about Rs 470 to Rs 590 and every other quarter's holder count changed by thousands, that is not credible as a snapshot. No revised pattern was filed. The most likely explanation is that the March data was resubmitted with a June date; the cause is unverified, and it is a Reg 31 compliance question for the company and its registrar.

Who owns it

  • Promoters: eight holders, 16.39 cr shares, 59.11% since Sep-2023. The Aluru Family Private Trust holds 51.30%; its trustee changed from Barclays to Kavita Prasad Aluru in Sep-2023 (Reg 10(5)/10(6) filings). The group bought 0.51 percentage points between Sep-2021 and Sep-2023 and has not bought or sold since. Pledge nil (XBRL every quarter; Reg 31(4) NIL at 31-Mar-2026). NSE's page shows 6.88% of HBL's demat shares pledged in the depository system; that column counts every holder's pledges, mostly broker margin pledges by the public, and is not a promoter pledge.
  • Early institutions exited: BanyanTree Growth Capital (10.46% in Sep-2021) was out by Sep-2022; Oman India Joint Investment Fund (9.68%) sold down to nil by Dec-2022, including 40.3 lakh shares in one bulk deal on 14-Nov-2022. Both sold before the Kavach re-rating.
  • Mutual funds: 8.78 lakh shares at Aug-2026 (0.32%), 99.5% in index funds and ETFs (Smallcap 250, Nifty 500, momentum-quality). The active holders of late 2025 (Union Active Momentum, Samco's momentum and small-cap funds, Taurus) all exited by Aug-2026. The only active entry in six months is AlphaGrep Flexi Cap with 3,249 shares. The 18-Mar-2026 meeting with SBI Mutual Fund produced no active SBI holding (AMFI data via Trendlyne, secondary).
  • Foreign: FPIs peaked at 7.10% in Sep-2025 and fell to 5.62%. Only Aware Super is named in the bulk-deal record; no FPI holds above 1%.
  • Retail: 3.5 lakh holders; the annual report's count fell from 3,90,313 (Mar-2025) to 3,40,740 (Mar-2026) as the stock fell from its Nov-2025 high, and has edged up since. The sharp rise from 1.44 lakh (Mar-2022) to 3.9 lakh (Mar-2025) tracked the Kavach story.

Reading: the register has the profile of a stock the market discovered through retail and momentum products and that institutions have looked at and passed on. That cuts both ways. There is no institutional overhang to sell, and a genuine second growth leg would find almost no one already positioned. But the price has no long-only anchor either, which is why a single letter moves it 7-10% in a day.

## 11. Kavach Systems: Comprehensive Coverage

**What is Kavach:**
Kavach is the Indian Railways' indigenously developed collision avoidance system (also called Train Collision Avoidance System, TCAS). HBL supplies onboard equipment and systems.

**Orders Secured:**

**CLW TCAS Order (Announced Dec-2024, secured):**
- **Ordering entity:** Chittaranjan Locomotive Works (CLW)
- **Order value:** Rs 1,522 cr (CARE-2025-03-31, line 133; CARE-2025-10-10, line 157-158)
- **Quantity:** 2,200 locomotives
- **Scope:** Supply, installation, commissioning of onboard TCAS equipment
- **Initial execution timeline:** 12 months from order receipt (CARE-2025-03-31, line 23)
- **Funding for execution:** HBL availed order-specific working capital limits of Rs 375 cr (Rs 350 cr OD/WCDL + Rs 25 cr LC) in FY25 (CARE-2025-03-31, line 25-26; CARE-2025-10-10, line 116)
- **Status as of 31-Dec-2025:** 76% of order completed within stipulated timeline; balance order cancelled per order terms (CARE-2026-02-25, line 27, 101)
- **Financial impact:** Working capital limits surrendered in full by end-Dec-2025 (CARE-2026-02-25, line 53-54)

**New Kavach Orders (Q1FY26):**
- **Value:** Rs 1,375 cr aggregate (CARE-2025-10-10, line 104; CARE-2026-02-25, line 104)
- **Scope:** Supply, testing, commissioning onboard Kavach equipment
- **Execution timeline:** 12 months (CARE-2025-10-10, line 105; CARE-2026-02-25, line 105)
- **Strategic importance:** CARE notes rollout of Vande Bharat trains and Kavach system implementation as drivers for electronics segment growth (CARE-2024-10-03, line 95-96; CARE-2025-10-10, line 113-117)

**Version & Regulatory Approval:**
- **Version 4.0 (V4.0) approval:** Secured from Research Designs and Standards Organisation (RDSO) prior to Dec-2024 (CARE-2025-10-10, line 26; CARE-2025-10-10, line 91)
- **Delay:** Unforeseen delay in RDSO approval caused elongation of inventory at FY25 year-end and softness in FY25 revenue (CARE-2025-10-10, line 91; CARE-2026-02-25, line 34)

**Revenue & Margin Contribution:**
- FY26 Q1: Railway segment (including Kavach order execution) contributed strong profitability; PBILDT margin 34.04%, PAT margin 23.06% (CARE-2025-10-10, line 101-105)
- FY26 9M: Railway segment drove 80% yoy revenue growth; PBILDT margin 37.23%, PAT margin 27.65% (CARE-2026-02-25, line 94-98)
- **Margin vs other segments:** Higher-margin revenue from railway/Kavach business offset decline in telecom segment (CARE-2025-10-10, line 97-105)

**Competition & Market Position:**
- CARE identifies Kavach as differentiator; no specific competitor names cited in rationales
- CARE rates Kavach as strategic driver for electronics segment diversification away from telecom dependency (CARE-2025-10-10, line 110-117)

**Payment Terms & Advances:**
- Not explicitly stated in CARE rationales; CLW working capital limits (Rs 375 cr) suggest substantial upfront inventory/advance payments typical for railway contracts
- **Bank guarantees:** Non-fund-based limits (BGs/LCs) increased from Rs 351 cr (Oct-2024) to Rs 576 cr (Oct-2025) to Rs 551 cr (Feb-2026), partly driven by CLW order execution requirements (line 330 in CARE-2026-02-25, shows Rs 551 cr ST BG/LC)

## 14. Evolution of Rating Sensitivities: What Changed

**2022 to 2023:** TOI sensitivity raised from "20% yoy, PBILDT 12%+" to "above Rs 1,700 cr PBILDT 12%". ROCE target raised from 13% to 15%. Operating cycle threshold and gearing cap remained consistent. CARE shifted to absolute rupee targets.

**2023 to 2024:** Major shift. Sensitivities simplified to single PBILDT margin floor (18%+, no longer % threshold). ROCE dropped as explicit metric. Debt covenant moved from gearing (0.75x) to Debt/EBITDA ratio (>1x) as negative trigger-reflecting capex funding focus. Operating cycle threshold retained at 250 days. **Rationale:** FY24 saw 63% yoy revenue growth; CARE upgraded to A+ and raised the bar to 18% PBILDT.

**2024 to 2025/2026:** Sensitivities remained stable; TOI threshold increased from implicit "above Rs 1,700" to explicit "₹2,800 crore and above" (reflecting Kavach order impact and higher revenue base). PBILDT margin and Debt/EBITDA thresholds unchanged. **Rationale:** Kavach order secured (Dec-2024), FY25 was a transition year (inventory buildup pre-approval), FY26 showed 80% yoy growth; CARE shifted outlook from Stable to Positive (Oct-2025) and held it in Feb-2026.

## 15. Key Rating Weaknesses & Risks (CARE's Assessment)

**Across all six rationales, CARE identified these recurring weaknesses:**

**Working Capital Intensity & Elongated Operating Cycle:**
- "Company operates in a working capital-intensive business marked by elongated operating cycle" (CARE-2022-10-07, line 82; repeated in all subsequent rationales)
- Inventory holding period ~102 days; collection period ~86 days (FY22 baseline)
- Operating cycle improved to 163 days (FY22) → 114 days (FY24) but elongated to 147 days (FY25) due to Kavach approval delay (CARE-2025-10-10, line 174-175)
- CARE threshold for downgrade: Operating cycle >250 days

**Volatile Raw Material Prices:**
- "Raw material is the major cost to company which contributes ~50 to 60% of TOI" (CARE-2024-10-03, line 158)
- "Lead comprises ~60% of battery costs" (CARE-2024-10-03, line 159-160)
- "Major raw materials required for manufacturing are on Lead Calcium and Lead Sub-oxide which are volatile" (CARE-2025-10-10, line 187-188)
- Company passes on price increases to customers but "with a time lag" (CARE-2022-10-07, line 130)

**Lead-Recycling Operations & Environmental Risk:**
- "Lead, a substance recognised for its high toxicity and environmental impact, serves as the fundamental raw material in battery production" (CARE-2024-10-03, line 151)
- "Enterprises involved in this industry are compelled to adhere to stringent pollution control regulations. As environmental regulations continue to become more stringent and environmental advocacy gains prominence, businesses operating in this sector face inherent risks related to ecological considerations" (CARE-2024-10-03, line 152-155)
- **Mitigant:** HBL's long tenure and compliance history (CARE rationales consistently note this)
- **Environmental initiatives:** Zero-liquid discharge for 2 decades; ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2015 certified; solar power + biomass energy infrastructure; circular economy approach (CARE-2025-10-10, line 211-223)

**Foreign Exchange Fluctuation Risk:**
- "HBL imports some raw materials and exports products/services resulting exposure to foreign exchange risk" (CARE-2025-10-10, line 191-192)
- **Mitigant:** Net exporter; uses EEFC account; natural hedging via FX inflows. FX gains in FY24-FY25 (Rs 6.32 cr and Rs 10.39 cr respectively) demonstrate effective hedging (CARE-2025-10-10, line 192-196)

**Competitive Intensity & Tender-Driven Revenue:**
- "The business is exposed to competition and growth prospects of the company depend on its ability to successfully bid for tenders and bag new orders" (CARE-2022-10-07, line 131-132)
- Specifically for railways: TCAS tenders held up in FY25, impacting electronics segment (CARE-2025-10-10, line 115; CARE-2026-02-25, line 130)

**Investment in Associate Entities (Tonbo Imaging Risk):**
- Commitment Rs 150 cr; actual investment Rs 86.67 cr (as of Mar-2024)
- "Investment forms about 15% of HBL's existing net worth base" (CARE-2023-08-28, line 106-107)
- CARE states: "while this presents an opportunity for growth, the uncertainty surrounding its outcome has an inherent element of risk" (CARE-2023-08-28, line 109)
- "Further increase in the investment in group/associate entities and ability of the company to generate commensurate benefits from the same, may remain critical from the rating and credit perspective" (CARE-2023-08-28, line 110-111)
- **Positive note:** By 2025, both Tonbo and Naval Systems are "reporting net profits" (CARE-2025-10-10, line 135)

**Proposed Debt-Funded Capex (FY23):**
- Lithium-ion cell and electric drivetrain (EDT) facility: Rs 110 cr total capex (2 phases)
- Phase I to be funded by Rs 80 cr term debt + internal accruals; Phase I COD Q3FY23
- "Timely completion of the said capex without incurring any significant time or cost overrun remains critical from credit perspective" (CARE-2022-10-07, line 121-122)
- **Status:** Funding achieved; regular capex thereafter to be self-funded (CARE rationales FY24 onwards)

Reading: either unit costs are over-stated, or funds "utilised" are booked on completion / payment (lagging installation), or some Kavach cost is funded outside the labelled Kavach head. The releases do not say (UNVERIFIED). This matters because HBL's own Railways-Electronics revenue (customer-category disclosure) was Rs 1,538.61 crore in FY26 standalone (AR-FY26 Ind AS 115 note, printed p.171 per notes-02; FY25 Rs 243.48 crore), against official reported all-India spend on Kavach of about Rs 814 crore over Mar-25 to Feb-26 (derived above). HBL's Railways-Electronics line also includes TMS/CTC and other signalling, so it is not all Kavach, but management says Kavach is "almost 50%" of sales (about Rs 1,626 crore if 50% of standalone Rs 3,251.80 crore; derived). One company's Kavach billing therefore exceeds the whole official Kavach expenditure for the year. ## PART 8 - INCIDENTAL FINDINGS
- Macro backdrop in 2026 that colours all cost commentary: WPI inflation 8.30% (Apr-2026, old base) to 9.92% (Aug-2026, new base 2022-23); Fuel and Power +22.93% YoY in Aug-2026; RBI April-2026 assumptions crude USD 85 a barrel and USD/INR 94 for FY27 (secondary). Management attributes shipping cost increases to a "West Asia conflict" (AR-FY26 p.07-08); the official WPI moves are consistent with an energy shock but the release read does not state the cause.
- Railway operating ratio 98.82% (FY26 RE), 98.40% (FY27 BE); 90% of revenue committed to salary, pension and lease (PRS 2026-27); 8th Pay Commission is a flagged pressure on Railways capacity to keep capex funded by internal revenue. Budget support supplies 95% of FY27 capex, so capex is tied to central budget priorities.
- Kavach unit cost data: Rs 70 lakh per loco (Aug-2023) rose to Rs 80 lakh per loco (Dec-2024); trackside Rs 50 lakh per RKm unchanged since Aug-2023. East Coast Railway sanction of Rs 270 crore for 631 RKm is Rs 0.43 crore per RKm (derived), below the Rs 0.50 crore official average, a hint that competitive bidding lowers trackside prices (inference only; sanction may not equal contract).
- Emergency procurement after Operation Sindoor is named by MoD as a driver of the FY27 defence budget (PIB 2221612); no fuze-specific line is disclosed.
- GTTP Phase 1 count differs between two PIB releases (8 tugs on the SOP, 16 in the Dec-2025 release).
